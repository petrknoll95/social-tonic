# Cheapest tests that can overturn the recommendation

**Proposed only, 17 October 2026. No recruitment, contact, purchase or experiment has taken place.** Cost ranges below are analyst spending caps excluding founder time, not quotes. Stop after each gate if evidence is negative. Start in Czechia; do not pay for several national registrations or a bespoke formula to test whether a problem exists.

| Order | Test and sample | What it resolves | Illustrative cash cap | Decision rule fixed before collecting data |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Audit 20 accessible office/coworking/café/specialist locations and online exact canned/bottled offers; then 15–20 adult interviews about the last three real occasions, including satisfied users/nonbuyers | Whether founder access and a switch reason exist; competitor price/availability; coffee habits as context only | €0–150 | Find at least 5 plausible accessible trial locations and a repeated specific complaint/value gap. If the only attraction is an unsupported ingredient claim, rewrite or stop. This does not count as buyer commitments. |
| 2 | Sequential concept/price screening: ~60–100 eligible Czech adults total, hold one realistic price and one can size fixed across three concepts first; test finalist prices separately; workday alertness versus ordinary energy versus CBD-free social ritual, same unbranded can treatment, explicit “keep usual choice/no purchase” | Meaning of focus; caffeine preference; difference from existing cans; comparative interest at CZK39/49/59, not an asserted acceptable price | €150–350 | Predeclare comparison; look for a credible switch reason and acceptance at a price that could support net margin. Signups/clicks are leads only; do not use their rate as the model's paid trial. Small cells are directional, not nationally representative. |
| 3 | Blinded tasting with 30–40 adults using compliant exact commercial can/bottle controls and, only after formula/process/allergen safety review, professionally prepared eligible finished-can/bottle prototypes; evaluate unbranded then branded/price | Taste, sweetness, portion, preference over CANS+ Performance/Vilgain and a canned/bottled tea/energy control; assess caffeine-free option separately | €150–300 for commercial controls; prototype work separately quoted | Advance only if candidate is at least competitive on taste at intended price and repeat intent has a concrete occasion. No medical or productivity-efficacy claims follow from a tasting. |
| 4 | Before any new prototype tasting (including free samples), and before a saleable pilot: documented formula/label/process/allergen screen for Czechia and intended next country, two suitable co-packer feasibility/landed-cost options, required stability/process testing | Permitted ingredients/claims, safety, packaging responsibilities, actual MOQ and contribution | Obtain scoped quotes first; no reliable public all-in cost found | No sellable pilot until legal, process and label gates pass. Supplier marketing is not legal clearance. Reject a run exceeding credible sell-through before expiry. |
| 5 | Small compliant paid pilot, ideally 100 unique adult first buyers through 3–5 accessible channels; single cans and transparent packs; follow cohorts for 8–12 weeks | Paid trial, actual basket size, dissatisfaction, repeat, shipping burden and margin | Set from compliant production/fulfilment quote; do not assume €500 can manufacture a safe custom drink | Predeclare 30/60/90-day repeat and contribution. A working gate is at least 30–35 second purchases per 100 first buyers at non-promotional price, positive per-order contribution, and several channels reordering for consumer pull. This is a learning gate, not statistical validation of 35% annual repeat or six annual repeat orders. |
| 6 | If Czech paid pilot passes, compare localized German and Austrian priced offers with equal acquisition spend, plus partner-led shipping/DRS cost models | Whether German regional scale or Austrian proximity actually gives lower cost per contribution-generating repeater | €200–400 per country, before physical stock launch | Enter only the better contribution-adjusted route with compliant packaging and local service; no launch based solely on clicks or a larger population. |

For 35 repeat buyers out of 100, a rough 95% Wilson interval is 26–45%; 35% is not established precisely. For paid trial, log **all unique qualified adults shown a priced, in-stock offer, including nonbuyers**, plus channel, price, stock, age/occasion qualification, purchase/no purchase, first-order date and acquisition spend. Deduplicate across venues and digital. Paid-trial conversion uses this exposure denominator; repeat uses first paid buyers. Neither impressions nor free tasters are substituted.

A paid pilot this small cannot substantiate cognitive efficacy, long-term safety or country prevalence. Report all buyers, failures and refunds, not only completers. Freeze definitions, collect first-order date and record time at risk; sample everyone who can fairly have reached each follow-up window.

Separate personal purchases from employer-funded fridge use. An office order is one commercial account and several possible consumers; count account orders for cash flow and identified adults for consumer repeat. Separate free tasting from paid trial, giveaways from consumption and stock delivered from sell-through.

## Outsourcing boundary

Hardtank publicly advertises about 3,000 cans/SKU for ready recipes and 6,000 for custom work; the custom page lists €3,000 development and 200/250 ml cans. These are vendor-published starting terms, not this project's offer or proof it can manufacture a proposed sparkling formula. Its current process excludes several ingredient types including protein, live probiotics and CBD. A 330 ml format needs another verified line or supplier. [FAQ, accessed 17 October 2026](https://hardtank.com/frequently-asked-questions/), [custom page, ingredient list updated September 2026](https://hardtank.com/custom-formula/).

The FAQ and custom page give inconsistent development-fee refund descriptions; do not deduct any refund from the budget without written terms. At Czech base volume, a 6,000-can first run is material; the low scenario sells only about 326 cans. Prefer cheap commercial-control tests before custom development, then one formula/one flavour and a staged order if feasible. Never claim broad industry MOQs or a final landed cost from this single vendor. [Refresco's service page](https://www.refresco.com/en/how-we-deliver-complete-beverage-solutions/) confirms outsourced can/formulation capability at scale but gives no suitable startup MOQ or quote.
