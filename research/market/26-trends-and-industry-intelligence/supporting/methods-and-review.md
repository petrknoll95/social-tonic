## Collection and evidence handling

- Search combined benefit terms, occasions, companies and negative/commercial terms. Generic “functional beverage” coverage was only one entry point.
- Reviewed accessible articles, issuer releases, company/retailer product pages, filings and regulatory material. Ordinary public HTML was used when page extraction was incomplete. No paywall was bypassed; no paid dataset or subscription was purchased.
- Format qualification applies to finished ready-to-drink cans and bottles, including coffee, tea and shots. A mixed-format company can inform business context, but its total revenue is not assigned to qualifying liquids.
- Product labels and brand declarations establish what is stated on the page, not laboratory composition or proven functional effects. No products, certificates or physical labels were acquired.
- Primary announcements establish announcements. Retail offers establish listings, subject to local stock and timing. Forecasts, claimed scanner rankings, original scanner data, filings and independent reporting remain distinct.
- Ingredient rationale, finished-product evidence and legal marketing eligibility are separate. Existing EU/UK retail listings do not grant permission to copy a formula.

The result is **44 signal records** and **164 distinct source URLs**, normalized from 170 workstream source records. These are not 164 independent proofs or 44 independent companies. Some register entries document discovery leads, exclusions, inaccessible originals or scientific/regulatory context rather than supporting a product signal.

## Material gaps

No representative Czech willingness-to-pay or paid-repeat study was identified for the proposed concepts. No new-line repeat cohorts, complete scanner database, definitive failed-launch rate, supplier quote or comprehensive Czech shelf audit was obtained. Public podcast summaries are not transcripts, and audio was not treated as reviewed. Full bodies of some Brewbound/Grocer/BevNET pieces, the full Kin filing and certain underlying proprietary reports were inaccessible. Access limitations are recorded per source; inaccessible content was not invented.

Regulatory checking is a targeted decision screen. It is not a final formula-specific authorization or label review. Clinical references are a targeted check against overclaiming, not a systematic evidence synthesis. Office, café and gym entry routes remain testable channel hypotheses where sustained sales evidence was absent.

## Reproducibility and completion checks

The CSVs are UTF-8 and retain stable source/signal IDs, uncertain dates and explicit source scopes. The accompanying data guide explains the fields. Publication checks validate CSV parsing, unique IDs, referenced sources, Markdown reference definitions and relative links. Remote publication is verified separately against the commit's file contents; the final chat response supplies that commit and direct GitHub links.

No companies were contacted. No purchases, paid subscriptions or monitoring automations were created. Commercial and legal uncertainty is preserved rather than resolved with invented precision.
