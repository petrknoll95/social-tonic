## Findings for lead resolution

1. **Retain the full alternative requested in the price comment (material precision improvement).** CZV04 and the report accurately record objection to 45 CZK and a proposed 250 ml/30 CZK alternative. However, comment `Pac26f2c2b027606f` also requests **taurine**. This is a combined formula, size and price suggestion, not acceptance of the same formula in a smaller package. Add the ingredient-change qualification to the paraphrase/limitation and avoid interpreting it as a clean portion/price tradeoff. [Aktin source, public question with relative date only](https://aktin.cz/vilgain-energy-focus-drink-2/12x44884).

2. **Hold the package constant within a price test (method improvement).** The current report and interview guide propose 39/49/59 CZK per “250/330 ml” can or bottle. These are valid exploratory stimuli only if each price comparison fixes one exact volume, package, flavor and caffeine description. Otherwise the apparent price response can be a portion or package response. Pick one concrete container for the first comparison; test another size separately or use a properly designed later choice study. This does not require choosing the launch format now. Current alternative/no-purchase and unaided spend questions are already appropriate.

3. **Keep household frequency conditional and historical (minor clarification).** The YouGov paragraph can be read as once per three weeks across every Czech household. Make “among households buying the category” explicit when repeating the frequency and 45 CZK purchase amount. Neither is a single-person serving frequency, a guaranteed single-can price, nor a 2026 acceptable-spend estimate. The existing warning is directionally correct; preserve it. [Category article, 3 March 2025](https://www.zboziaprodej.cz/2025/03/03/zboziprodej-1-%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A02-2025-energie-za-vice-nez-sest-miliard/).

## Conclusions that pass with existing qualifications

- No representative claim is made from the 15 Czech voice rows. Multiple rows from the same discussion, unverified ages/residency, and nonbuyer/former-user accounts are disclosed. Coded observations must continue to be reported as observations, not recruited participants.
- The 2026 study-use review is subjective and cannot establish adult-only demand or better work/study outcomes. The report preserves that distinction. The review's date was independently confirmed.
- Ordinary canned-water discussion is separated from CANS+ Performance/Balance. Kombucha enjoyment is separated from gut effects; the older free-sample account is distinguished from paid purchase. No brand-wide sales are assigned to an exact SKU.
- The lead's five meanings of productivity correctly distinguish wakefulness, attention, errors, duration and completed tasks. The diary is observational. The matched-caffeine study paragraph does not claim incremental workplace output or treat a nonsignificant comparison as proof of equivalence.
- Actual offers are not presented as accepted prices. **49 × 3 = 147 CZK/week** and **49 × 5 = 245 CZK/week** are correct hypothetical arithmetic, not purchase forecasts. Frequency ranges remain labelled planning assumptions.
- The early-afternoon desk/study audience is supportable as a low-cost learning choice, provided it remains an assumption rather than a conclusion about the best market. Broader workplace examples suggest that customer-facing or practical work can also involve focus needs; the chosen desk-work recruitment frame is a deliberate narrowing, not an evidence-based exclusion of others.

## Rival explanations to retain in fieldwork

An afternoon drink may accompany a pause, social interaction, task switching or lunch effects; it may not produce the desired change. “Focus” can mean feeling activated, enjoying a ritual or avoiding distraction rather than finishing more work. A premium purchase can be an occasional treat, a promotion response or a gift rather than a daily functional routine. A smaller-container request can concern affordability, carrying convenience, sensory intensity or changed ingredients. Existing water/free coffee, no purchase or stopping work can be successful alternatives. The current interview guide generally accommodates these rivals; keep them when shortening it.
