# Data and blank fieldwork templates

All files are UTF-8 CSV, dated **17 October 2026**. No dataset contains completed interviews or diary fieldwork.

| File | Unit and purpose |
|---|---|
| [occasion-need-substitute-matrix.csv](occasion-need-substitute-matrix.csv) | 12 candidate occasions; segment, timing, eligible packaged substitutes, other habits, question and evidence references. Analyst hypotheses, not measured effects. |
| [ranked-hypotheses.csv](ranked-hypotheses.csv) | 10 proposed tests ordered by decision importance/learning value; not a market ranking or probability model. |
| [work-focus-voices.csv](work-focus-voices.csv) | 12 public review/discussion observations with paraphrases, dates and limits. |
| [evening-needs-voices.csv](evening-needs-voices.csv) | 12 public review/discussion observations; incentives/verification limitations retained. |
| [czech-context-voices.csv](czech-context-voices.csv) | 15 Czech-language observations; exact variants, purchase badges and age uncertainty recorded. |
| [interview-coding-template.csv](interview-coding-template.csv) | Header-only blank schema; use with the [guide](../supporting/interview-guide.md). |
| [diary-template.csv](diary-template.csv) | Header-only blank schema; use with the [diary protocol](../supporting/diary-study.md). |

`source_id`/`evidence_refs` resolve to [sources.csv](../sources.csv). WF = work/focus; E = evening; CZ = Czech context; L = lead. Separate workstream registers preserve original metadata. URLs may point to a whole discussion/review page; row dates, variant and locators identify the selected account where available. Thread-level contrasts are not single-person quotes. All text is short paraphrase; no full scraped source material is included.

The 39 voice rows are **purposively selected observations**, not 39 unique respondents, independent confirmations, adult participants or a population sample. Do not calculate segment shares, sentiment percentages or purchase rates from them. Public platform geography is not verified residency. “Verified purchase” means the retailer's badge, not an independent audit; intention is not paid repeat. Free/promotion-collected reviews and older recipe/context limitations remain visible.

Dates may be unknown, approximate or multi-date; do not coerce them into invented ISO days. `unknown`, blank and zero are different: unknown means not established; a blank template has no respondent; zero spend means free only if explicitly recorded. Candidate frequency ranges and price cells in the report are assumptions, not observations. Source registers are evidence provenance, not a set of independent studies.

Keep completed fieldwork and scheduling/contact records private and separate; do not commit raw participant data to this public repository. Use participant codes and publish only appropriately consented, de-identified aggregates or carefully reviewed paraphrases. Caffeine dose is recorded only when known, and subjective wakefulness, attention and task completion are separate fields. These templates cannot establish a causal productivity or health effect.
