# Relaxation, social, sleep and CBD workstream

Research date: **17 October 2026**. Geography: US commercial benchmarks, with explicitly labelled regional comparisons. Status: complete; integrated and independently reviewed. [SKU dataset](../data/relax-sleep-cbd.csv) · [Workstream source register](relax-sleep-cbd-sources.csv).

This workstream complements the higher-priority focus/energy landscape. It is not a recommendation to make a social tonic or CBD drink the initial product. Only finished liquid cans/bottles appear in the dataset; historical and uncertain-availability rows are marked separately. All experienced-benefit and timing statements are **brand claims**, unless explicitly stated otherwise. No finished-product efficacy trial was verified here. Prices are US observed offers, not EU willingness-to-pay estimates, and exclude tax/delivery unless specified.

## What deserves attention

1. **VYBES Mood is the clearest formulation-pivot benchmark.** Its founder announced an exit from CBD on 24 January 2025 and a magnesium replacement on 29 January. This is unusually useful negative evidence: a brand can retain its occasion and flavors while changing its functional ingredient. The stated reason was state-level retail restrictions; it does **not** establish that consumers rejected CBD. [Founder exit email](https://milled.com/vybes/big-news-about-our-cbd-drinks-xRwezgyBS7YCKfNb), [replacement announcement](https://milled.com/vybes/introducing-mood-OoFc2tnqH2ymRbBw). Confidence: high on the pivot, medium on its commercial explanation.
2. **Recess Mood is a mature calm/reset benchmark, not a new 2025 launch.** It provides a directly observable mainstream retail comparison at Target and a useful distinction between a workday reset and bedtime sedation. Recess describes a narrowly defined 2026 SPINS velocity ranking; that is stronger than an influencer count but remains a company-reported metric without the underlying dataset. The original hemp line launched in 2018 and Mood in 2021. [Historical interview](https://www.beveragedaily.com/Article/2024/10/28/Recess-on-carving-out-the-relaxation-beverage-category/), [Target SKU](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-94810235), [company metric](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/drinkrecess_another-milestone-for-recess-we-started-activity-7452420532446081024-GkkM). Confidence: high on product/retail facts, medium on ranking.
3. **BRĒZ Dream is the stronger recent sleep/wind-down challenger.** The CBD/THC-free Dream launch and separate THC-containing Drift show that related occasions do not imply equivalent formulas. Trade reporting places Dream in Sprouts before the June 2025 press announcement. The most useful evidence is product-specific distribution; total BRĒZ sales include intoxicating products and cannot establish Dream's success. [Launch reporting](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2025/brez-platforms-with-non-infused-innovation-hard-to-build-with-thc/). Confidence: high on launch/segmentation; low on product sell-through and efficacy.
4. **Hiyo is informative for social distribution and experience language.** Its verified 2021 origin prevents mislabelling it as new; Pineapple Coconut is a genuine July 2025 flavor launch. The required October 2026 Fitt article reports financing and forward-looking case/door numbers. Those are not realized sales or proof of its “float” effect. [Original history](https://drinkhiyo.com/pages/our-story), [flavor/retail announcement](https://bevnet.com/pr/2025/07/22/hiyo-announces-nationwide-launch-at-whole-foods-market-with-exclusive-new-flavor-pineapple-coconut), [Fitt funding report](https://insider.fitt.co/hiyo-raises-20-8m-to-scale-social-tonics/). Confidence: high on dates; medium on commercial scale; efficacy unestablished.

## Additional comparisons and evidence limits

TRIP Cherry Lemon is a verified US flavor launch in October 2025. Its current US page explicitly presents two recipes: the later one removes erythritol and lists 35 rather than 25 calories. The reformulation date is unknown, so it is not assigned a fabricated 2026 date. The US 355 ml product must not inherit the UK 250 ml formula. Global TRIP revenue cannot prove the US liquid SKU's success, especially as the brand also sells non-beverage formats. [US launch report](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2025/trip-reveals-new-flavor-format-hire-as-brand-crosses-sales-milestone/), [US formulation page](https://us.drink-trip.com/products/cherry-lemon-mindful-blend).

Sly DREAM existed by May 2024; its October 2026 Sprouts story is retail expansion, not first launch. Blue Bear's April 2026 news concerns Refresh and the wider brand; it does not establish Sleep's launch date or growth. Cheribundi Sleep qualifies as a bottled juice comparator, while 2025 gummy launches do not qualify. All three add useful format/occasion comparisons, but no verified recent product-level sell-through was found. [Sly prior sale](https://www.ghostgaming.com/blog-posts/sly-and-ghost-gaming-announce-partnership), [Sly expansion](https://bevnet.com/pr/2026/10/09/doctorfounded-functional-beverage-sly-launches-nationwide-at-sprouts-farmers-market), [Blue Bear announcement](https://bevnet.com/pr/2026/04/07/blue-bear-launches-refresh-functional-tea-for-immunity--hydration-support-expands-into-fullday-wellness-lineup), [Cheribundi exact liquid SKU](https://cheribundi.com/products/sleep-tart-cherry-juice).

The current VYBES bottled label says 12 oz and 300 mg magnesium; a June 2025 distributor catalogue says 14 oz for the Mood flavor. Preserve the dated versions rather than silently combining them. The label lists elemental magnesium as a nutrient; the mass of a magnesium salt and elemental magnesium are not interchangeable. [Current label](https://idrinkvybes.com/products/strawberry-lavender), [older catalogue](https://goodstuffdist.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PL-2025-No-Price-1-1.pdf).

**Daytrip is a useful current CBD reference but not a proven 2025–2026 breakout.** It lists CBD water and a separate CBD-free prebiotic soda range. A dated Oregon retailer flyer confirms sale in July 2025; that is better evidence of actual market presence than a generic trend article. Current DTC template text contains both “Unavailable” and an add-to-cart control, so fulfillment is not claimed verified. A peer-reviewed US survey published in November 2024 found 58% of 2,010 respondents **would or may** try CBD beverages. This measures stated interest, not actual purchase, repeat purchase, a 2025–2026 growth rate or Czech willingness to pay. CBD and THC were studied separately; their demand must not be merged. [Staples, Journal of Wine Economics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-wine-economics/article/canning-cannabis-consumer-preferences-for-cbd-and-thcinfused-beverages/4343A6856F99D7C186836D32ADBD810A).

For a separately assessed CBD extension, the sourcing questions are therefore evidence gaps, not endorsements: extraction type, exact cannabinoid profile, batch-specific residual THC and quantification limit, pesticide/heavy-metal/solvent/microbial results, dispersion stability through processing and shelf life, and active dose at end of shelf life. “US-grown,” “broad-spectrum,” “nano” and a legal hemp threshold do not answer these questions. No supplier was contacted and no batch COA was audited. The lead report owns EU/Czech legality, clinical evidence and claims assessment.

## Patterns to test in Europe

- **Separate calm daytime function from sleep.** Test whether customers want to remain alert during an afternoon reset or become sleepy before bed. Category names frequently blur these different jobs. A beverage positioned for both may create an expectation conflict.
- **Compare an understandable mineral-led product with a complex botanical blend.** VYBES, Recess and TRIP illustrate simpler consumer explanations, while Hiyo and sleep formulas use multi-ingredient stacks. Investigate taste, trust, perceived effect and repeat purchase separately. Ingredient count is not potency.
- **Match format to ritual.** Blue Bear's smaller still tea and Cheribundi's juice offer different sensory and portion cues from sparkling social cans. In interviews, test bedtime liquid-volume tolerance and whether carbonation fits that occasion; do not assume either answer.
- **Use flavor novelty to enter retail, but measure continuing demand.** Hiyo and TRIP's exclusive flavors help win a shelf occasion. Retail placement, launch publicity and celebrity visibility do not demonstrate velocity after the novelty period.
- **Design a cannabinoid-independent brand architecture.** VYBES shows a regulatory-driven ingredient pivot; Daytrip and BRĒZ show separate lines. A brand centered on a customer occasion can support a CBD-free core and only later an independently justified, lawful CBD extension.

These are hypotheses for low-cost concept interviews and sensory prototypes, not preselected formulas or launch markets. Predeclare what would disconfirm each concept; compare repeat interest at a locally tested price, not just first-sip novelty.

## Exclusions and unresolved checks

- Som Sleep's current main shop sells powders requiring mixing. These are excluded; the observed shop change alone does not prove a formal discontinuation of every historic canned SKU. [Current shop](https://getsom.com/).
- VYBES' 2 oz Morning Mood Shot is a qualifying **format** but is advertised with a waitlist and “coming in January”; the year is not specified. Treat it as an announcement watchlist, not a sold benchmark. [Official homepage](https://idrinkvybes.com/).
- No verified numerical onset/duration was found for most products. Blue Bear's timing and VYBES' multiweek narrative are marketing statements, not comparable trial results. Unknowns are preserved in the dataset.
- No new standalone CBD-only US launch with convincing 2025–2026 growth was verified in this workstream. This is a research limitation, not proof that none exists. Do not fill this gap with THC-led sales, unverified market forecasts or brand-wide revenue.
- No clinical effect, EU legality, nutritional deficiency prevalence or product safety assurance should be imported from brand copy. A supplier's ingredient study needs dose, extract, population, duration and beverage-processing checks before it can inform a proposed drink.
