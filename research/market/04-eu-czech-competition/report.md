# EU and Czech competition for functional RTD drinks

**Research date: 17 October 2026.** Geography: Czechia, Germany, Austria, Poland, the Netherlands and France; UK and Switzerland are separate non-EU references. Products: finished ready-to-drink liquids in cans or bottles. No formula, brand, budget or launch country has been selected.

## Decision-relevant findings

**Czechia already has direct focus and calm competitors.** A new drink cannot credibly claim an empty category merely because it differs from conventional energy drinks. CANS+ Performance, Vilgain Energy & Focus and Magnesia Plus Focus compete for attention/work occasions; CANS+ Balance and Magnesia Plus Antistress already address calm-related needs. There are also established alternatives in beer, soda, bottled/canned tea and coffee, kombucha and sports drinks. See the [country-by-country matrix](competitor-matrix.md) and [Czech evidence](supporting/czechia-research.md).

**The defensible next step is a local switching test, not a national launch claim.** Focus remains a high-priority research opportunity because the job is identifiable and caffeine has a plausible acute rationale. The specific unmet need, price premium and distribution advantage remain unproven. A CBD-free evening proposition merits a separate test. Neither requires copying Hiyo's formula or promising a perceptible pharmacological effect. Hydration/gut opportunities are secondary and must beat familiar alternatives on a defined occasion.

**Public evidence is stronger for availability than success.** An in-stock domestic retailer page can establish an offer; it cannot establish national distribution, recognition, units/store/week or repeat purchase. Most exact functional SKUs have unknown local scale. Established parent companies, emerging operators and products with unknown scale are labeled separately in the [dataset](data/competitor-matrix.csv). There is no defensible numerical estimate of category saturation or founder addressable market here.

**CBD is commercially visible but not a launch shortcut.** Some listings are unavailable, internally inconsistent or only in specialist channels. No EU authorization applicable to the intended CBD beverage was established. The September 2026 positive EFSA opinion concerns a specific synthetic-CBD supplement in oil; it does not authorize a canned drink. The [separate CBD assessment](supporting/regulatory-and-evidence-update.md) covers demand, effects, safety, sourcing, permission and claims independently.

## Coverage and confidence

The shortlist is deliberate: Czechia offers founder access for learning; neighboring Germany, Austria and Poland provide realistic nearby comparisons; the Netherlands and France add different functional and adult-soft-drink retail contexts. This is not a ranking of market size or launch readiness. Slovakia and the rest of the EU remain unassessed, rather than rejected. UK/Swiss prices and sales are never substituted for EU evidence.

The [matrix](competitor-matrix.md) supplies exact SKU/pack, proposition, formulation type, whole-container price, channel, stock state, scale, business maturity and evidence quality. Source dates, geography and limitations are in [sources.csv](sources.csv). One serving means one full can/bottle; larger bottles are identified. Local currency is retained, with no implied exchange-rate or affordability equivalence. Retail price snapshots are not manufacturer revenue or quoted production economics.

Evidence labels distinguish **observed facts**, **brand/retailer claims**, **analyst estimates/calculations**, and **hypotheses**. High confidence in a price or label is compatible with unknown sales and unproven effects. No representative country-specific awareness survey for the sampled functional brands was obtained; distribution and company longevity are recognition proxies only. The [protocol](supporting/research-protocol.md) records sampling, delegation, exclusions and concurrent repository changes.

## Czech competitive assessment

### Direct competition and practical price anchors

CANS+ Performance's verified 330 ml proposition includes approximately 42 mg caffeine; the Czech scan found a [dm offer at **CZK39.50**](https://www.dm.cz/p/d/3149633/cans-performance). [Vilgain Energy & Focus lemon/lime, 330 ml](https://aktin.cz/vilgain-energy-focus-drink-2/12x44884), is **CZK45**, with a declared 106 mg caffeine. These are meaningful direct controls for a lower-/moderate-caffeine work drink. Magnesium/B-vitamin products and ordinary caffeinated alternatives widen the comparison. Formula/stock qualifications are in the [Czech memo](supporting/czechia-research.md) and matrix; an accessible chain listing does not establish stock in every branch.

The competitive challenge is therefore more specific than “healthier energy.” A prospective product must explain why a customer would switch from an already accessible drink: a sensory preference, useful portion, verified caffeine preference, occasion-specific convenience or better value. All of those remain hypotheses until customers describe the problem and choose the candidate against actual alternatives at price.

Calm competition also exists at different price and portion levels. Small canned formats compete with functional mineral-water bottles and with beverages bought for enjoyment. The existence of stress-related wording does not establish a measured anti-stress effect. A new evening drink must win a drinking occasion against CANS+ Balance, premium soda, kombucha, a bottled/canned mocktail and non-alcoholic beer, not only against other products labeled “functional.”

### What demand and commercial evidence actually show

- **Energy:** NIQ figures reported on 3 March 2025 put Czech energy-drink retail value above **CZK6 billion**, up **10%**, for the 52 weeks ending **1 December 2024**. This is older category evidence, not 2026 focus-drink sales. [Zboží & Prodej / NIQ](https://www.zboziaprodej.cz/2025/03/03/zboziprodej-1-%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A0%E2%81%A02-2025-energie-za-vice-nez-sest-miliard/).
- **More recent direction:** December 2025 reporting describes volume growth of **15% for energy** and **78% for vitamin waters** in its discussion of 2025 trading. The underlying category tables and exact denominators were not supplied; use this directionally, not as a precise new-product forecast. [Zboží & Prodej / NIQ, 15 December 2025](https://www.zboziaprodej.cz/2025/12/15/zboziprodej-12-2025-vitezove-roku-jsou-energeticke-napoje-a-vitaminove-vody/).
- **Non-alcoholic beer:** the Czech brewers' association reports **1.68 million hl produced in 2025**, including flavored variants, **+4%**. Production includes export; it is not domestic retail sales or demand for botanical social tonics. Its reported 28% hospitality share concerns total beer consumption, not specifically alcohol-free beer. [ČSPS, 2026 report on 2025](https://ceske-pivo.cz/tz2026/spotreba-piva-cesko-2025).
- **Social occasion:** a sponsor-published g82 survey of over 900 Czech pub/bar guests reports 47% choosing a non-alcoholic drink instead and four in ten alternating alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks. This supports investigating moderation occasions. Sampling/weighting/fieldwork details are insufficient to treat it as a representative estimate for all Czech adults or demand for a new functional product. [Prazdroj, 23 July 2025](https://www.prazdroj.cz/vztah-cechu-k-alkoholu-se-meni-piji-mene-a-prokladaji-ho-nealkem).

These sources establish strong incumbent occasions and some broader purchasing activity. They do **not** establish that more focus drinks are unnecessary, that social tonics have no demand, or that an attractive new drink will achieve acceptable shelf velocity.

The Czech research explicitly rejects two misleading scale shortcuts: the reported CANS million-can milestone predates the functional range, and PRAGER'S company results combine cider/kombucha and multiple markets. Neither demonstrates Czech sales of the exact functional or kombucha SKU. Stockouts, including the reviewed Powerade listing, are not interpreted as commercial success or discontinuation. [Czech source analysis](supporting/czechia-research.md).

### Distribution is not uniform

Mainstream retailer websites and drugstore listings demonstrate more access than DTC alone, but nationwide physical distribution remains unmeasured. Specialist e-commerce offers can establish an obtainable niche product without establishing broad consumer recognition. Domestic DTC allows a local offer to be observed; cross-border shipping must be separately evidenced and is not a domestic retail presence. Hospitality coverage is especially incomplete: serving suggestions and generic stockist logos are not outlet-level proof. No systematic Czech menu, coworking-fridge or office-vending audit was performed.

Consequently, “no national competitor” and “no convenient drink at this particular workplace or venue” are different hypotheses. Only the latter is a plausible small-founder distribution question at this stage, and even that requires named-outlet observation and buyer interviews.

CANS already advertises an [office supply offer](https://cz.cans.com/pages/cans-do-kancelare) and a [hospitality/retail/fitness business offer whose trial pack includes CANS+](https://cz.cans.com/pages/b2b-business). Therefore office delivery or café supply alone is not a novel route. The pages establish an offered service, not verified exact-SKU account coverage or sales. Any proposed access gap must be more specific than “sell to offices.”

## Competition by job and occasion

| Job / occasion | Direct proposition | Eligible product substitutes | Research implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning/early-afternoon focused work | Focus/nootropic cans, functional waters and any verified bottled shots | Canned/bottled coffee or tea, conventional energy | Compare caffeine amount and taste separately from nootropic storytelling; high energy-shelf density does not measure every productivity niche. |
| Short alertness boost / travel / activity | Energy, caffeine-forward tea or coffee RTDs | Other ready-to-drink caffeinated bottles/cans | Convenience, portion and channel availability may matter more than extra ingredients. No unsupported driving-performance claim. |
| Caffeine-limited afternoon | Lower-caffeine focus or caffeine-free clarity proposition | Caffeine-free bottled/canned soft drinks or functional water | Low-dose competition already exists. Demand for no caffeine and demand for a noticeable effect must be tested separately. |
| After-work calm / reset | CBD-free calm/relax drinks; CBD studied separately | Premium soda, herbal RTDs, kombucha, alcohol-free beer | Sensory ritual and stress-related efficacy are different value propositions. Do not promise anxiety treatment. |
| Socializing while moderating alcohol | Social tonics and premixed non-alcoholic mocktails | Beer, premium soda, kombucha, botanical sparkling drinks | Taste, adult presentation and convenient availability can be tested without a drug-like effect promise. |
| Bedtime/wind-down | Dedicated sleep-positioned finished drink if exact qualifying SKU verified | Caffeine-free bottled herbal drink or ordinary packaged beverage | The sample is thinner here; a search gap is not proof of an unmet market. Avoid inferring sleep benefit from “relax” positioning. |
| Exercise/heat hydration and recovery | Electrolyte/sports bottles and relevant recovery RTDs | Water/mineral-water bottles; established sports drinks | Specify the occasion and compare against water; generic desk hydration does not establish incremental benefit. |
| Soda replacement / gut-health routine | Kombucha and qualifying fiber/prebiotic RTDs | Premium soda, sparkling water and other bottled/canned soft drinks | Taste/tolerance and repeat matter; fermentation or fiber presence alone is not a verified benefit. |

Freshly prepared coffee/tea and supplement routines belong in customer interviews as competing habits. They are excluded from the product matrix, price benchmarks and drink-line scale evidence. Dedicated sleep and genuinely non-stimulant cognitive RTDs remain coverage/evidence gaps; they are not ranked as proven open markets.

## Shortlist of opportunities to validate

**No launch-ready white space is proven.** The following are prioritized experiments supported by identifiable occasions or competitive/channel differences; their unmet-demand component is explicitly hypothetical. Priority reflects learning value and founder constraints, not forecast market size.

| Priority / candidate | Evidence supporting investigation | Specific possible gap | What must be true / decisive rejection |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · CBD-free work-occasion drink with a demonstrable switch reason** | Local direct focus offers around CZK40–45, lower-priced Magnesia bottles and distinct caffeine amounts establish a real competitive set; US productivity positioning provides a benchmark. | A particular adult segment dislikes the taste, portion or caffeine profile of available options, or lacks convenient access at a named work occasion. **Neither low caffeine nor office supply is an empty category.** | Interviews identify repeated dissatisfaction; blind taste and price-choice beat CANS+/Vilgain, Magnesia and eligible coffee/tea controls. Reject if only unsupported nootropic promises win or existing options satisfy the job. |
| **2 · CBD-free, caffeine-free adult evening drink in a narrowly selected venue/channel** | Czech moderation survey supports the occasion; calm products, mocktails and beer prove active competition. Dutch specialist calm versus grocery energy illustrates different routes, not a universal absence. | A specific venue's customers want an adult flavor/ritual beyond its available beer/soda choices. **Not a claim that Czech calm or mocktails are missing.** | Audit named outlets and interview buyers before claiming a distribution gap; repeated choice must beat local calm/soda/beer/mocktail controls. Reject if taste/price or buyer rotation cannot justify an additional SKU. |
| **3 · Clear formula, dose and version information as a differentiator within either concept** | The Czech scan identifies specific information defects: inconsistent manufacturing origin, botanical copy, flavor-dependent formulas and stale dose listings; some CBD amounts are undisclosed. | Buyers cannot always compare like-for-like composition from the displayed pages. The information gap is observed; willingness to pay for solving it is unknown. | Test concise per-container disclosure and consistent retailer information against ordinary copy. Treat this as a supporting feature, not a sufficient brand proposition; reject a premium if it improves comprehension but not paid choice. Tea-based or other RTD liquids remain options, not selected formats. |
| **Conditional secondary · Gut-health soda or occasion-specific hydration** | Local kombucha, mainstream sports bottles, vitamin-water growth and Austrian private-label competition establish relevant substitutes. | A narrow taste/tolerance or activity-use gap, still to be identified. | Select one job, verify lawful composition/claims, compare with incumbent and water/soda controls, validate stability and repeat. Reject generic “wellness for everyone” or a cost premium unsupported by choice. |
| **Hold · Dedicated sleep / genuinely non-stimulant cognitive effect** | Clear intended jobs, but weak exact-product efficacy and thinner verified local SKU coverage. | Unmeasured; lack of search results is not a gap. | Require credible restrained proposition, classification/claims review and differentiated repeat before formulation spend. |
| **Separate hold · CBD extension** | Identifiable niche listings and consumer-interest questions; important safety/regulatory constraints. | Unknown CBD-specific willingness to pay independent of relaxation. | Actual beverage authorization/use route, qualified supply, safety, viable communication and independent demand signal. No reliance on THC demand or overseas permission. |

Start with inexpensive Czech interviews and an observed retail/venue audit, then compare one second country if the segment/channel case survives. The [validation protocol](supporting/validation-protocol.md) gives proposed samples, actual incumbent controls, a price experiment and a retail-data request. Proceed to outsourced bench development only after a credible switch reason and formula/claims route emerge. No manufacturing quotes, buyer commitments, customer interviews or tests were obtained here.

## What is reliable now, and what needs more evidence?

Reliable within the dated sample: direct focus/calm competition exists; qualifying RTD substitutes span several price/format/channel choices; country-specific lists are not interchangeable; CBD and non-CBD ranges must be separated; disclosed ingredient benefits are claims unless independently supported. The [regulatory/science memo](supporting/regulatory-and-evidence-update.md) preserves the difference between ingredient evidence and a finished drink's effect.

**Retail data or buyer interviews are required** before relying on conclusions about national distribution, SKU scale, recognition, saturated shelves, whitespace, margins, minimum viable velocity or a launch-country ranking. **Customer interviews and observed choice/repeat are required** before relying on a proposed taste, caffeine, occasion or access gap. **Technical/legal review and assays are required** before relying on formula feasibility, product effect, claims or shelf-life performance.

The research team did not contact anyone or make purchases. The [independent reviews](README.md#review-and-methods) document challenges and resolutions; the [publication checks](supporting/publication-checks.md) record arithmetic, links, scope and the publishing procedure.
