# US focus, productivity and energy workstream

Research date: **17 October 2026 (Europe/Prague)**. Geography: US commercial products, with original non-US launch history retained where relevant. Status: research complete for integration; commercial and formulation gaps remain explicit. This workstream covers **11 qualifying canned/bottled products and one labelled historical comparator**, rather than an exhaustive market census. The lead agent owns shared indexes, final synthesis, EU context and publishing.

Files: [structured product data](../data/focus-energy.csv), [source register](focus-energy-sources.csv), [integrated report](../report.md).

## Findings

Focus deserves its own category. Neutonic explicitly sells the work session; Magic Mind sells a daily cognitive ritual; FOCUSfactor transfers a brain-health identity into a can. Monster and Red Bull remain useful controls for taste, availability and the basic caffeine proposition. Red Bull also uses concentration language, so the word “focus” alone cannot define a distinct segment. Classification below uses the lead proposition, occasion and formulation together. [Neutonic exact US SKU, undated, US](https://www.neutonic.com/en-us/products/orange-sunrise-12-pack-us); [FOCUSfactor exact SKU, undated, US](https://focusfactor.com/products/focus-energy-drink-orange); [Red Bull exact SKU, undated, US](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/energydrink/products/red-bull-pink-edition).

There is evidence of commercial expansion, but very uneven evidence of paid repeat demand. Bloom's reported **35 million energy cans in the first year** is more useful than its total supplement-and-beverage company revenue; it remains a company-reported figure. Monster's earnings statement specifically calls Ultra Blue Hawaiian a fast-rising US seller, but supplies no SKU units. Distributor authorizations, fundraising, celebrities, customer testimonials and new shelf sets should remain separate evidence types. [Bloom company release, 1 July 2025, US](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bloom-nutrition-launches-better-for-you-soda-bloom-pop-exclusively-at-walmart-302495845.html); [Monster issuer results, 8 May 2025, US within global report](https://investors.monsterbevcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/monster-beverage-reports-2025-first-quarter-results).

## Four illustrative benchmark comparisons

1. **Neutonic**: explicit work-session positioning with disclosed ingredient doses. The evidence supports a sold product and retail expansion, not proven productivity improvement. The new Walmart formula and existing Orange Sunrise SKU cannot be assumed equivalent from the conflicting disclosures.
2. **Magic Mind Original plus FREE**: paired products that position a small daily shot around mental clarity, with and without caffeine. The FREE formula cannot inherit caffeine-combination findings; its product-specific efficacy and willingness-to-pay evidence remain limited.
3. **Bloom Sparkling Energy**: an accessible-price/retail-scale contrast in this workstream. Reported can sales are directly relevant to liquid drinks, while influencer reach, powder sales and total company revenue are excluded from the success inference.
4. **UPDATE**: useful customer-problem and reformulation benchmark. It shows demand being sought for controlled energy and less overstimulation, but cannot establish that these effects occur or that paraxanthine belongs in an EU beverage. It is neither CBD-led nor stimulant-free.

These are unranked comparisons of positioning, disclosure and commercial evidence. Their factual basis is the linked table and source register.

## Observed patterns

- **Work-specific focus and general energy overlap.** Neutonic and FOCUSfactor explicitly emphasize cognitive work; established energy brands also use concentration language. Distinct positioning does not establish a distinct cognitive effect or Czech willingness to pay.
- **Caffeine level is a visible product distinction.** Magic Mind and Odyssey offer different caffeine levels across their lines. UPDATE's zero-caffeine claim coexists with paraxanthine stimulation; it is not evidence of a non-stimulant formula.
- **Fruit flavors recur.** Citrus, berry, tropical and lemonade flavors appear repeatedly. Odyssey's founder described orange-ginger as polarizing and a reason to refine flavors. That account is not a controlled taste test or proof of discontinuation. [F22, 3 November 2025, US](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/11/03/functional-energy-drinks-odyssey-on-clean-energy-and-cognitive-boost/).
- **Shots and cans have different observed price and occasion profiles.** Magic Mind's bottled shots emphasize a compact routine and have higher observed prices per serving than many sampled cans. These are retail observations, not manufacturing costs or margins.
- **Adjunct doses are often missing.** Named botanicals and branded ingredients are common, but their inclusion does not establish an effective dose or an incremental effect beyond caffeine.

## Contradictions, exclusions and scientific limits

**Neutonic:** the exact US Orange Sunrise page says **355ml** and lists rhodiola rather than alpha-GPC. Its generic collection copy still says 330ml, while the October 2026 Walmart release names alpha-GPC. This could reflect regional/SKU changes or stale copy; the evidence does not resolve it. The dataset uses the exact US SKU and does not claim the future/new retail assortment has the same formulation. The planned nearly-1,000-store rollout is not counted as audited in-stock doors. [F02, 1 October 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neutonic-expands-us-retail-footprint-with-launch-in-walmart-stores-302895438.html); [F03, accessed 17 October 2026](https://www.neutonic.com/en-us/products/orange-sunrise-12-pack-us).

**Historical dates:** UPDATE's company founding is June 2022; a July 2023 founder interview carries a live purchase offer. The exact first commercial date is unresolved. Phorm's 2025 partnership launch likewise does not erase the earlier 1st Phorm Energy product. C4's 2026 design refresh is explicitly not a formula change and is excluded from the recent-event cohort. [F14, 11 July 2023](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/caffeine-gets-an-update-the-future-of-instant/id1121467751?i=1000620780128); [F45, 2023 historical exhibit](https://ttabvue.uspto.gov/ttabvue/ttabvue-91269945-OPP-35.pdf); [F43, 11 September 2026](https://blog.priceplow.com/energy-drinks/c4-smart-energy).

**Efficacy:** one randomized crossover trial tested a C4 Smart Energy formulation in 45 adult gamers and reported improvements on several cognitive tests versus an inactive placebo. It did not isolate the contribution of added nootropics from caffeine and reported a blood-pressure increase. That is more direct than an ingredient-only study, but does not establish sustained workplace productivity or equivalence to today's can. A separate 2026 C4-versus-caffeine-matched study in 116 participants found no between-group attention advantage; exact C4 SKU equivalence must be checked before treating it as a direct replication. [F46, 2023 online/January 2024 issue](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37186677/); [F47, 2026](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/282BA962234A78ECE314F5CAD201E85D/S2048679026101220a.pdf/consumption_of_caffeinecontaining_beverages_improves_sustained_attention_and_alertness_but_has_no_effect_on_strength_or_static_muscular_endurance.pdf).

**Exclusions:** Neutonic pouches, capsules and powder blends; Bloom energy sticks; and supplement-only cognitive brands do not qualify. Their existence is mentioned only to prevent whole-brand revenue being misattributed to cans. No THC products appear in this workstream. None of the selected products declares CBD/THC in the retrieved materials; this is not an independent laboratory finding.

**Discontinuation:** no focus/energy SKU was independently confirmed discontinued during 2025–26 in this workstream. A missing listing, localized stockout, a customer's complaint or a seasonal limited release is insufficient proof. Documented discontinuations from other categories are covered in the integrated report.

## Research limits

Czech customer demand, price acceptance, sensory preferences, repeat purchase, manufacturing feasibility and effects of the exact current formulas were not established. No people were contacted, purchases made, supplier quotes obtained or consumer tests performed.
