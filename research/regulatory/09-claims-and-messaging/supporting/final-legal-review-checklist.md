# Focused final legal-review checklist

**17 October 2026 · to be completed for the exact can/bottle, formula, serving instructions, countries and advertising.** This is an action protocol, not evidence that any item is already cleared. A contract manufacturer or ingredient supplier does not replace the food business operator's responsibility.

| Review decision | Evidence to provide | Release condition / unresolved issue |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Category and countries** | Actual RTD can/bottle volume; ordinary beverage versus supplement/medicine assessment; intended EU markets; daily consumption | Check each country. A bottled shot is a format, not a legal exemption. Apply Czech oral melatonin ≥0.5 mg/day practice; do not infer a food route from an EU claim. |
| **2. Ingredient-use permission** | Exact identities, Latin species/part/extraction, nutrient forms, production processes, specifications; novel-food/ordinary-food use basis | Match the actual beverage category, process, limits and restrictions. Pending applications, supplement permissions and US GRAS/retail listings are insufficient. |
| **3. Claim inventory** | Every sentence/name/image/icon across front/back, outer pack, e-shop, collection names, search ads, social/influencer material and adopted reviews | Classify nutrition, specific health, general wellbeing, sensory/occasion, factual and medicinal implications. Assess the combined message and average consumer understanding. |
| **4. Authorised wording and attribution** | Current register record, binding act, exact official Czech wording; each proposed paraphrase | Preserve the authorised meaning and endpoint, including “normal”/“contributes”. Do not attribute B5/Mg effects to CBD, botanicals or a blend. Productivity, perceived energy and no-crash promises need their own basis. |
| **5. Caffeine exception dossier, only if pursued** | Exact claim ID/endpoint, current status, Article 13 category, Article 28 paragraph, historic national use/evaluation/filing evidence and Czech interpretation | Do not approve from ≥75 mg or EFSA science alone. Distinguish the vetoed 2016 draft and refused 40–75 mg application. If evidence is absent, omit caffeine effect wording. |
| **6. Botanical transition, only if pursued** | Species/preparation, exact on-hold record, proposed meaning, lawful historical use and notification/application evidence, current decisions, scientific dossier | Apply C-386/23 and the proper 28(5) or 28(6) route. Neither a pending ID nor the 2008 EFSA receipt date settles eligibility. A new business does not itself prove or disprove the historical claim route. |
| **7. Amount and serving basis** | Per-100-ml and per-package nutrient levels, NRVs, true portion count, available forms, all-source exposure, process and shelf-life assays | Confirm 7.5%/100 ml versus genuine one-portion 15% route; high doubles source. Use elemental minerals. Do not rely on a nominal input at the legal floor or a raw-premix certificate. |
| **8. Caffeine and other warnings** | All-source caffeine assay, mg/L and mg/100 ml calculation, actual base/name, label layout, target group | Above150 mg/L: Annex III warning/quantity and same-field-of-vision placement unless exact coffee/tea exception. Check all other ingredient-specific warnings and national rules. Per-can statement does not replace mandatory units. |
| **9. Nutrition claims** | Sugars/energy/fibre/protein analysis; sweetening purpose of ingredients; comparator evidence if reduced claim | Low sugars≤2.5 g/100 ml; sugars-free≤0.5 g/100 ml; no-added requires neither added sugars nor food used for sweetening. Reduced sugars≥30%, equal/lower energy and compliant same-category comparison. Fibre uses mass or energy route; protein energy share. |
| **10. Secondary benefits** | Exact material/intake and target group; hydration osmolality/carbohydrate/sodium; inulin identity; culture viability | Match the full exercise, bowel, water or lactose-digestion claim. Sugar-free status alone does not decide carbohydrate-electrolyte eligibility; glucose polymers may supply qualifying carbohydrate. No general recovery, gut or hydration superiority extrapolation. |
| **11. General claims and layout** | Actual sized artwork and all linked disclosures | Related specific claim with substantive and perceptible visual connection. Use C-524/18 conditions; no routine back-panel fine-print cure. Include diet/lifestyle, consumption pattern and applicable avoidance/excess information under Art10(2). |
| **12. Czech language and borderline terms** | Czech translations; proof for natural/source/process statements; proposed probiotic/prebiotic wording; tonic composition/name | Current official national practice, dated guidance and superior EU law must be reconciled. Check tonic's quinine/carbonation/descriptor scope. Do not treat 2017 probiotic guidance as EU authorisation or current safe harbour. |
| **13. Claims and testimony** | Scripts, selected customer quotes, professional endorsements, ingredient-study links and moderation process | No disease-treatment or prohibited individual-doctor recommendations. Authentic testimonials and “results vary”/US disclaimers do not legalise unsupported claims. Keep sensory claims truthful too. |
| **14. Separate CBD release gate** | Exact Commission beverage-use route, national classification, food-safety dossier, supplier traceability, independent cannabinoid/contaminant assays, emulsion/particle/stability data | No established route in this research. February/September2026 supplement opinions do not authorise an RTD. No CBD focus/calm/sleep claim. A nutrient claim cannot cure an unlawful CBD ingredient. |
| **15. Evidence and business expectations** | Ingredient/finished-product evidence separated; study dose/route/population/endpoint transfer analysis; concept/taste/paid-repeat data | Label legal eligibility is not a prediction of felt efficacy. Do not invent sales, tests or customer feedback. Review expectations created by names and advertising before ordering an outsourced run. |

Keep a dated decision sheet per claim and per country, recording approved exact copy, the permitted formula/portion range, evidence owner and change triggers. Re-review after any ingredient, supplier, serving, processing, packaging, language, advertisement or market change that could affect the conclusion.
