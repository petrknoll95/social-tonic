## Evidence protocol

- Search current official Commission register and deferred-claim resources; verify binding wording and conditions in EUR-Lex. Register absence alone does not settle a transitional claim. Record inaccessible interfaces and unresolved status explicitly.
- Prioritise full judgments over press summaries, and Czech-language SZPI/MZe guidance for Czech practice. Guidance and EFSA opinions are distinguished from binding authorization.
- Use US brand material only for what the identified can/bottle says, and dated announcements only for the event actually evidenced. Do not infer efficacy, achieved sell-through, SKU sales or EU legality. No powders, pouches, sachets, gummies, consumer concentrates or other excluded forms are benchmarks.
- Separate legal eligibility, scientific efficacy, safety, ingredient-use permission and commercial demand. Studies using other forms are relevant only with an explicit delivery/dose/population/duration transfer assessment.
- Source tables record URLs, publisher, publication date (unknown/undated where absent), access date, geography, supported claims and limitations. Avoid unauthorised source copies and long quotations; legal texts and short product phrases are referenced directly.
- Numerical examples are hypothetical, never a recommended or safety-cleared formula. Evaluate ready-to-consume quantities at end of shelf life, pack size and the actual consumption instruction.
- No contacts, purchases, fieldwork or consumer tests are performed. Validation and legal-review protocols are proposed work.
