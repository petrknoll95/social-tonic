# Range and format strategy for a functional RTD launch

**17 October 2026 · US 2025–2026 benchmarks; Czech/EU launch assessment · Desk research and conditional recommendation, not validated demand or an approved formula.**

## Recommendation

**Validate a single-category daytime drink first: one CBD-free core formula, one size, one lead flavour.** Compare 250 ml with 330 ml cans before selecting one; use 250 ml as the initial brief hypothesis. Test two flavours, then manufacture only the winner unless the second independently earns its production run. A pleasant, clearly portioned drink for a work break is a more tractable starting proposition than separate “focus” and “energy” products. Its switching advantage remains unproven against existing Czech drinks and ordinary routines.

Keep a **caffeine-free evening/social drink as a challenger launch**, not an automatic second SKU. A broader daytime/evening range is viable only after both occasions demonstrate paid demand and compatible economics. Do not prescribe a daily system or imply one drink compensates for another. Dedicated sleep, high-stimulant energy, hydration/recovery, gut-health and CBD extensions should each earn their own case.

Czechia is the practical first research location for this founder, not a predetermined winning market. No production order is justified by the desk research alone. The [provisional brief](product-brief.md) marks every unsettled specification; the [validation protocol](supporting/validation-and-rfq-protocol.md) makes the recommendation falsifiable.

## Scope, evidence and existing research

Only **finished liquids sold in cans or bottles** are product benchmarks: qualifying RTD tea/coffee and bottled shots are included; consumer powders, sachets, liquid/solid pouches, tablets, capsules, gummies, gels, drops, dilution products and cartons are excluded. An ingredient study in another form is assessed separately for transfer to a processed, stored drink. The primary product is CBD-free; THC-led/intoxicating products are outside the intended scope.

**Facts** below are source-supported observations; **brand/supplier claims** remain attributed; **estimates** are calculations with stated inputs; **hypotheses** require customer or product testing; **unknown** means not established. A launch or subscription offer does not demonstrate repeat purchase. Whole-brand revenue including excluded formats is never assigned to a liquid line. Commercial demand, scientific effect and legal permission are separate questions.

## Three viable architectures, with different conditions for launch

All counts, portions, sensory directions and price ideas below are **hypotheses requiring testing, quotes and legal review**. “Viable” means a coherent route worth testing, not an economically or legally cleared offer.

| Architecture | Customer rationale and recognisability | Range, occasion and brand | Trade-offs and operational implications | What earns launch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. One daytime platform — recommended** | Adult work-break buyer wants enjoyable refreshment and clear caffeine information; familiar citrus/tea cues reduce explanation. Morning and early-day use can share one job | One core formula; 1 hero flavour, second flavour conditional; one 250 **or**330 ml can. Focus/energy are one initial proposition, with wording subject to review | Simplest exposure message and stock plan. Cannot also satisfy strict caffeine avoiders. Must beat familiar cheaper drinks on a concrete reason; merely using less caffeine is not unique locally | Taste/portion preference, acceptable actual price, sufficient SKU-level paid repeat, lawful ingredients/wording and viable minimum run |
| **B. One caffeine-free evening platform — alternative first launch** | Adult wants an enjoyable transition or drink with dinner/social company; sensory ritual can be valuable without a promised sensation | One caffeine-free, CBD-free core; 1 hero flavour, possibly 2 later; 330 ml can lead hypothesis, small screw-cap glass bottle only if a local hospitality route wins. No dedicated sleep claim or melatonin prerequisite | More separation from mainstream energy; less dependable daily frequency and unknown willingness to pay. Social, private wind-down and sleep are different jobs: choose one lead occasion. Glass may raise delivered cost/minimums | Outperforms A on a paid switching reason and contribution; customers buy without requiring a “buzz,” sedation or proven stress relief |
| **C. Two occasion platforms — staged expansion** | Same customer may want a daytime drink and a caffeine-free evening option, but cross-purchase is unmeasured |2 formulas ×1 flavour each, shared container where feasible; clearly named occasions and unmistakable caffeine information. Launch winner first; second later | Broader relevance and potential cross-selling, offset by separate formula development, safety/claims, QC, forecasts and slow stock. Mixed packs introduce ratios/fulfilment constraints. Not a prescribed daily system | Each SKU independently repeats and pays for its run; brand is understood across both; combined exposure reviewed. A small simultaneous pilot is defensible only if both gates already pass |

**A versus B is a real decision, not an automatic funnel into C.** If neither creates a viable switch, defer custom production. A generic energy launch is not the fallback just because its category is larger.

Focus and energy usually belong under the same initial brand when they describe one task-break choice. A separate high-caffeine tier would invite dose confusion and split demand before a second need is demonstrated. Relaxation can fit under a broader adult-drinks brand built around taste and occasions, but will strain a name or identity built exclusively around stimulation, extreme performance or “sleep.” Test unaided understanding of both products and whether the brand implies medical effects. Retaining `social-tonic` as the repository name does not select the consumer brand.

## Common core versus several formulas

A shared core means the **same intended functional ingredients and dose**, not identical finished recipes. Flavour changes can alter acidity, bitterness masking, colour, sediment, stability and can compatibility. Shared active procurement and communication help, but each flavour still needs a specification, artwork, inventory forecast and justified quality plan. A co-packer may charge a new recipe fee or require a full run for every flavour.

Two different formulas create extra sourcing, dosage, interaction and claims work even if they use the same can. Two formulas ×two flavours ×two sizes already creates **eight liquid SKUs**. Pack bundles add further sellable/fulfilment items without being new liquids. For example, two flavour-specific 12-packs plus one mixed 12-pack are three packing configurations; the mixed pack can stop selling when one flavour runs out.

**Add a flavour when preference variety increases paid trial/repeat enough to cover its own run; add a function when a distinct occasion does the same.** Neither is inherently the correct second product. Avoid seasonal flavours, a stronger-energy edition and “day/night/restore” systems merely to make the range look established.

## Can or bottle, serving size and sensory targets

Engineering comparisons are reasoned implications requiring package/process validation, not product-specific life-cycle or cost results. Supplier evidence and limits are in the [operations memo](supporting/packaging-and-operations.md).

| Finished format | Useful job / advantage | Principal constraint | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **250 ml aluminum can** | Portable, short single occasion; light barrier; clearly bounded serving | Not resealable; price/volume comparison with 330 ml; dose concentration and liner/seam compatibility | Lead hypothesis for A; test against 330 ml |
| **330–355 ml can** | Familiar soda-length refreshment/social ritual; more sensory room | More liquid to ship/finish; US 355 ml does not require EU 355 ml tooling |330 ml alternative; do not launch both sizes |
| **200–330 ml glass bottle** | Premium tea/adult-drink presentation; screw cap can reseal | Weight/breakage, light in clear glass, separate cap stock; crown caps generally do not reseal. Returnable economics require actual collection/washing | Valid alternative if local channel preference pays for it |
| **400–500 ml PET/rPET screw-cap bottle** | Carry and sip over a longer hydration/travel occasion | Food-contact/closure/process/barrier specification; partial consumption and whole-bottle dose | Later distinct hydration need; no initial duplication |
| **60–100 ml bottled RTD shot** | Quick, portable small-volume ritual | Little refreshment; concentrated sensory challenges; small pack does not mean small production minimum | Defer unless shot preference is demonstrated |
| **750 ml–1 L resealable finished RTD bottle** | At-home shared/repeated pours, no preparation | Dose per pour/bottle, settling, opened-life hygiene, carbonation loss and repeat exposure; resealing does not control dose | Defer for active-led/caffeinated range |

Aluminum bottles can provide resealability but add line/closure uncertainty without a validated need. No material is declared environmentally superior: actual weight, recycled content, transport, collection and return rates are unknown.

Compare **light sparkle** with still in development, then choose one. Sparkle may suit citrus refreshment/social drinking; a still tea or small wind-down bottle can fit customers avoiding bubbles. Neither is inherently more functional. Initial daytime targets are dry-to-lightly-sweet, **0–2.5 g sugars/100 ml** and approximately **5–25 kcal/250 ml**, with clean finish and little bitterness. These are sensory/nutrition hypotheses, not required legal claims. Sweetness intensity is not sugar content; caloric ingredients other than sugar matter. The upper sugar and calorie targets may conflict in a finished recipe. See the [tagged brief](product-brief.md).

EU “low sugars” permits at most 2.5 g/100 ml and “sugars-free” at most 0.5 g/100 ml; “energy-free” has a separate 4 kcal/100 ml ceiling. A US zero-sugar label does not settle an EU label. Any sweetener, claim, beverage/supplement classification and final nutrient values need review. [EU nutrition-claim rules, consolidated 13 Dec 2014](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?qid=1787446686193&uri=CELEX%3A02006R1924-20141213).

## Trial, repeat, multipacks and price

Use **single cans for physical trial**, a 4/6-can pickup/paid-delivery trial pack, and a 12-pack for wanted replenishment. Keep 24-packs and subscriptions for customers who demonstrate that need. A subscription button on a US brand is evidence of an offer, not retention. Optional cadence, skip/pause and no daily-consumption implication are preferable to a mandatory monthly day/night bundle.

Czech asking-price anchors accessed 17 Oct 2026: **CANS Performance raspberry 330 ml at CZK 39.50**; **Vilgain Energy & Focus lemon/lime 330 ml at CZK 45**, with 12/24 packs at CZK 41/38 per can. These are offers, not willingness-to-pay measurements. Vilgain's page contains different flavour recipes; nutrition cannot be freely transferred. CANS' retailer nutrition table has anomalous entries, so it is not used as a validated formula. [dm](https://www.dm.cz/p/d/3149633/cans-performance), [Aktin](https://aktin.cz/vilgain-energy-focus-drink-2/12x44884).

Test **CZK 39/45/49/55 per single can including VAT**; 49 is only a working anchor. A 250 ml can at 49 must justify a premium against 330 ml local controls. The Czech NIQ report published 2 Mar 2026 says **70.2% of energy revenue was promotional** in the 52 weeks ending 30 Nov 2025. It also reports over-litre growth driven by **multipacks**: that is not evidence consumers want litre-sized single containers. **Price is not brand margin.** At 49 CZK shelf price, assumed 21%VAT, 35%retailer margin, 15%distributor margin, 22 manufactured cost and 2 variable trade cost, calculated contribution is **−1.63 CZK/can via a distributor**, versus **+2.32 direct to retailer**, before acquisition and fixed costs. ## Safety, functional dose and claims remain constraints

The provisional 40–60 mg caffeine concept range is **not an approved formula or efficacy promise**. Caffeine has a firmer acute-alertness rationale than many complex blends, but exact-product productivity, no-crash and universal tolerance are unproven. Dose must account for other drinks and timing; EFSA adult reference levels are not consumption targets. The 2025 theanine/caffeine study used capsules and sleep-deprived adults without a caffeine-only comparator, so it does not isolate an incremental theanine benefit in a shelf-stable can. [EFSA caffeine assessment, 2015](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/caffeine), [primary study, 12 Aug 2025](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114525104169).

Pack size changes labeling:50 mg in 330 ml equals 151.5 mg/l, above the 150 mg/l covered-beverage warning threshold; 60 mg in 250 ml equals 240 mg/l. Specific coffee/tea naming exceptions are not an exemption simply because a formula contains tea. Scientific support for alertness and legal permission for wording are separate; a 40–75 mg caffeine alertness claim was specifically refused in 2016. No proposed product name or benefit copy is approved here. [Food-information regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN-IT/TXT/?fromTab=ALL&uri=CELEX%3A32011R1169), [claim decision](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/CS/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R1411).

Before any dual range, assess one/two cans, each cross-SKU combination and realistic background caffeine/nutrients/other supplements. Repeated magnesium/B 6 premixes can accumulate; “caffeine-free” does not make an evening blend safe for unrestricted consumption. Do not claim evening products neutralize caffeine. Review exact forms, interactions, age/medication exclusions and foreseeable multi-serving use. No combined-use assessment has been completed.

Ingredient identity can change the legal answer. New 2026 decisions distinguish a particular microbial theanine route and particular fruiting-body mushroom extracts; they are not universal permissions or prohibitions. Authorized normal-function nutrient claims do not establish extra performance or sedation. Botanical on-hold claims need a valid legal route under the 2025 CJEU ruling. The [safety memo](supporting/safety-and-regulatory-gates.md) preserves direct official decisions, science transfer limits and new findings that refine earlier generic repository cautions. EU packaging rules and Czech producer responsibilities also need allocation in the production contract; a filler does not remove founder obligations. [Packaging review](supporting/packaging-and-operations.md).

## CBD: three independent strategic choices

**Recommend no CBD extension in the initial roadmap commitment.** Maintain an option to research one later; the primary proposition must succeed without it. Czech CBD-only incremental demand, an effective safe drink dose, lawful exact ingredient use and viable claims remain unestablished. US interest, UK CBD purchases and THC growth are not interchangeable evidence.

| Choice | Incremental commercial case | Compliance, safety, cost and brand implications | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No CBD extension** | Gives up possible CBD-seeking buyers, whose incremental Czech demand is unknown; preserves clear mainstream occasion | Fewer formula/analytical/emulsion/label/channel dependencies; baseline still needs ordinary food compliance | Preferred; validate taste, occasion and repeat |
| **Later CBD version** | Existing buyers may pay more, or merely switch away from the original; demand uplift/cannibalisation unknown | New formula/run, traceable lawful ingredient, aqueous stability/dose uniformity, independent cannabinoid/contaminant tests, claims and restrictions. Similar flavour increases confusion risk | Conditional only after a lawful drink route, safety and incremental economics are demonstrated |
| **Separately positioned CBD SKU** | A distinct adult CBD-seeking segment might justify separate acquisition/channel economics | Clearer separation, but extra brand/education/inventory overhead; separate identity does not change legal or safety requirements | Defer; assess independently if evidence supports a distinct segment |

EFSA's September 2026 favourable opinion concerns a **specified synthetic CBD in MCT-oil supplements at 2 mg/day**, not an aqueous RTD or Commission marketing authorization. February guidance and the separate May opinion also contain formulation/population limits. Czech SZPI has documented enforcement against CBD foods; no applicable beverage authorization or CBD calm/focus/sleep claim was established. The 150 mg sublingual insomnia pilot does not validate low-dose social cans. [EFSA opinion, 18 Sep 2026](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154), [SZPI, 29 May 2025](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/soubor/27-2025-redigovano.aspx), [clinical study, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38174873/).

Sourcing must identify the authorized exact material/use, food-grade traceability, independent assays with THC detection limits, contaminants, emulsifier/nano status and finished-container stability. No supplier is qualified and no CBD cost per can is estimated. The existing Daytrip CBD page still makes 25 mg/can claims but shows an unavailable offer in this check; an earlier retailer flyer could not be refetched. It supplies no new traction evidence and is not counted as a current sold-product benchmark. [Current listing, accessed 17 Oct 2026](https://wearedaytrip.com/products/sparkling). See the [full independent CBD assessment](supporting/safety-and-regulatory-gates.md).

## Launch first, earn later, or stop

1. **First:** compare A and B with real recent buyers and a no-purchase option; test size/flavour/sweetness sequentially. Choose one occasion and one hero SKU only after a switching reason survives its actual price.
2. **Before filling:** obtain comparable written outsourced quotes and exact ingredient/claims clearance; validate formula/process/package, shelf life and end-of-life composition. Use the feasible run and saleable window to set SKU-specific demand hurdles.
3. **Then:** a narrow paid local pilot with singles and a practical replenishment pack; measure unpromoted sell-through, paid cohort repeat and contribution. Subscription and broad distribution follow evidence rather than manufacture it.
4. **Defer:** second flavour, daypart pair, high-energy tier, sleep/melatonin, CBD, shot, larger hydration bottle, gut/protein/recovery hybrids and multi-serve formats. Hydration earns a larger resealable portion only for a distinct exertion/heat/travel job; gut soda must win taste/tolerance and repeat; protein/recovery changes processing and meal expectations. None belongs simply to complete a portfolio.
5. **Stop or redesign** if preferred taste cannot carry the price, buyers need unsupported effects, compliant ingredients cannot support the proposition, or minimum runs exceed credible demand. A smaller outsourced learning programme is preferable to committing stock on hypothetical subscription retention.
