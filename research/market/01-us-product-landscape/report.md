# US functional RTD landscape and European opportunity assessment

**Research cut-off: 17 October 2026 · US commercial benchmarks · EU/Czech founder implications**

The most useful first test is a **CBD-free drink for a specific focused-work occasion**, compared against a caffeine-free evening drink and a simple hydration proposition. Caffeine offers the clearest acute ingredient rationale, but a “productivity” label does not establish better performance than an ordinary caffeinated drink. A caffeine-free cognitive concept deserves a separate demand test; its immediate-effect evidence is weaker. Keep CBD as a separately gated research extension. This is an analyst recommendation about the next experiment, not a predetermined brand, formula, budget or launch country.

The landscape contains **31 exact liquid product records**: 12 focus/energy, 8 relaxation/sleep records with no declared cannabinoids, 3 CBD including one historical withdrawal, and 8 secondary-category products. One secondary record crosses into energy and is counted once. The [dated landscape table](landscape.md) and [consolidated dataset](data/product-landscape.csv) preserve launch chronology, current packaging, disclosed doses, caffeine, prices, claims, timing, occasions, distribution, confidence and unknowns. See [scope and files](README.md), [methods](supporting/research-protocol.md) and [sources](sources.csv).

## Taxonomy: classify the job, then the active approach

| Category | Consumer job and boundary | Useful comparators | Main uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus/productivity | Sustained work or study, mental clarity and task performance are central; not merely the word “focus” on an energy label | Neutonic, Magic Mind Original/FREE, FOCUSfactor, C4 Smart Energy | Incremental benefit beyond caffeine; willingness to pay |
| Alertness/energy | Wakefulness, stimulation, workout or general pick-me-up | Monster, Red Bull, Bloom, Phorm; Odyssey overlaps focus | Crowded distribution and taste competition; high dose is not differentiation |
| Caffeine-free stimulation | Stimulant energy without caffeine as the named molecule | UPDATE/paraxanthine | “Caffeine-free” is not “non-stimulant”; EU use authorization unresolved |
| Calm/daytime reset | Less keyed-up feeling while remaining functional | Recess Mood, TRIP US Mindful Blend, VYBES Mood | Ingredient doses, acute vs repeated-use expectations |
| Social tonic | An adult drink and ritual for social participation or alcohol substitution | Hiyo | Sensory/occasion value versus unproven pharmacological “float” |
| Sleep/wind-down | Bedtime transition, sleep onset/quality or nighttime recovery | BRĒZ Dream, Sly DREAM; Blue Bear and Cheribundi contextual | Strong promises, missing doses, timing, national ingredient/medicine rules |
| Hydration/recovery | Fluid/electrolyte replacement in a defined exertion, heat or travel context | Gatorade Lower Sugar, Electrolit | Benefit over water for the intended consumer; full-bottle nutrition |
| Gut-health soda | Enjoyable soda replacement with fiber/gut positioning | Poppi, OLIPOP, Simply Pop | Fiber type/dose, tolerability, authorized claims and repeat purchase |
| Protein/recovery hybrid | Convenient protein in a refreshing liquid; optionally fiber or energy | Clean Simple Eats Clear Protein Soda, Beyond Immerse | Processing, sensory stability, economics and added complexity |
| CBD calm extension | CBD-containing but not THC-led; separate from CBD-free drinks above | Daytrip CBD, Recess Original; historical VYBES CBD | Demand, actual CBD/THC specification, efficacy and legal route |

Every category can contain more than one ingredient approach. Classifications reflect positioning, not established effects. For example, Red Bull also mentions concentration; that does not make its primary proposition a specialized productivity drink. Unknown cannabinoid status in a dataset is not a verified cannabinoid-free certification.

## Demand, commercial success and efficacy are different evidence sets

**Demand signals.** IFIC's US 2025 survey found energy/less fatigue sought by 40% and digestive/gut health by 37%; the full report was published in January 2026, although fieldwork was in 2025. Its October 2026 release reports energy at 36%, with healthy aging taking the lead. The 2026 US sample was 3,005 adults aged 18–80, weighted separately from Canada. These are self-reported desired benefits across foods, drinks and nutrients, not purchases of functional cans or proof of Czech willingness to pay. [2025 report](https://ific.org/wp-content/uploads/2025-IFIC-Food-Health-Survey-Full-Report.pdf), [2026 results](https://ific.org/media/trying-harder-yet-feeling-worse-americans-self-reported-health-status-hits-a-record-low/), [2026 methods](https://ific.org/research/2026-ific-food-health-survey-a-focus-on-food-nutrition/).

CBD demand evidence is weaker than the broad wellness narrative suggests. A US study published in November 2024 found 58% of 2,010 respondents would **or might** try CBD beverages; that combines firm and tentative interest, predates this commercial window, and measures neither actual purchase nor Czech demand. It is not a CBD-drink sales forecast. [Staples, Journal of Wine Economics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-wine-economics/article/canning-cannabis-consumer-preferences-for-cbd-and-thcinfused-beverages/4343A6856F99D7C186836D32ADBD810A).

**Commercial signals.** Bloom's reported 35 million first-year cans concern its energy line, which is more relevant than its broader supplement business. Monster's earnings commentary specifically identifies Ultra Blue Hawaiian as a fast-selling product, but gives no SKU units. Other strong-looking signals—Hiyo financing, Neutonic store expansion, Recess/VYBES rankings and the Poppi transaction—have different meanings and limitations. None establishes Czech product-market fit. The row-level evidence and attribution appear in the [landscape](landscape.md) and [workstream notes](README.md).

Hiyo's supplied Fitt article reports $20.8 million funding, a **projection** of more than one million cases in 2026, and expansion toward 13,000-plus doors. It does not report audited achieved annual cases or store-level sell-through. Its broad functional-beverage market headline is not used as the size of social tonics or this project's addressable market. [Fitt Insider, 5 October 2026](https://insider.fitt.co/hiyo-raises-20-8m-to-scale-social-tonics/).

**Efficacy.** Caffeine is a better-supported acute alertness rationale than a proprietary multi-herb “brain” blend. A 45-person trial of C4S reported selected cognitive improvements versus placebo, but it cannot isolate added nootropics from caffeine. A separate 2026 study of a C4 drink against a 200 mg caffeine-matched control found no between-group sustained-attention advantage; it is not necessarily the same C4 Smart Energy SKU and is not a direct replication. Neither establishes real-world productivity or every energy drink's effects. [C4S trial, online 2023/issue 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37186677/), [C4 comparison, 3 August 2026](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/282BA962234A78ECE314F5CAD201E85D/S2048679026101220a.pdf/consumption_of_caffeinecontaining_beverages_improves_sustained_attention_and_alertness_but_has_no_effect_on_strength_or_static_muscular_endurance.pdf).

The [evidence memo](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md) compares study doses, delivery, duration and populations with RTDs. Examples: chronic ashwagandha capsule results do not establish an immediate social effect; lion's mane trials are small and mixed; a CBD sleep pilot at 150 mg sublingually does not validate a low-dose can. Disclosed ingredient presence is not proof of an effective dose. No validated onset/duration was found for most exact SKUs; brand timing statements are retained as claims.

## Seven patterns to investigate for Europe

1. **Own an occasion before adding ingredients.** Neutonic's work language, Hiyo's social ritual and bedtime-specific products create different reasons to buy. Test Czech wording for a concrete moment—morning work, afternoon reset or evening transition. Hypothesis: clarity of occasion matters more than a long ingredient list. It remains untested locally.
2. **Dose transparency may differentiate; more caffeine is not the only route.** The sample spans 55 mg shots, 85 mg mushroom cans and 200–222 mg energy cans. Run caffeine-matched comparisons and disclose per container. UPDATE shows why “caffeine-free” needs a separate stimulant flag. EU caffeine labeling depends on concentration, not just total can dose. [Evidence/regulatory memo](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md).
3. **A familiar fruit flavor can make a functional proposition accessible.** Citrus/tropical/berry formats recur across energy, mood and gut-health products; floral/chamomile/vanilla cues differentiate evening occasions. This is an observed sample pattern, not evidence that Czech consumers prefer US sweetness. Blind-test sweetness, bitterness, acidity and aftertaste before branded claims.
4. **CBD-free calm has independent commercial logic.** VYBES' pivot, Recess Mood, TRIP's US formula and Hiyo demonstrate distinct non-CBD approaches. Separate “I want to relax” from “I want CBD.” Regional variants must be tracked at SKU level: US TRIP Mindful Blend does not inherit CBD from European/UK brand pages. [Variant analysis](supporting/relax-sleep-cbd.md).
5. **Format and stability can be as consequential as the functional story.** Bottled shots sell a compact routine; pantry-stable cans support different logistics from refrigerated products. OLIPOP's 6 g and 9 g versions must not be conflated. Start with a contract packer's existing can/bottle and process capability; validate shelf-life potency and taste before custom packaging. [Secondary analysis](supporting/secondary-categories.md), [production protocol](supporting/validation-and-outsourcing-plan.md).
6. **Formula drift is a commercial signal and a research hazard.** VYBES changed its active proposition, Beyond changed fiber/sweetening composition, TRIP's current formula differs from older material, and Neutonic's PR conflicts with its SKU page. Track dated label versions and retain contradictions. A design refresh, such as C4's 2026 refresh, is not material reformulation. [Focus notes](supporting/focus-energy.md), [secondary notes](supporting/secondary-categories.md).
7. **Sleep, gut health and recovery need narrower claims than US marketing often suggests.** Bedtime use is not proof of sleep improvement; a few grams of mixed fiber do not automatically qualify for a specific EU inulin claim; electrolytes do not automatically outperform water in office life. Protein+fiber+energy hybrids merit watching, but combining three jobs adds cost and technical risk before demand is established. [EU/evidence screen](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md).

## Opportunity ranking across categories

This is an **ordinal analyst judgment**, not a market forecast or a measured weighted score. Criteria: frequency/specificity of occasion; credible benefit; EU route using conventional permitted ingredients; ability to differentiate; and inexpensive outsourced validation. Legal feasibility is a gate, not a score that strong demand can offset. Ranks can change after Czech evidence.

| Priority | Opportunity | Evidence / feasibility judgment | What would change the ranking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CBD-free moderate-caffeine focused-work drink | Clear acute ingredient rationale and testable occasion; need sharper value than coffee/ordinary energy | No local switch reason or no premium acceptance; wins only on claimed exotic ingredients |
| 2 | CBD-free, caffeine-free evening transition/calm | Distinct occasion; can start from sensory ritual and permitted ingredients; acute functional promise uncertain | Strong paid repeat at realistic price; conversely, consumers require an unsubstantiated intoxicating-like feeling |
| 3 | Occasion-specific hydration | Understandable use and conventional composition; differentiation is difficult | A narrowly defined underserved use or channel; outperforming a water/established-brand control in repeat choice |
| 4 | Fiber soda / gut-health proposition | Established US competitive activity and familiar daily occasion; claims/tolerance need care | Czech taste/repeat advantage and achievable fiber specification without cost or tolerance penalty |
| 5 | Genuinely non-stimulant focus | Attractive need for caffeine-sensitive adults; ingredient-level acute efficacy weaker | Clear demand even with restrained claims, and credible product-specific evidence |
| 6 | Dedicated sleep drink | Specific problem and occasion, but missing doses and classification/safety complexity increase burden | Legally viable simple formula, credible differentiation and repeat without strong medical promises |
| 7 | Protein/recovery soda or energy hybrid | Distinct refreshing protein option; technical and cost burden, emerging sales evidence | Real local preference and acceptable contract-production economics/stability |
| 8 | Generic high-caffeine energy | Mature demand but difficult founder differentiation and distribution competition | An unusually strong channel or audience advantage not yet established |
| Separate hold | CBD extension | Czech/EU authorization applicable to intended drink not established; dose/effect and local demand unresolved | Specific lawful use route, qualified ingredient/specification, safety and claims review plus independent demand evidence |

See the machine-readable [opportunity ranking](data/opportunity-ranking.csv). A canned/bottled functional coffee or tea can fit priority 1; none was added merely to fill the sample without a verified recent exact-SKU event. Coverage of that format is a gap, not a finding of absent competition.

## European implications, assumptions and next decisions

Keep the primary concept CBD-free. EFSA's 2026 provisional CBD level concerns qualifying supplements and does not authorize drinks. Czech enforcement is documented. Supplier application validation, UK availability, THC crop thresholds and US retail listings do not solve the food-use question. The CBD appendix separately covers demand, effects, sourcing, safety, legality and claims; no THC-led formula is a launch blueprint. [EU/Czech and CBD assessment](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md).

Ingredient permission and advertising permission both matter for the CBD-free options. US citicoline/paraxanthine/adaptogen stacks cannot be copied into an EU can without checking the exact ingredient, process and allowed food category. Authorized nutrient claims may offer a narrower route, but do not substantiate immediate cognitive enhancement or anxiety treatment. All proposed concepts remain subject to a formula-specific screen.

Next, run the [low-cost validation sequence](supporting/validation-and-outsourcing-plan.md): problem interviews, randomized concept/price choices, ingredient-and-label feasibility, outsourced sensory prototypes, then a small paid pilot with reorder measurement. Start Czech-language discovery because it is accessible to the founder; do not choose Czechia as the launch market solely from US evidence. Set a spending ceiling for each stage before commissioning work. No manufacturing quote, interview, taste test or purchase occurred in this research.

Outstanding gaps are exact-SKU velocity and repeat purchase, verified current inventory for some CBD listings, undated reformulations, standardized extract identities, independent whole-drink evidence, Czech category demand/pricing, destination-specific legal clearance, and actual contract-packing costs/minimums. Out-of-stock pages are not treated as discontinuations. No market size is inferred from this sample, funding or the number of brands. The [review record](supporting/independent-review.md) and [secondary review](supporting/secondary-independent-review.md) document independent challenges and resolutions before publication.
