# Beverage benchmark workstream

Prepared 17 October 2026 for the naming research lead. This memo and `beverage.json` cover the 24 preassigned brands under the shared coding framework. Current sites were checked on 17 October; historical scale figures retain their reporting periods. The JSON contains 24 brand records, 32 commercial/status observations and 56 sources, with BEV source identifiers. All tone, semantic, descriptor-independence and pronunciation coding is analyst interpretation unless explicitly identified as documented origin.

## What the evidence supports

The clearest naming lesson is the division of work between identity and explanation. In these **24 deliberately selected names**, 15/24 are coded category-opaque without supporting words, 8/24 partly informative, and 1/24 category-clear. This is a desk judgment, not a comprehension study. Nineteen of 24 names are one whitespace token, including the hyphenated CLUB-MATE and fritz-kola; this does not establish an optimal length. The selected set contains 5 established businesses by scale or longevity, 7 with qualified emerging commercial evidence, 11 with active offers but unverified scale, and 1 discontinued brand. These are heterogeneous evidence categories, not an ordered sales league table.

The sample contains several routes to identity: an explained science/soda blend in OLIPOP; a pause in Recess; shared feeling in Kin Euphorics; a foreign real word in Taika; coastal place associations in Pentire; category-containing compound fritz-kola; and deliberately negative Ugly Drinks. Their existence establishes available strategies, not relative effectiveness. Ghia is coded as an opaque invented-type token provisionally: neither its origin nor actual coinage is established. Excluding or recoding that row is an appropriate sensitivity check before discussing invented-name counts.

A name can accommodate expansion without itself describing each formula. **Recess** currently separates Mood, Zero Proof and its original hemp-labelled offer; **Kin Euphorics** differentiates RTD variants and made-to-mix products; **Ghia** separates aperitif bottles, Le Spritz cans and Le Fizz. On the other hand, fritz-kola's explicit category element has not prevented the related fritz-limo and fritz-spritz families. Broad and specific architectures both occur; the selected evidence does not show that abstraction is inherently superior. [Recess, BEV005](https://takearecess.com/), [Kin, BEV006](https://www.kineuphorics.com/), [Ghia, BEV007](https://drinkghia.com/), [fritz-kola, BEV023](https://fritz-kola.com/en/).

## Scope and current-product corrections

- **CBD is a range attribute.** Recess still has a live original product page identifying hemp/CBD, alongside its separate Mood range. TRIP has a currently listed UK CBD product at Ocado alongside magnesium-led Mindful Blend. Neither brand should be labelled wholly CBD-free. Live pages do not prove postcode-level fulfillment. [Recess original, BEV033](https://takearecess.com/shop/sparkling-water/blackberry-chai), [Ocado, BEV037](https://www.ocado.com/products/trip-cbd-infused-peach-ginger/563192011), [Mindful Blend, BEV038](https://drink-trip.com/products/mindful-blend-variety-two).
- **Daytrip is not a verified single reformulation story.** The legacy site still represents CBD beverages and links separate prebiotic-soda and yerba-mate ranges. The newer pages contain both unavailable wording and purchase controls. Code the architecture and uncertain fulfillment; do not infer that all existing products became CBD-free. [Legacy site, BEV010](https://wearedaytrip.com/), [soda, BEV039](https://daytripbeverages.com/products/prebiotic-soda), [mate, BEV040](https://daytripbeverages.com/products/organic-yerba-mate).
- **Adjacent formats must remain visible.** Seedlip's sampled core bottles and Three Spirit's botanical elixirs are made for serves/mixing, rather than direct formula comparators for a canned tonic. Three Spirit also offers ready-to-pour wine alternatives. Pentire and Botivo now show RTD cans as well as mixing bottles: their canned products are direct RTD precedents, while the bottles remain architecture comparators. [Seedlip, BEV013](https://www.seedlipdrinks.com/en-gb), [Three Spirit, BEV014](https://threespiritdrinks.com/), [Pentire, BEV015](https://pentiredrinks.com/), [Botivo cans, BEV056](https://botivodrinks.com/products/botivo-can).
- **Category words require precision.** Culture Pop is a probiotic soda, whereas OLIPOP and poppi use prebiotic descriptions. Those words are not interchangeable, and their appearance in US branding is not evidence of permitted EU claims. [Culture Pop, BEV003](https://drinkculturepop.com/), [OLIPOP, BEV001](https://drinkolipop.com/), [poppi, BEV002](https://www.drinkpoppi.com/).

## Commercial evidence that can actually be used

OLIPOP's founder reported **USD400–450 million in 2024 revenue**, with ecommerce accounting for roughly 5% of total revenue by the 2025 interview. This is company testimony reported by a trade journalist, not an independent audit. It is particularly useful against equating a brand's digital visibility with a predominantly DTC business today. [Beverage Industry, BEV026](https://www.bevindustry.com/articles/97412-olipop-blends-indulgence-functionality-with-prebiotic-soda-portfolio).

PepsiCo's 2026 management remarks estimate poppi's **2025 retail sales at nearly USD745 million**, while its completed acquisition announcement gives **USD1.95 billion gross consideration** including expected cash-tax benefits. These are different quantities: retail sales are not net revenue, and acquisition price is not sales or retention. The official history records the Mother Beverage-to-poppi rebrand in 2020, but supplies no experiment isolating the new name from investment, product, packaging and distribution. [Management remarks p.5, BEV028](https://investor.pepsico.com/docs/pepsico-5v9wci20/media/Files/investors/q4-2025-prepared-management-remarks.pdf), [acquisition, BEV027](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2025/pepsico-completes-acquisition-of-poppi-accelerating-strategic-portfolio-transformation), [history, BEV029](https://www.drinkpoppi.com/pages/our-story).

TÖST provides an unusually useful issuer filing: **2023 revenue USD6,763,816.23**, versus USD3,206,994.66 in 2022, alongside a **2023 net loss of USD432,228.91**. The evidence supports revenue growth and continuing losses, not profitable success or a naming effect. [Form C-AR, BEV042](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1733215/000185177324000008/tostcar.pdf).

For emerging brands, use narrower claims. Culture Pop's founder estimates over 18,000 stores; hiyo's founder interview summary reports over 500,000 cases annually, without a case-size definition or precise completed year. Recess's company post cites a first-place velocity rank in a narrowly specified SPINS single-can category and time window, with the raw data unavailable. TRIP's November 2025 release reports 10,000 US and 50,000 worldwide doors; its October and November revenue wording is inconsistent, so this dataset does not treat the headline USD100m as clean realized revenue. [Culture Pop, BEV030](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2025/culture-pop-lands-15m-to-close-the-gap-in-modern-soda/), [hiyo, BEV032](https://heritageradionetwork.org/episode/66-inside-hiyos-500k-case-growth-playbook-with-co-founder-george-youmans-business-of-drinks/), [Recess, BEV034](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/drinkrecess_another-milestone-for-recess-we-started-activity-7452420532446081024-GkkM), [TRIP, BEV036](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/trip-the-worlds-leading-calming-drinks-brand-raises-40-million-302609480.html).

Czech examples add useful local evidence without resolving the future strategy. CANS' founder reported **800,000 cans sold in 2024**. Kofola reported **CZK34 million 2024 PRAGER'S portfolio revenue**, up from CZK13 million in 2023, but that portfolio includes alcoholic cider. It cannot be relabelled nonalcoholic-tonic revenue. PRAGER'S unifies the former cider and kombucha identities in 2025; Vilgain's official explanation similarly supports brand consolidation across markets, with no naming-only conversion measurement. [CANS, BEV047](https://cc.cz/milion-prodanych-plechovek-za-rok-a-pul-jaroslav-beck-miri-s-cans-do-sveta-a-chysta-dalsi-napoje/), [PRAGER'S, BEV048](https://company.kofola.cz/aktuality/f-h-prager-se-meni-na-prager-s-a-v-roce-2025-cili-na-60-milionovy-obrat), [Vilgain, BEV049](https://vilgain.cz/aktin-vilgain).

Ugly Drinks prevents a simple equation of playful, unexpected identity with commercial durability. BevNET reported the UK operation ending in 2021 and US shutdown communication in early 2022; its current database marks the brand discontinued. This does **not** show that the name caused closure. It shows that provocative naming and a DTC-to-retail story can coexist with an unsuccessful commercial outcome. One failed comparator is insufficient to estimate whether any name type is more failure-prone. [Closure report, BEV052](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2022/ugly-ending-sparkling-water-brand-goes-dark/), [current database, BEV024](https://www.bevnet.com/brands/ugly-water).

## Documented origins and interpretation boundaries

Reliable accounts support OLIPOP's oligosaccharide/pop blend, hiyo's happy-in-your-own acronym, De Soi's French self-control phrase, Seedlip's agricultural basket, Taika's Finnish magic, and Botivo's botanical/aperitivo blend. These establish intended constructions; they do not establish that unprompted consumers understand them. Sources: [BEV025](https://drinkolipop.com/blogs/digest/why-is-olipop-called-olipop), [BEV031](https://drinkhiyo.com/pages/our-story), [BEV035](https://www.vogue.com/article/katy-perry-de-soi-non-alcoholic-aperitifs-launch), [BEV043](https://www.seedlipdrinks.com/en-gb/our-story), [BEV046](https://www.bristolfarms.com/founders/mtf-michael-sharon-kalle-freese-taika-coffee), [BEV045](https://www.beveragedaily.com/Article/2024/03/15/Non-alcoholic-aperitif-Botivo-reveals-how-to-create-the-ultimate-alcohol-free-cocktails/).

Do not turn plausible stories into evidence: Ghia's etymology, Leilo as “lay low,” Vilgain as “will gain,” PRAGER'S as a particular founder, and TÖST's specific source-language origin were not verified. Pentire's place meaning and coastal positioning are supported separately, but the precise founder naming decision remains an inference. CLUB-MATE's sampled name dates to 1994 even though its product lineage goes back to 1924. [Pentire story, BEV053](https://pentiredrinks.com/pages/our-story), [place history, BEV054](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cornwall/pentire/history-of-pentire), [CLUB-MATE history, BEV050](https://www.club-mate.de/en/historical/).

## Implications for the later Czech/EU exercise

Explore a broad identity plus a clear, localizable category descriptor, but test it against a more explicit alternative. Test the name alone and the same name on equal packaging separately. A digital search can contain a clarifying term; a spoken bar order, creator mention or small shelf-facing label may lose that help. These are task-design implications, not measured conversion findings from the brands above.

Prioritize audio-to-spelling tests for altered spellings, punctuation and foreign words: poppi's final i, TÖST's Ö, PRAGER'S apostrophe and De Soi's French reading illustrate different retrieval issues. Their presence among active brands is neither a universal ban nor proof of harmlessness. Czech and other language-group testing remains required. No native-speaker panel, consumer experiment, systematic search-ranking audit or trademark clearance was conducted.

This workstream provides an auditable descriptive sample. It is weighted toward brands visible in English-language trade reporting and current brand websites; status data have unequal dates and quality. Unknown scale is deliberately not failure. UK examples are labelled UK rather than EU. DTC-origin is asserted only when the source describes channel history; a current subscription button is not enough. No acquisition, funding, celebrity association, review count or media visibility has been treated as direct proof that a name caused sales.
