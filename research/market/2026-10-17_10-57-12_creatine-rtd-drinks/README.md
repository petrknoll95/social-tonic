# Creatine-led ready-to-drink beverages

**Created:** 2026-10-17T10:57:12+02:00 · Europe/Prague (UTC+02:00). **Research cut-off:** 17 October 2026.

Public-source research into finished non-alcoholic creatine-led cans and bottles, prioritising sparkling soda. Adjacent still, energy/recovery, protein/dairy and shot formats are distinguished. Powders, dry-cap activation bottles and other mixing products are substitutes or historical context only.

**Status:** completed desk research with explicit evidence/access gaps. No purchases, outreach, consumer validation, formulation trials or laboratory testing. No launch or formula clearance. This is not an exhaustive global census.

Workstream CSVs and source fragments are retained for provenance; the four final `data/` registers and root `sources.csv` are the integrated outputs. Prices are dated observations, in original currencies; stock, shipping and regulatory status should be rechecked before decisions. A label declaration is not an end-of-shelf-life assay. Source reuse is marked and does not count as independent corroboration.
