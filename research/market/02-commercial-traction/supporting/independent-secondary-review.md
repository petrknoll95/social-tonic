Review date and cutoff: **17 October 2026, Europe/Prague**. Reviewer: focus/energy research agent, reviewing a different agent's work. This review does not certify the reviewer's own focus/energy files.

Inspected [secondary memo](secondary-categories.md), [secondary metrics](../data/secondary-metrics.csv), [secondary sources](secondary-sources.csv), and the corresponding tables, blueprints, counterexamples and reconciliation text in [the integrated report](../report.md). The reviewed dataset includes SCM01–SCM16 and the new Oikos sources SC29–SC33. Decisive sources were independently reopened on the review date.

**Result: the main commercial conclusions and metric distinctions are supported. One narrow timing correction is needed for Unwell. No other material factual correction was identified in the inspected secondary-category sections.** This is a targeted source and interpretation review, not an audit of underlying retailer datasets or financial accounts.

## Correction requested

**Unwell: avoid turning an internally inconsistent report into an established August announcement date.** The [13 October BevNET article](https://www.bevnet.com/magazine/issue/2026/bevscape-the-latest-beverage-brand-news-48/) (SC17) says the venture shut in August, while also saying production and launches would continue through Halloween and operations cease afterward. The memo properly identifies the inconsistency and the integrated report properly treats completion as prospective. However, “treat August as the announcement,” SCM15's “announced August 2026,” and the integrated report's “An August announcement” are an inference presented too definitively.

Recommended replacement for the dataset period: **“Reported 13 October 2026; production expected through Halloween 2026; August timing internally inconsistent in source.”** In the memo/report, describe the disputed August date without asserting what event occurred then. No verified reason for closure or quantified line-level sales should be added. The interpretation of cessation after Halloween as prospective at the cutoff is correct. This correction does not change the cautionary commercial conclusion.

## Access and review limits

The Electrolit rank, Halfday cumulative threshold, Numerator ranks and excluded watchlist were checked for consistency and appropriate caveats in the local deliverables, but were not all independently re-researched in this targeted pass. The no-material-issue conclusion applies to the stated scope, not a claim that every source in the entire project was audited.
