**Reviewer:** CBD/sleep/secondary-claims workstream agent, independently reviewing the botanical/Czech workstream and lead benchmark memo. **Date:** 17 October 2026. This review did not review its own substantive CBD conclusions and is not final legal clearance.

## Findings requiring integration

1. **Clarify Neutonic's exact-SKU/event scope.** The Orange Sunrise US page verifies a 355 ml can, 120 mg caffeine and the rhodiola-containing formulation. The 1 October 2026 Walmart announcement lists four-packs of Blue Raspberry, Tropical Ice, Strawberry Lemonade and Wild Citrus and describes a formula containing alpha-GPC; it does not name Orange Sunrise. The draft already acknowledges the formula mismatch but still places the 2026 event beside the exact Orange SKU. Either use a specifically named new canned four-pack as the recent benchmark (volume unknown if unverified), or identify Orange explicitly as a contextual current messaging example and the 2026 event as line-level, not Orange-specific. Do not infer that Orange's formula is the Walmart assortment. [Exact SKU](https://www.neutonic.com/en-us/products/orange-sunrise-12-pack-us), [dated company announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neutonic-expands-us-retail-footprint-with-launch-in-walmart-stores-302895438.html); both re-accessed 17 October 2026.

2. **Keep the Czech probiotic allowance provisional and geographically limited.** The botanical memo already accurately labels the 2017 national recommendations nonbinding and asks for current practice confirmation. Add or retain the later EU-level contrary context: the Ombudsman's **20 December 2024** decision found the Commission's interpretation of “contains probiotics” as an implied health claim reasonable. Paragraph 30 says divergent national practice would not itself invalidate the Commission interpretation. This is an administrative review, not a CJEU ruling, and does not itself resolve the exact Czech label; it makes a pan-EU safe-harbour characterization particularly inappropriate. [Official decision](https://www.ombudsman.europa.eu/cs/decision/cs/197581), accessed 17 October 2026; the linked Czech page is machine translated, so the English original should govern precise quotations. Source CS16 is included in the CBD-workstream ledger.

3. **Qualify the Unwind magnesium comparison.** The live FAQ states 42 mg magnesium and describes a can, but its maximum-serving sentence mixes “shots cans / servings”. The numerical comparison with the EU 56.25 mg magnesium single-serving source threshold is correct **if 42 mg is the amount per can**. Retain the brand-assertion label and this serving caveat; do not present it as a verified EU-compliant ingredient or formula. [Brand FAQ](https://tryunwindco.com/pages/faq), re-accessed 17 October 2026.

## Material conclusions independently supported

- **The botanical judgment is bounded correctly.** The 30 April 2025 C-386/23 operative ruling addresses specific psychological/behavioural botanical claims and general wellbeing references, subject to Article 28(6). The memo distinguishes this from Article 28(5) and avoids both blanket permission for on-hold entries and a universal botanical ban. [Official operative text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A62023CA0386), rechecked 17 October 2026.
- **“Social tonic” has no blanket exemption.** Regulation 2019/343's Annex restricts the tonic derogation to specified quinine-flavoured carbonated nonalcoholic beverages and listed language forms; “all Member States” is the actual Annex geography. It does not authorize added mood or social-confidence claims. [Official Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32019R0343), indexed official text rechecked 17 October 2026.
- **General wellbeing support must be visible and relevant.** The C-524/18 account correctly describes both substantive connection and visual relationship, with a narrowly conditioned asterisk exception rather than a general back-label cure. [Official case summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/SUM/?uri=CELEX%3A62018CJ0524), rechecked 17 October 2026.
- **The Czech “natural” treatment is not overgeneralized.** Decree 417/2016 §2 expressly names přírodní, then recognizes specified lawful names and demonstrably justified nonmisleading descriptive context. The memo's recommendation for precise ingredient/process wording follows this distinction. [MZe legal text](https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/legislativa/vap87546-131), rechecked 17 October 2026.
- **The proposed labels preserve legal/efficacy separation.** Nutrient normal-function claims are not translated into immediate calm, higher productivity or reliable sleep effects. Brand testimonials are not treated as clinical evidence; commercial re-use is correctly flagged as claims-bearing content. No whole-brand revenue is used to establish a finished-liquid SKU's success.
- **Format discipline is maintained.** All named consumer benchmarks are cans/bottles; excluded products appearing on Neutonic's website are explicitly removed from benchmark evidence. Court/supplement evidence is used as legal authority, not as an excluded-format product recommendation.
