## Outcome and limits

The reviewed report preserves the material distinctions required for commercial diligence: liquid-line versus mixed-format results, retail purchases versus supplier revenue, forecast versus achieved figures, cumulative versus annual volume, and US versus UK/global evidence. No blocking numerical or attribution error was found in the sampled focus/calm/lead claims. Two publication corrections were identified: an available exact SEC filing date and links to a topic not present in this checkout. Their final disposition is for the lead to record before publishing.

This is a sampled evidence review, not a re-performance of every web search, a financial audit, a systematic clinical review, or a legal clearance. It does not certify the private panel data, source-company honesty, every current retailer stock position, or future GitHub publication. Documents behind access restrictions remain qualified rather than silently treated as fully inspected.
