# 12 · Range and format

**Research date/cut-off: 17 October 2026. Status: completed desk research and conditional portfolio recommendation; independent reviews completed with corrections recorded. Customer tests, supplier quotes, regulatory clearance and production remain pending.**

Scope: finished non-alcoholic ready-to-drink liquids in **cans or bottles only**. US 2025–2026 commercial/product benchmarks inform a potential Czech/EU launch. Focus/alertness/energy take priority, with caffeine-free calm/social and sleep assessed separately; hydration, gut-health and recovery are considered only for distinct needs. The primary product is CBD-free; a possible CBD extension receives an independent assessment. THC-led/intoxicating products are outside the intended launch scope.

**Recommendation:** validate one occasion, one core formula, one size and one hero flavour. Compare a daytime platform with a caffeine-free evening alternative. A two-occasion range and a second flavour must earn their extra production and safety requirements; no daily system is presumed.
