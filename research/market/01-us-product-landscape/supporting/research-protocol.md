## Inclusion and sampling rules

- Finished, ready-to-drink **liquids in cans or bottles** only. A drinkable bottled shot qualifies. Exclude powders, sachets, all pouches, tablets, capsules, gummies, gels, drops and consumer concentrates/syrups requiring preparation. Ingredients supplied to a manufacturer may be powders or concentrates. Ingredient studies in other delivery forms are evidence inputs, never product benchmarks.
- Discover independently by occasion: focused work; energy/alertness; relaxation/social; bedtime; hydration/recovery; gut health. Sample is purposive and non-exhaustive, not a category market-share estimate.
- Core recency gate: US product introduction, documented material reformulation, or substantive commercial event in **2025–2026 through the cut-off**. New flavors are range extensions, not brand launches. Older leaders and informative products without a verified recent event are explicitly contextual comparators; discontinued products belong in the negative-evidence/watchlist record.
- Record **original launch**, **recent event**, and **availability** separately. An announcement does not prove execution. An orderable page supports an offer for sale, not completed transactions or store-level stock. Unknown means not established in reviewed sources, not zero or absent.
- Tag caffeine-led, caffeine-free/non-cannabinoid, CBD-containing, and THC-led separately. Caffeine-free need not mean stimulant-free. THC-led products are excluded from launch blueprints.

## Evidence and confidence

For each material claim prefer an exact label/product page, dated official announcement, regulatory act or primary study. Use trade reporting for corroboration and reporting unavailable in primary disclosures. Record geographic scope and access date. A publisher's displayed date overrides a search engine's relative-date metadata. Undated product pages have `publication_date=unknown` and the access date.

Keep distinct: **verified documentary fact** (e.g. a label discloses caffeine); **brand claim** (e.g. no crash); **reported commercial result** (who measured it and at what scope); **analyst calculation**; **hypothesis**. A press release verifies what was announced, not the claimed outcome. Funding, valuation, acquisition price, authorized store doors, shipment projections and retail sell-through are different measures. Mixed-format brand revenue is never assigned to its liquid line.

High confidence means direct exact-product evidence and a clear dated event; medium means primary evidence with chronology, scope or current-stock gaps; low means unresolved or single-source inference. Confidence concerns the cited fact, not efficacy. No inference of category size from brand count. No inference of consumer demand from funding alone. No invented sales, interviews, quotations, supplier prices or tests.

## Prices and calculations

Use USD per **whole can/bottle** as the common consumption unit; identify any label serving mismatch. Divide the quoted pack price by pack count, retaining date, seller and promotion/subscription caveats. Do not impute missing prices or convert US prices into a Czech business case. Taxes, deposits and shipping are excluded unless specifically stated. Prices are snapshots or dated launch SRPs, not a weighted market average. No FX model is required.

## Clinical and regulatory transfer

Compare study extract identity, standardization, dose, delivery, treatment duration, population, comparator and endpoint with the drink. Acute attention, perceived calm, repeated-use stress scores and sleep are different outcomes. Ingredient studies cannot establish efficacy of a multi-ingredient commercial drink. EU-authorized claims and conditions, novel-food permitted use categories, and Czech requirements must be evaluated separately from biological plausibility and US availability.
