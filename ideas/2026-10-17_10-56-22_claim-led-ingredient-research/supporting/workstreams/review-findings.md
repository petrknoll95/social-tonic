Two further wording corrections were sent to the lead before publication:

1. **Medium, CA-ST03 / E01:** “adding theanine removed” caffeine’s significant behavioural benefits could imply demonstrated pharmacological antagonism. The reviewer independently retrieved Dodd 2015 full primary XML [CA03](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4480845/). Results report caffeine-versus-placebo cognitive benefits and no significant corresponding combination-versus-placebo benefits; those contrasts alone do not establish a caffeine-versus-combination difference. Preferred wording: “Caffeine improved choice reaction time/Stroop versus placebo; the combination did not show those significant placebo-controlled benefits; direct antagonism was not established.” The same standard should govern positive synergy and negative interaction claims.
2. **Minor, EN-ST02 / E02:** “Double-blind six-condition crossover in one sample; six visits, not six independent studies” could imply six independent replications. Describe the one sample as a **double-blind six-condition crossover**, with six visits, n=24.
