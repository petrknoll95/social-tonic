## Scope and inclusion rules

Finished non-alcoholic liquids sold in cans/bottles only; RTD bottled shots, canned/bottled coffee and tea qualify after exact-SKU verification. No consumer powders, sachets, pouches, pills, gummies, gels, drops or concentrates requiring preparation. Raw manufacturing ingredients and non-drink clinical interventions are not product benchmarks. They are identified separately where relevant. A bottle of concentrate does not qualify just because it is bottled.

The core commercial window is **1 January 2025–17 October 2026**. Original launch, later material reformulation, achieved traction, distribution announcement and source publication are separate events. Older leaders and failed predecessors are contextual and labeled. A 2025 article about 2024 revenue is not a 2025 result. A 2026 full-year projection is not achieved annual revenue at this cut-off.

Primary product scope is CBD-free. CBD is a separate segment, and THC-led/intoxicating products are excluded as launch benchmarks. Brand-level sales mixing CBD, non-CBD, THC, powders or concentrates are not proof of the qualifying line. Mixed-format figures may appear only as attribution warnings or business-risk context.

## Evidence grading

| Class | Meaning | Practical treatment |
|---|---|---|
| A | Filing/audited financial evidence | Strongest for the stated entity/period; still may have unusable SKU/format scope. Quarterly filings are not automatically audited. |
| B | Retailer/panel consumer purchases | Prefer common channel, period and segment; identify when a company or journalist is the intermediary and raw panel tables are unavailable. |
| C | Independent reporting with a clearly attributed metric | Trace the underlying origin. A founder quote remains a founder claim, not independent sales verification. |
| D | Company/founder claim or promotional release | Useful directional evidence with precise wording; no audit assumed. |
| E | Weak proxy: doors, listings, financing, followers, awards, rank without denominator | Establishes access/activity, not strong sales. |
| U | Unknown or unusable for qualifying line | Do not substitute zero; no ranking as a commercial leader. |

Confidence **high/medium/low** describes support for the exact scoped observation, not a brand's likelihood of success or efficacy. A high-confidence closure announcement and high-confidence profit figure answer different questions. A panel claim quoted in a brand release can be B-origin/D-transmitted and only medium confidence. Register entries retain that chain instead of promoting all PR into independent evidence.

## Normalization and comparisons

- Preserve native currency; no arbitrary FX conversion or cross-currency league table.
- Keep net brand revenue, gross consumer retail value, distributor shipments, units/cans, cumulative cases and forecasts separate. Do not multiply retail price by reported units to invent brand revenue.
- Do not infer cans from cases without a defined case pack. Do not divide annual revenue by the latest store count to manufacture weekly velocity: store cohorts, distribution dates, channel mix, promotions and prices are missing.
- Compare within the same category first. Specialist focus, mainstream energy, CBD-free calm, CBD, sleep, soda and hydration have different maturity, price, usage and distribution. Cross-category assessment is about evidence strength and transferable mechanisms, not a summed sales rank.
- Rank repeated/cohort purchases only when a denominator, cohort entry, time window, channel and paid-order definition are supplied. Subscription defaults, autoship and new distribution can distort apparent repeat.
- Record missing margin, repeat, velocity, profitability, EU-only sales and SKU split as **unknown**. Do not conclude they are zero or that a business is unprofitable.

## Search and source handling

Reuse the repository's source leads, reopen decision-changing evidence, search official investor relations/filings, panel publishers, trade reporting and exact product pages. Search failure/delist/decline/closure terms as well as success terms. Czech-language NIQ reporting and Czech/EU official food sources inform transferability. Syndicated releases count as one origin; independent coverage of the same release does not create a second sales observation. Search snippets and inaccessible originals are flagged; unsupported precise figures are withheld from headline tables.

Demand, sales and effects are independent evidence sets. A survey of desired benefits is not RTD spend. A sales increase does not establish ingredient efficacy. Ingredient studies require matching identity, dose, delivery, processing/stability, population, comparator and duration before transfer to a commercial drink. Favorable scientific safety opinions and application validation are not market authorization or health claims.
