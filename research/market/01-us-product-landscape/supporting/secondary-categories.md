## Most informative secondary benchmarks

1. **OLIPOP 6 g shelf-stable:** high confidence on product architecture; medium on brand-reported commercial results. It shows that distribution requirements can change the formula itself. The 9 g refrigerated and 6 g ambient versions coexist; a study or label for one cannot automatically substantiate the other. The founder attributed the lower-fiber version partly to consumers wanting more frequent consumption in [August 2024 reporting](https://www.carolortenberg.com/p/stepping-off-the-gas-olipops-new). Exact-product clinical effects and gastrointestinal tolerance are not established here.
2. **Gatorade Lower Sugar:** high confidence on launch and current offer, low on new-line sell-through. It is a useful simple hydration comparator for a workday drink: ordinary ingredients, clear lower-intensity occasion, resealable bottle. It does not substantiate added concentration in already hydrated people. Mineral comparisons differ depending on whether label servings or whole bottles are used.
3. **CSE Clear Protein Soda**, with **Beyond Immerse** as its plant-protein comparator: high confidence on disclosed nutrient payload; low on independently measured commercial performance. These disclose measurable protein content. Finished-product stability, mouthfeel, manufacturing costs and tolerability were not independently evaluated; no supplier quotations were obtained.

## Observed patterns

- **Storage format can accompany formulation changes.** OLIPOP's shelf-stable version differs from its refrigerated version. The 2024 introduction predates this report's recent-event window; 2025 funding does not change that chronology.
- **Fiber dose is not interchangeable with a proven health outcome.** Poppi's current 3 g, OLIPOP's 6 g and Simply Pop's 6 g use different blends. Their acquisition/funding and shelf presence do not prove a clinical benefit; US prebiotic positioning does not establish EU claims permission.
- **Hydration positioning extends beyond intense exercise.** Gatorade Lower Sugar explicitly targets lower-intensity and everyday occasions. The reviewed evidence does not establish a general productivity effect in already hydrated people.
- **Protein, fiber and energy propositions are being combined.** The October 2026 Beyond extension discloses all three, but preorder availability provides no realized demand or repeat-purchase evidence.

## Corrections, uncertainty and exclusions

The official Beyond January release has a **2025 typo inside its body**, while its header says 15 January 2026 and its forward-looking discussion references late-2025 filings. This study treats it as a 2026 launch. The energy extension's press release says it is launching online, but the exact SKU page prominently says **preorder**; a generic lower-page Add to Cart and apparently inherited reviews do not establish shipments. Limoncello Ginger and Yuzu Grapefruit are explicitly coming soon and are not counted as sold benchmarks.

Electrolit's press release gives a poorly specified aggregate electrolyte figure; it is not used. The 21 oz bottle must not be assigned a 12 oz label's nutrient amounts. Gatorade's dataset provides transparent 28/12 scaling as an estimate; package rounding prevents treating it as an assay. Price bases are regional offers, not US averages.

All benefit wording in the table is a **brand proposition**, unless stated as a label fact. This workstream did not establish a finished-product clinical trial for these exact current SKUs. Ingredient evidence needs matched identity, quantity, population, outcome and processing stability before transfer to the beverage. Caffeine absence is sometimes based on a non-caffeinated retailer declaration; where only an ingredient list was available, the numerical dose remains unknown.

No confirmed discontinuation was found among these eight exact SKUs. “Limited edition,” an out-of-stock message and a formula replacement are not sufficient proof of discontinuation. Powder hydration products, drink mixes, gummies, concentrates and liquid pouches were excluded at discovery. No contacts, purchases or tests were made.
