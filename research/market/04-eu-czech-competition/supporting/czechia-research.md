# Czechia: verified RTD competition and substitutes

Research/access date: **17 October 2026**. Status: desk-research snapshot, ready for integration; no purchases, interviews or sales-data access. Country scope: Czechia. The [dataset](../data/czechia-competitors.csv) contains 16 exact canned/bottled finished liquids; [source register](czechia-sources.csv) preserves dates, geography and limitations. This extends the repository's [US landscape](../../01-us-product-landscape/report.md) and [EU/Czech evidence memo](../../01-us-product-landscape/supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md). US launch/traction screens belong to the parent report; Czech products here need not have launched in 2025–2026 to be relevant competition.

## Main finding

A Czech focus launch enters an existing set of explicit productivity propositions. **CANS+ Performance, Vilgain Energy & Focus, Brite and Magnesia Plus Focus** compete on that occasion already. Caffeine strength, sweetness, volume, price, purchase channel and credibility are more useful comparison dimensions than a binary “energy versus nootropics” distinction. CANS+ also offers Balance, while Magnesia sells Antistress. Neither daytime focus nor calm can responsibly be described as empty Czech white space. [CANS+](https://cz.cans.com/products/cans-performance), [Vilgain](https://aktin.cz/vilgain-energy-focus-drink-2/12x44884), [Brite local listing](https://www.osatka.cz/e/detail/brite-energeticky-napoj-citron-limetka-330-ml), [Magnesia](https://www.magnesia.cz/magnesia-plus) (undated pages; accessed 2026-10-17; Czech offers).

## Distribution, scale and recognition are different questions

- **Mainstream retailer online:** dm lists exact CANS Performance and Starbucks bottles; Tesco lists the exact bottled Birell. This supports presence in those retailers' Czech online assortments. Store counts, regional shelf availability, facings and velocity remain unknown.
- **Online grocery:** Rohlík displays the selected products in a default Prague 7 catalogue. No delivery address was entered and no transaction tested. Do not turn this into national physical distribution or guaranteed fulfillment.
- **Specialist retail:** Ošatka confirms Brite; Pilulka provides a second CANS Performance listing; Econea confirms Bohemsca; Kvalitní pití confirms the Virgin Mojito. Such listings do not establish supermarket penetration.
- **Domestic direct sales:** CANS, Aktin/Vilgain and CBDUX provide Czech offers. The Bohemsca direct pack is 825/15 = 55 CZK per glass bottle, distinct from Econea's 50 CZK single offer. [Official Bohemsca catalogue](https://obchod.bohemsca.cz/limonady-a-cola/) (undated, accessed 2026-10-17).
- **Cross-border shipping:** a Czech-currency international Brite storefront alone would be weak local distribution evidence; its Ošatka listing is stronger. Cross-border availability must not be counted as a domestic chain listing.
- **Hospitality:** no current exact-SKU venue inventory or sales was established in this focused pass. Distributor logos and portfolio menus cannot fill that gap. Venue-by-venue checks remain necessary.

Business maturity is a qualitative operating-history classification, not a revenue threshold or brand-awareness score. Established beverage businesses underpin Red Bull, Starbucks, Powerade, Magnesia and Birell; Club-Mate has long product history. CANS is an emerging business. PRAGER’S combines an emerging functional line with an established Kofola parent. Business maturity of other selected specialist propositions remains unknown on this source set. **Measured Czech recognition is unknown for every row**: retailer presence and history are visibility proxies, not aided/unaided awareness results.

Two scale signals are useful but cannot answer the exact-SKU demand question. On **4 February 2025**, CzechCrunch reported CANS' own figures of 800,000 cans in 2024 and one million cumulatively; the then-described portfolio was unsweetened sparkling water. These totals do not validate Performance or Balance and include more than Czechia. [CzechCrunch](https://cc.cz/milion-prodanych-plechovek-za-rok-a-pul-jaroslav-beck-miri-s-cans-do-sveta-a-chysta-dalsi-napoje/). On **21 March 2025**, Kofola reported PRAGER’S 2024 revenue of CZK34 million and 2.05 million units across its cider/kombucha business; Czech canned Kombucha Original sales cannot be isolated. Its CZK50–60 million 2025 figure was a **target**. [Kofola release](https://company.kofola.cz/aktuality/f-h-prager-se-meni-na-prager-s-a-v-roce-2025-cili-na-60-milionovy-obrat). No lack-of-demand conclusion follows from missing SKU data.

## CBD: local product presence, unproven commercial and regulatory viability

[CBDUX x Zichovec Drink and Chill Ø](https://cbdux.cz/product/cbdux-x-zichovec-pivo-drink-and-chill-o/) is a concrete 500 ml canned competitor listing: CBDUX displays 100 CZK and 37 in stock, while the [brewery](https://pivovarzichovec.cz/produkt/drink-and-chill-o/) displays 96 CZK and out of stock. The brewery declares under 0.5% ABV and CBD in ingredients. The actual CBD milligrams, THC test result, batch certificate and Czech sell-through remain unknown. Marketing about a fraction of a recommended CBD dose cannot supply the missing milligrams. Listings were accessed 2026-10-17; publication dates unknown.

[Haze CBD iced tea 250 ml](https://www.zlatecbd.cz/cbd-ledovy-caj-250ml/) is an additional **sold-out watchlist**, not verified current availability. A [Cannabis Sativa energy-drink listing](https://www.galaxiekratomu.cz/cannabis-sativa-0-25-l/) was excluded from verified CBD rows because CBD headline language and hemp-flavour ingredient copy do not establish a CBD dose. Hemp branding is not enough. Neither retail presence nor apparent stock is a food authorization, a compliant claim, a safety finding or evidence of repeat demand. Apply the parent report's separate regulatory gate; this research provides no basis to make CBD the launch dependency.

## Product data contradictions and efficacy limits

CANS Performance's brand and dm pages disagree on manufacturing country. Brite's Czech retailer and international brand copy differ on botanical details. Vilgain flavours have materially different formulas; the lemon/lime row must not acquire mango's ginseng. The Le Coq retailer has an unrelated Blue Lagoon subheading, so detailed formula copy is not trusted; the exact Virgin Mojito label/format and distributor range establish eligibility. Powerade locales disagree on offer state; the current Czech page governs its availability classification. These are reasons to inspect labels and batch documents before formula or sourcing decisions, not reasons to silently choose convenient values.

All quantities in the dataset are declared or calculated from label concentrations; none is independently assayed. “Unknown caffeine” often means a quantitative declaration was not located, rather than evidence of caffeine presence. “Unknown CBD” in ordinary products means no quantitative assay/declaration was collected; no CBD ingredient was identified in their selected formulas. Product names, nutrient claims, reviews and retailer text are not clinical evidence. Caffeine/theanine ratios, mineral forms, fermentation, dose and liquid stability matter to transferability; see the existing evidence memo and the parent update. No purpose-built sleep RTD was established in this selected Czech set; this is an incomplete search finding, **not proof of market absence**.

## Opportunity hypotheses and low-cost next work

1. **A better workplace purchase/use experience, conditional on dissatisfaction with current drinks.** CANS Performance already offers a low-caffeine unsweetened proposition and Vilgain offers explicit focus. A new offer needs a specific reason to switch: taste, reliable repeat supply, serving size or a demonstrated workplace buying friction. Interview office buyers and users about their last actual purchase; compare a future can or bottle against these products and bottled coffee/tea. An office channel is a hypothesis, not an identified unmet demand until buyer evidence exists.
2. **An adult evening taste and occasion, conditional on a clear sensory gap.** Balance, Antistress, mocktails, kombucha and non-alcoholic beer already cover overlapping jobs. Test taste expectations and willingness to pay against these exact products before adding expensive actives. Do not interpret botanical names as demonstrated relaxation or promise sleep outcomes.
3. **Clear dose and evidence communication.** Conflicting labels and unquantified botanicals are identifiable information gaps. Test whether transparent, lawful factual formulation information influences selection and repurchase; better information alone is not proven demand. Any caffeine-free or CBD-free promise needs formulation specifications and verification.

Retail/buyer work is required before claims about saturation, launch-country attractiveness or profitable price points are reliable: obtain category and SKU velocity, distribution, promotion depth, repeat purchase and retailer margin expectations; map exact stores and hospitality accounts; compare planned COGS/MOQs against realistic net receipts. Consumer work should test actual occasion substitution and acceptable price with neutral concepts. Supplement routines and made-to-order coffee/tea may inform those interviews, but they remain **habits**, not extra product rows. Outsourced trial production should follow legal/formula review and early demand evidence; all finished test concepts remain cans or bottles.
