# 05 · Adult segments and occasions

**Research date: 17 October 2026 (Europe/Prague)**

**Status: completed desk research and independent review; provisional segments, no customer fieldwork.**

This topic explores adults' work/study focus, afternoon energy, exercise/recovery, everyday wellbeing, private evening/sleep routines and social drinking. It proposes five overlapping behavioural segments, twelve occasions and ten ranked hypotheses. The recommended first **research audience** is adults facing a recurring early-afternoon drink decision before returning to work/study; neither launch market nor formula is selected.

Product benchmarks and proposed consumer formats are finished ready-to-drink liquids in **cans or bottles only**. Home preparation and non-drink actions are interview context, not qualifying products. The primary concept is CBD-free; CBD is assessed separately, with THC-led/intoxicating products outside launch scope. US 2025–2026 commercial activity, customer accounts and scientific evidence are kept separate.
