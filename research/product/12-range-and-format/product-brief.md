# Provisional product brief — one daytime drink

**17 October 2026 · Architecture A recommended for validation · Not an approved formulation or manufacturing specification.**

**Decision:** develop and validate one CBD-free core formula, one container size and one lead flavour. Keep a second flavour as a conditional extension of that core. Test two flavour candidates before selecting the first; do not produce both merely to offer choice. If a clear paid switching reason does not emerge, stop or choose the separately tested evening architecture described in the [report](report.md).

**Status key:** **[T]** customer/sensory testing needed; **[Q]** supplier/process/cost quote needed; **[R]** regulatory/claims/classification review needed; **[S]** safety, composition or stability evidence needed. Multiple tags mean all gates apply. These are development targets, not facts about an existing product.

| Specification | Provisional target and reason | Unresolved dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Primary customer | Adults already buying or considering a packaged drink during work/study, who want enjoyable refreshment with a clearly disclosed amount of caffeine; include people satisfied with current options in research | **[T]** size, frequency, need, willingness to switch and pay unknown; not a demographic market estimate |
| Primary occasion | A morning or early-day task break; compare the early-afternoon occasion with actual caffeine habits and bedtime. Not a universal recommended drinking time | **[T, S]** timing, tolerance and cumulative exposure; late-day and caffeine-avoiding customers do not inherit this proposition |
| Geographic learning market | Czechia because the founder is based there; test local access and economics before committing. Export country undecided | **[T, Q, R]** local operating advantage is a hypothesis, distribution uncontracted |
| Consumer format | Finished liquid in **250 ml aluminum can**, single serving; compare **330 ml can** as the only size challenger. Select one | **[T, Q, S, R]** perceived value, line access, coating/migration, process and shelf-life compatibility; 250 ml is a hypothesis, not proven optimal |
| Drink identity | Familiar citrus-led sparkling refreshment, possibly with a recognisable tea base if it improves preference and process feasibility | **[T, Q, S, R]** taste and permitted source; tea is not automatically simpler and any tea naming/label exception must actually apply |
| Flavour candidates | Lemon/lime first candidate; peach/citrus challenger. Same intended functional ingredients and dose, sweetness system and process. Flavour-specific acidity/pigments/aromas still require validation | **[T, Q, S]** blind preference and cost; no claim that either is a Czech favourite |
| Range | One hero SKU at first release. Add second same-core flavour only if it recruits/repeats beyond the hero and its own MOQ clears within validated saleable life | **[T, Q, S]** SKU-specific demand, artwork, release testing and run economics |
| Functional direction | Caffeine-led alertness rationale. For concept/bench comparison only, consider **40–60 mg total caffeine per container** alongside a separate zero-caffeine concept. No final dose selected. In 250 ml this is 160–240 mg/l, above the covered-beverage warning threshold | **[T, R, S]** legal identity/source, natural caffeine contribution, assay tolerance, maximum foreseeable intake, label and benefit wording. No nootropic stack is required |
| CBD/cannabinoids | **CBD-free primary product; no THC-led or intoxicating ingredients.** CBD is an independent, deferred research gate | Scope decision; **[Q, R, S]** supplier specs and finished-product verification where contamination risk is relevant |
| Sweetness and calories | Compare a dry/very lightly sweet profile with a lightly sweet profile. Initial target **0–2.5 g total sugars/100 ml**, approximately **0–6.25 g per 250 ml**; target **5–25 kcal per 250 ml** as a sensory/nutrition development window | **[T, Q, R, S]** full recipe energy and sugars; maximum sugar and maximum calorie targets may not both be achievable once other energy-bearing ingredients are added. Zero-sugar route is a test variant, not a second launch SKU |
| Sweetener choice | No predetermined ingredient ideology: compare no intense sweetener against a legally permitted sweetener route if needed; reject lingering aftertaste | **[T, Q, R]** dose/category permissions, consumer preference, ingredient statement and required sweetener wording |
| Carbonation | Light sparkle as lead; still version as a bench control only. Desired experience: refreshing, comfortable to finish, low foam and no harsh bite | **[T, Q, S]** CO 2 specification and retention set by sensory trials and filler capability; no numeric gas target approved |
| Sensory quality | Clear recognisable flavour, clean finish, limited caffeine/mineral bitterness, no sediment/ring or objectionable aroma drift; enjoyable cold and acceptable as it warms | **[T, Q, S]** trained/consumer evaluation and aged samples; “clear” is a visual target, not a cognition claim |
| Claims and name | Internal work-break/alertness concept only; transparent factual ingredient/portion information. Evaluate any qualifying authorized nutrient claim separately, without implying extra performance in nutrient-replete adults | **[R, S]** even “focus,” “clarity,” “calm” or an implied benefit in a product name requires review; no no-crash, productivity, anxiety or sleep-treatment promise approved |
| Price hypothesis | Test **CZK 39 / 45 / 49 / 55 incl VAT per 250 ml can**, with **49** as a provisional shelf-price anchor to challenge. Consider **CZK 43 per can in a 12-pack (516 goods total)** only if costs support it | **[T, Q, R]** 250 ml at 49 is a premium versus observed 330 ml controls, not validated willingness to pay. Shipping, deposit and final tax treatment stated separately where applicable |
| Channel and packs | Paid single cans through a small reachable local channel; 4/6-can trial collected locally or delivery charged transparently; 12-can replenishment once wanted. Use existing cans to assemble packs; no new formula or size | **[T, Q]** margin, picking, packaging protection and channel terms. No free-delivery promise assumed |
| Subscription | Defer launch subscription. After repeat evidence, test optional 12/24-can replenishment, skip/pause and customer-chosen cadence | **[T, Q, R]** positive contribution after discount/delivery and absence of stockpiling; subscription does not imply recommended daily use |
| Manufacturing | Outsourced, preferably an existing can/process platform with simple decoration; do not purchase plant or custom tooling for this hypothesis | **[Q, S, R]** written per-SKU minimum, yield, lead time, quality ownership, release tests, migration and validated preservation |
| Storage/shelf life | Target ambient distribution if validated; planning aspiration **at least 9 months unopened**, not a promised expiry. No launch until required remaining shelf life is achieved | **[Q, S]** process authority, challenge/microbiological and real-time stability work, potency, sensory, package integrity; 26-week model window is separate from shelf life |
| Repeat and expansion | Customers repurchase for the same occasion at viable undiscounted economics. Add flavour before benefit only when evidence favours it; either can lose to staying with one SKU | **[T, Q]** actual cohort and SKU-level sell-through; do not set a daily consumption quota to improve retention |

**Release gates:** exact ingredient/claim approval for the chosen country and food category; signed feasible production specification and quote; acceptable taste and dose uniformity through the intended shelf life; readable caffeine/serving differentiation; positive credible channel contribution; sufficient demonstrated orders or sell-through to justify each run. No gate has been represented as passed. See the [validation protocol](supporting/validation-and-rfq-protocol.md) and [safety assessment](supporting/safety-and-regulatory-gates.md).
