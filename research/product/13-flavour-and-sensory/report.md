# Flavour and sensory research for a premium functional RTD

**17 October 2026 · Czech/EU customer decision, US 2025–2026 benchmarks · Desk research and proposed experiments.** No products were tasted, no customer results collected, and no suppliers contacted. No launch category, market, formula or budget is selected. All consumer products and proposed concepts are finished liquids in cans or bottles; raw formulation ingredients and scientific vehicles are separately identified.

## Recommendation: test two workday directions first

Begin with **citrus refreshment** and **peach/tea**, comparing them with a local focus can and a still bottled tea. Test enjoyment before brand/function information, then a real full serving and a second serving on a permitted schedule. This is a low-cost learning priority, not a prediction that these flavours will win. Keep a CBD-free, caffeine-free evening hypothesis separate; do not force an aperitif identity or carbonation onto productivity, hydration or bedtime drinks.

The key gap is not a shortage of flavour ideas. It is the absence of evidence showing which sensory balance a reachable Czech customer segment would choose repeatedly at a viable price. Existing [local competition](../../market/04-eu-czech-competition/report.md) already includes explicit focus and calm drinks. Existing [customer research](../../customers/05-segments-and-occasions/report.md) records useful taste objections, but these do not establish prevalence. The [commercial report](../../market/02-commercial-traction/report.md) distinguishes liquid-line sales from company revenue and shows why a successful US energy line is a commercial reference, not proof of Czech flavour preference or ingredient efficacy.

A concurrently published [range/format study](../12-range-and-format/report.md) was reviewed during final integration. Its one-lead-flavour approach is compatible with testing two directions here; its 250/330 ml, light-sparkle and dose targets remain provisional hypotheses, not sensory constraints. This study keeps a still-tea challenger and broader flavour exploration open. Its [material-specific regulatory memo](../12-range-and-format/supporting/safety-and-regulatory-gates.md) should inform the exact-material clearance before fieldwork.

Use the [six briefs](flavour-briefs.md), [sensory vocabulary](sensory-vocabulary.md), [blind/full-serving protocol](supporting/tasting-protocol.md), [Czech/English scorecard](supporting/tasting-scorecard.md), [benchmark dataset](data/us-sensory-benchmarks.csv) and [ingredient register](data/ingredient-risk-register.csv). Source dates, geography, claims and limitations are in [sources.csv](sources.csv).

## What is published, and what remains a hypothesis?

| Evidence class | Supported conclusion | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| Published sensory research | A 2024 Italian/US experiment found different sour-liking response groups; a mean can hide useful segments. [CZ03, primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11014420/) | Czech segment shares, ideal acid level, or a sour-cherry winner |
| Local exploratory evidence | A Czech 2021 thesis's 14 sensory assessors preferred sucrose on average over tested alternatives. [CZ01](https://theses.cz/id/2qezrj/) | A national rejection of stevia, or any conclusion about a specific low-sugar RTD |
| Adjacent EU experiment | In 60 Warsaw-area adults, sugar and steviol-glycoside versions did not differ significantly in overall acceptance within high-juice fruit/herbal matrices. [CZ02, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7762522/) | Interchangeability of sweeteners across matrices; the formulation and panel differ materially |
| Current manufacturer/retailer labels | Local offers span unsweetened sparkling focus, sweetened focus and still tea, with relevant version conflicts | Actual liking, taste intensity, clinical effects or sell-through |
| US launch/commercial records | Specific cans/bottles provide current fruit, tea, floral, mineral and fibre architecture contrasts | Why a flavour sold, whether Czech customers recognize it, or whether an active works |
| Formulation and occasion hypotheses | Shorter finish may help desk sipping; restrained floral notes may suit an evening; still small portions may fit bedtime | These are research questions until real, controlled tasting and repeat-use observations exist |

The apparent stevia disagreement is useful: small local solution tests and a Polish high-juice study test different materials, matrices and participants. Do not choose whichever confirms a preference. Exact sweetener composition and aftertaste must be tested in the finished drink. Similarly, “familiar in local products” and “preferred by local customers” are different propositions. The [local evidence memo](supporting/czech-eu-sensory-evidence.md) covers all seven dimensions—sweetness, acidity, bitterness, aroma, body, carbonation and aftertaste—and the limits of each source.

## Occasion-specific taste expectations to investigate

This table is **proposed positioning/sensory logic**, not measured Czech preferences. Recruit by actual behaviour; do not assign tastes by nationality, gender or occupation.

| Job | Plausible sensory expectation to test | Main risk/contrast | Relevant setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workday focus/energy | Recognizable fruit or tea; manageable sweetness; short finish; easy sipping as it warms | Candy-like intensity versus too little flavour; caffeine bitterness; tea drying; distracting aroma; still versus light bubbles | Desk break and ordinary lunch; local focus can and still packaged tea controls |
| Hydration | Easy drinking, light body, refreshing acid/sweet balance; mineral cue may be acceptable in context | Salt/potassium bitterness, chalky or heavy texture; large bottle fatigue; water may satisfy the job | Separate exercise/heat occasion, with still bottled water and electrolyte bottle |
| Social relaxation | Adult fruit or botanical interest; enough flavour with food; repeatable finish | Floral perfume, lingering bitter peel, syrupiness; an aperitif may be a niche rather than the default | Caffeine-free social flight, alone and with a standardized savoury pairing |
| Bedtime/wind-down | Gentle aroma, restrained acid, comfortable volume and texture | Strong sourness, bubbles, herbal bitterness or sweetness becoming tiring; no drink may be preferable | Separate caffeine-free evening use; no sleep-effect inference |
| Gut/soda or recovery adjacent | Enjoyment and tolerance must justify added fibre/protein and texture | Coating, fullness, sediment, gastrointestinal tolerance; more ingredients/process cost | Only after a distinct need emerges; not part of the first focus flight |

Carbonation is a variable, not a brand requirement. Temperature also affects carbonation sensation in controlled model solutions [ME04, 1992](https://doi.org/10.1093/chemse/17.4.435), so compare fresh pours at matched temperatures. A drink that scores well in a cold sip may behave differently after a normal serving; that remains to be observed.

## Functional ingredients: specify the material before predicting taste

The [13-row risk register](data/ingredient-risk-register.csv) distinguishes measured material effects, supplier assertions and unknown finished-drink outcomes. The [technical memo](supporting/ingredient-sensory-and-cbd.md) supplies sources and required specifications.

- **Caffeine, tea and theanine:** caffeine bitterness and individual tea-catechin bitter/astringent effects have human sensory evidence. Theanine's model-solution interactions do not establish bitterness masking at the intended RTD dose. Quantify caffeine, polyphenols, extraction, carriers and pH effects; test base versus actual proposed material. [Caffeine study, 2008](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950329308000207), [catechin study, 2010](https://academic.oup.com/ijfst/article/45/8/1579/7865323), [theanine study, 2010](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/fstr/16/5/16_5_487/_article/-char/en/).
- **Magnesium and electrolytes:** chloride/sulfate evidence cannot be generalized to citrate, lactate or glycinate. Exact hydrate and elemental assay change required ingredient mass. Supplier neutral-taste claims for named lactate/citrate materials remain unverified in the product. Record sodium/potassium ions, counterions and background water; mineral flavour may be acceptable in one context and unwanted in another. [Salt study, 2003](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950329302001283), [named lactate specification](https://www.jungbunzlauer.com/ingredients/magnesium-lactate/), [named citrate specifications](https://www.jungbunzlauer.com/ingredients/trimagnesium-citrate/).
- **Botanicals/mushrooms:** earthy, rooty, bitter, drying and sediment descriptors are screening questions, not measured flaws of every extract. KSM-66's manufacturer even claims neutral taste. Exact plant/part/extraction/carrier, safety and lawful beverage use precede any consumer ingestion. These materials are not needed in the first pilot. [KSM-66 manufacturer](https://ksm66ashwagandhaa.com/why-choose-ksm66.php), [BfR, September 2024](https://www.bfr.bund.de/cm/349/ashwagandha-food-supplements-with-potential-health-risks.pdf).
- **Sweeteners/fibre:** rebaudioside composition matters; reduced bitterness may coexist with lingering sweetness. Fibre chemistry and dose determine what must be investigated; no unidentified “prebiotic blend” gets a blanket sensory-neutrality or tolerability assumption. [Glycoside experiment, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7466183/).

Keep liking, efficacy and marketability separate. A technically attractive formula may lack a lawful ingredient/claim route; a scientifically plausible ingredient may spoil taste or have no demonstrated effect at the available drink dose. Process and pack trials must include analytical stability, aroma/colour drift, precipitation and whole-serving sensory rechecks. If a gentle-acid bedtime brief leads to a higher-pH formula, the process and shelf stability need separate validation. Perceived sourness and pH are not interchangeable.

## CBD extension: separate, currently gated

No Czech CBD-specific paid-demand case or authorized route for the intended beverage was established. The existing [CBD commercial/transfer assessment](../../market/02-commercial-traction/supporting/eu-czech-transfer.md) keeps UK and US signals separate from EU permission. A sublingual 150 mg insomnia pilot cannot validate a low-dose canned drink [IN15, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11063694/).

EFSA's February 2026 provisional level concerns qualifying high-purity **supplements without nanoparticles**; the September 2026 opinion concerns a specific synthetic CBD in MCT-oil supplement. Neither clears an aqueous RTD or establishes a benefit claim. Czech enforcement records also prevent treating listings as permission. [EFSA February](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/provisional-safe-level-cannabidiol-novel-food), [EFSA September](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154), [SZPI May 2025](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/soubor/27-2025-redigovano.aspx).

The technical memo separately covers supplier traceability, exact isolate/emulsion composition, contaminants, dose uniformity, cannabinoid profile through shelf life, safety, authorization and viable claims. **Recommendation: concept research only for CBD now; no CBD in the first tasting.** The main brand and six directions work independently of CBD; no THC-led/intoxicating extension is proposed.

## The first executable study and decision sequence

1. **Comprehension and need screen:** 8–12 adult interviews to check Czech vocabulary, recent workday occasions and actual dissatisfaction. No national share estimate. Ask what they currently choose, including water/no purchase.
2. **Outsourced bench development:** F1 citrus and F2 peach/tea; characterize bases, then exact proposed active materials. Use finished bottled/canned samples and an actual process plan. Do not select a flavour on inactive base alone.
3. **Blind pilot:** proposed 48 adults, four products, balanced serving order. Pass A collects liking and preference before a separate diagnostic pass. Identical cup/fill/temperature and opening-time controls; no brand/claim/price cues. Score sweet, sour, bitter, aroma, body, bubbles and timed aftertaste; optional minerals/drying as relevant. Formula differences are not single-ingredient experiments.
4. **Matched refinements:** vary sweetness/acidity/carbonation or an exact active material systematically in one base. Do not infer causality from unrelated commercial products.
5. **Real portion and repeat:** proposed 24–30-person crossover with two finalists and control; measure first-sip, halfway and stopping/end enjoyment, actual consumption and reasons for leftovers. Test an actual second serving on another matched day by default; label/total-exposure rules govern any same-day option. No forced completion.
6. **Small range gate:** aim for one good workday SKU initially; add a second only if it reaches otherwise underserved users or a clearly different validated occasion, with feasible shared materials and outsourced production. The gate is contingent and reversible; no budget/market/category selection is implied.

All counts and gates are planning assumptions. The [protocol](supporting/tasting-protocol.md) provides paired analysis, uncertainty intervals, missing-data handling and provisional decision thresholds. A first sip, hypothetical second-can intention and free repeat cannot substitute for full-serving enjoyment or paid repeat. Work, exercise, meals, social use and bedtime have separate modules; none is a clinical trial.

## Remaining unknowns and next actions

Most important unknowns are actual customer preference distributions, portion/second-serving tolerance, bitterness and sweetener persistence at the chosen doses, processing/storage changes, truthful concept value and unit economics. Brand websites cannot resolve these. Low-cost learning means testing fewer, better-controlled propositions before buying a broad import set or commissioning six formulas.

The immediate next step is to select the precise recruited workday occasion, obtain current lot-label documentation, and brief an outsourced formulator on F1/F2 plus the safety/processing constraints. Contacts, quotations, purchases and consumer testing are **not performed** by this report. After a positive sensory result, investigate price and actual paid repeat separately.
