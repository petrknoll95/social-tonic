# 26 · Trends and industry intelligence

**Research date:** 17 October 2026, Europe/Prague.
**Status:** Completed targeted research and independent claim review; customer validation, formulation and legal clearance remain open.
**Audience:** Czech founder exploring an affordable outsourced EU non-alcoholic functional-drink launch.

The review prioritizes **19 July–17 October 2026**, uses the preceding 12 months for momentum, and compares developments across 2025–2026. Publication, event and original launch dates are kept separate. Only finished ready-to-drink cans and bottles qualify, including coffee, tea and bottled shots. Primary concepts remain CBD-free; CBD is a conditional variant. THC-led products are not launch blueprints.

The main recommendation is to compare **work-break refreshment, caffeine-free evening/social participation, and familiar refreshment with a simple nutritional benefit** before selecting a formula. Recent retail entry validates retailer interest; it does not establish Czech willingness to pay, repeat purchase or finished-product effects.
