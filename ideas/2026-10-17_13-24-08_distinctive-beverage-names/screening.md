# Initial screen — distinctive beverage names

**17 October 2026 · Europe/Prague.** Public-web searches, not trademark clearance. All twenty final names were searched as quoted strings with drink/beverage terms; selected candidates received broader brand/soda searches. Search results and excerpts were inspected. No native-speaker tests, complete phonetic searches, official multi-market trademark examination or handle checks were performed.

No exact packaged-beverage brand was clearly established for a final candidate in the inspected results. That statement is limited to this search; it is not evidence of worldwide uniqueness. The findings below include substantial non-beverage uses and possible confusion. They are retained so the creative recommendations are reviewable.

## Removed during this round

- **Brizzo:** a [Brizzo Cola trademark discovery record](https://www.indiafilings.com/search/brizzo-cola-tm-4838896) and a spiked-seltzer reference surfaced. Removed for direct beverage overlap.
- **Feral Fizz:** an existing [Citizen Cider product](https://untappd.com/b/citizen-cider-citizen-cellar-feral-fizz/1928439). Removed.
- **Peel Club:** an existing [fictional soda-brand design project](https://zacharycohn.work/work/branding-visuals/peel-club/) uses the same name and citrus/social territory. This is a concept, not proof of a trading drink company; still removed to avoid repeating an existing creative identity.
- **Zestrel:** broader searching surfaced an artwork and medication-name references in a [health-price database](https://www.haiweb.org/MedPriceDatabase/brand_product.php?SRV_ID=486). Removed for the medical association; no clinical claim or current drug-market conclusion is made.
- **Plinka:** potential confusion with pálinka, plus existing word and business usage. Removed for alcohol/language confusion.
- **Tazlo:** too close to Tazo for a potential tea drink; removed on creative similarity grounds.
- **Sippra:** an existing [wine/coffee tasting app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.sippra.tastingnotes) and a Swedish dictionary word. Removed as an unnecessary adjacent collision.
- **Zavro:** [Zavro Flame](https://www.talabat.com/uae/restaurant/800088/zavro-flame-zakher?aid=6849) food-and-drink service surfaced. Removed from this early list.
- **Fruit Thief:** beverage-related references and an unverified product-information page surfaced. Parked rather than presented as an original drink identity.
- **Quenlo, Quivra, Sprindle, Rindly:** not advanced after the creative review; existing usage and/or weaker distinction from the retained candidates. No legal rejection is implied.

No established brand's spelling, characteristic suffix, mascot, packaging or claimed product effect was reused as a template. The research references are mechanisms and comparison points, not evidence that a new candidate will perform similarly.
