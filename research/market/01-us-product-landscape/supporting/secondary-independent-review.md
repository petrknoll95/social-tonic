Review date: **2026-10-17**. Reviewer: the relaxation/sleep/CBD workstream agent, independently reviewing the other agent's [secondary dataset](../data/secondary-categories.csv), [analysis](secondary-categories.md) and [source register](secondary-categories-sources.csv). ## Findings requiring integration

1. **SEC01: remove the unverified exact-flavor first-sale date.** The dataset says Strawberry Lemon was available by 2020. The cited [CAVU case study](https://www.cavuconsumer.com/case-study/poppi-1/) supports a planned March 2020 brand launch but does not date this flavor. The source register itself correctly records that limitation. Use “brand launch planned March 2020; exact flavor first-sale date unknown” unless a separate dated flavor source is added. This does not affect the 2025 acquisition event.
2. **SEC06: avoid presenting the $30.09 promotion as a reproducible current price.** The [official CSE page](https://cleansimpleeats.com/collections/drinks/products/frosted-lemonade-clear-protein-soda?variant=42270355423310) reopened during this review listed a 16 oz twelve-pack at $42.99 and a separate 12 oz twelve-pack at $35.99. The $30.09 figure was absent from the retrieved page. The page also displays inconsistent per-serving price widgets, so calculate from case price/count. Keep the defensible regular benchmark $42.99/12 = **$3.5825 per 16 oz can**; describe any earlier promotion only as an observation that could not be reproduced, or omit it. Preserve the 16 oz label's 20 g protein and 90 kcal; the current 12 oz page sections show a different 10 g / 50 kcal formula.
3. **SEC02: caffeine classification needs a direct supporting statement if reported as numerical zero.** The independently retrieved [Target 6 g shelf-stable listing](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-91859620) confirmed the product/label and price, but the retrieved text did not expose the claimed “non-caffeinated” classification. If the original reviewer cannot recover that statement, use “unknown; no caffeine ingredient listed.” This is a limited verification gap, not evidence that the drink contains caffeine.

## Additional UPDATE variety-pack check

The [Walmart UPDATE variety-pack listing](https://www.walmart.com/ip/17820764826), reopened on the review date, identifies an actual twelve-pack of 12 fl oz cans, a $21.98 one-time price, caffeine-free positioning and 300 mg paraxanthine. It names Berry, Grape, Peach, Mandarin and Pineapple and says they share the functional formula. **The pack is a qualifying, identifiable retail SKU**, but it should be counted as one benchmark row, not five independent products or evidence of all flavors' sales. The row's normalized **$1.83/can** is correct after rounding ($21.98/12 = $1.8317). If individual flavor labels or flavor-specific sweeteners become important, obtain those labels separately; the shared pack listing does not resolve every formulation detail. Caffeine-free remains compatible with a stimulant proposition and is not a safety conclusion.

Review disposition: **usable after the small date/price/evidence-language corrections above**. No material error was found in the sold-versus-preorder separation or the distinction between company transactions and finished-liquid sales.
