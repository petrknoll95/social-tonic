# Czech/EU transfer, demand and efficacy

**As of 17 October 2026.** These checks supplement the [existing scientific and legal assessment](../../01-us-product-landscape/supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md). They do not clear a formula or establish local product-market fit. Source IDs below resolve in the topic's [source register](../sources.csv).

## Czech commercial context: a meaningful denominator correction

NIQ data reported by Zboží & Prodej on **2 March 2026** put the **combined** Czech energy/vitamin-water/sports-isotonic grouping at **CZK7.127 billion retail sales**, +21.2%, for the 52 weeks ending **30 November 2025**, including petrol stations. Energy drinks account for 82% of that grouping; vitamin waters 16%; sports/isotonic 2%. The energy-drink segment itself grew **15.6% in value and 13% in volume**. Thus CZK7.127bn must not be labeled focus-drink sales or pure energy-drink sales. The same report puts energy's promotional-sales share at **70.2%**. This suggests a demanding price environment; it does not set the right price for a specialist can. [L01, NIQ via Czech trade reporting](https://www.zboziaprodej.cz/2026/03/02/zboziprodej-1-2-2026-energeticky-apetit-trva/).

This is newer and better scoped than the prior topics' >CZK6bn headline for the year ending December 2024. Do not splice them into a growth series without reconciling category definitions. No Czech specialist-focus, CBD-free social-tonic or CBD-only beverage revenue/retention series was established. Evidence of a large energy occasion is not evidence of a premium nootropic opportunity.

The published [EU/Czech competition research](../../04-eu-czech-competition/README.md) supplies exact local offers and price comparisons. The [adult-segment research](../../../customers/05-segments-and-occasions/README.md) supplies provisional occasions and interview instruments, not validated local demand. Existing offers and retail listings are competitive context, not sell-through proof. Czechia is a practical low-cost learning market because the founder is based there; no evidence here ranks it as the best launch market.

## Demand and scientific effects are separate from sales

The US CBD willingness-to-pay study published **28 November 2024** surveyed **2,010 adults**: **58% would or might try** CBD beverages. It is hypothetical interest, combining firm and tentative responses; the conditional willingness-to-pay sample excludes unwilling respondents. It does not estimate purchases, retention or Czech demand. THC results from the same paper are outside the intended product scope and are not used to justify CBD. [L02, Staples, Journal of Wine Economics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-wine-economics/article/canning-cannabis-consumer-preferences-for-cbd-and-thcinfused-beverages/4343A6856F99D7C186836D32ADBD810A).

Caffeine has an established alertness rationale, with dose, timing, other sources and tolerance affecting suitability. EFSA's adult safety reference levels are not recommended formula targets or permission for a particular advertising claim. [L03, EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/caffeine). A successful energy can cannot establish an added cognitive benefit from its botanical ingredients. The [prior evidence memo](../../01-us-product-landscape/supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md) distinguishes selected task results, chronic supplement studies and exact finished-drink outcomes.

For CBD, a **30-person Australian insomnia pilot**, published **1 May 2024**, tested **150 mg sublingually nightly for two weeks**. Most sleep outcomes did not differ from placebo, although some secondary measures improved. That intervention is not a low-dose canned drink: dose, delivery, clinical population and duration differ. It cannot support reliable immediate calm or sleep from a social can. [L04, Narayan et al., primary trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38174873/). Current recheck accessed the indexed PubMed abstract; the direct PMC page presented a browser challenge.

For any proposed drink, assess ingredient identity/standardization, per-container dose, absorption, processing and end-of-shelf-life stability, study population and comparator, and acute versus repeated use. A formulation can sell for taste, branding or ritual even where the promised physiological effect remains unproven.

## CBD update: scientific progress does not clear an RTD

**New evidence missing from the earlier landscape:** EFSA published a favorable application-specific opinion on **18 September 2026** (amended 21 September). It concerns **≥98% synthetic CBD exclusively dissolved in MCT oil for food supplements, maximum 2 mg/day**, for adults **over 25**, excluding pregnancy/lactation; safety in people taking medicines was not established. This is genuine progress for that specified ingredient/use, not beverage authorization or evidence of efficacy. It does not cover an aqueous CBD emulsion in a social drink. [L05, EFSA Journal 24(9), e10154](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154).

EFSA's earlier **9 February 2026** statement gave a provisional **0.0275 mg/kg/day** level, approximately 2 mg for a 70 kg adult, only for qualifying ≥98% CBD supplements without nanoparticles and subject to production/genotoxicity conditions. It was not a general beverage limit. The later opinion has its own conditions and assessment; the two must not be blended into a universal safe per-can dose. [L06, EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/provisional-safe-level-cannabidiol-novel-food).

Commission status decisions identify CBD isolates/extracts as novel foods. The Union list is the reference for authorized identity, specifications, food categories and conditions. This review **did not establish an authorization applicable to the intended canned/bottled CBD beverage**; a favorable EFSA opinion alone is not the Commission authorization. [L07, status decisions](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food/consultation-process-novel-food-status_en), [L08, Union list](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food/authorisations/union-list-novel-foods_en). This is a documented unresolved route, not a claim that every CBD application failed.

Czech SZPI's **29 May 2025** disclosure confirms CBD-food sanctions, recalls/withdrawals and marketing prohibitions since 2021. Its statement about no current proceedings was specific to that response date and was not permission to market a drink. [L09, Czech official disclosure](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/soubor/27-2025-redigovano.aspx). UK CBD sales, US availability, hemp cultivation thresholds and Czech rules for other cannabis activities do not settle food-market authorization.

## Sourcing and viable communication

A regional supplier's claim of a validated application is only an assessment milestone. CBDepot's **24 February 2022** announcement is a historical sourcing lead, not a verified current food-use authorization, production-location audit, quotation or beverage qualification. [L10, supplier news archive](https://www.cbdepot.eu/pages/blog).

If the legal route later becomes viable, require exact authorized ingredient/use documentation, batch identity and independent cannabinoid/THC testing with suitable detection limits, contaminant and solvent limits, food-grade traceability, emulsion compatibility, dose uniformity, and finished-drink potency/stability. All remain unverified here. An isolate label is not a measured finished-can cannabinoid profile. Raw ingredients may be supplied as powders/concentrates, while the consumer product remains a finished liquid in a can/bottle.

No applicable authorized CBD calm/focus/sleep claim was established. Ingredient-use legality and advertising permissions are separate. Review the exact claim and conditions in the [EU health-claims register, L11](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims/eu-register-health-claims_en). For a CBD-free product, qualifying nutrient normal-function claims may be an avenue, but they do not establish acute cognitive enhancement, anxiety treatment or a social intoxication effect. A supplier's scientific dossier does not automatically produce a permitted consumer claim.

**Commercial implication:** assess CBD-specific preference and repeat independently of generic relaxation. Until a beverage-specific route, usable supply specification, safety case and viable wording are established, do not base the brand, economics or main launch on CBD. The primary CBD-free business must stand on its own. No THC-led extension is proposed.
