## E01 — L-theanine; pure L-enantiomer, route specified

### A. Identity and provenance

L-theanine, pure L-enantiomer. Tea extraction, enzymatic synthesis, chemical synthesis and fermentation are distinct production routes. The Hidese trial used enzymatically synthesised Suntheanine; a tea image does not establish tea extraction. Match identity, purity, enantiomer profile and residual caffeine. Verified pure materials may share pharmacology more plausibly than differing plant extracts, but process-specific food status still differs. [CA06](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6836118/); [EU08](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/1e468cb7-af61-4aa2-8d4f-976f9783dbae_en?filename=novel-food_consult-status_l-teanina.pdf); [EU09](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/a498e343-3403-427d-a95d-6a8fa4226c3b_en?filename=novel-food_sum_ongoing-app_2024-15277.pdf)

### B. Perception and commercial use

Calm focus, tea-associated naturalness and mitigation of caffeine jitters are marketed associations. Current CANS+ Balance discloses about 203 mg per 330 mL by label arithmetic, yet has contradictory caffeine statements; Recess lists theanine without a confirmed amount. Exact brand wording is retained in the benchmark CSV. Recognition/purchase influence in Czech consumers is unknown; plant-origin appeal cannot establish efficacy. [CP01](https://cz.cans.com/products/cans-balance); [CP10](https://takearecess.com/shop/recess-mood/strawberry-rose-mood); [CP20](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15458801/)

### C. Controlled human evidence

Attention and stress must remain separate from energy. The 2026 synthesis reports a choice-reaction-time signal but no fatigue benefit; its full methods were not independently accessed. Factorial trials, short stress studies and repeated use give mixed results. Some positive beverage evidence is a blend and responder-subset analysis. [CA24](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-026-03727-9); [CA01](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18006208/); [CA02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17891480/); [CA05](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4728665/)

- **CA-ST01 — Haskell et al. 2008** ([source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18006208/)). Isolated L-theanine/caffeine in modified 250 mL peach iced tea; **250 mg theanine; 150 mg caffeine; both; placebo**. Population: Healthy undergraduates 18–34; 12 h caffeine abstinence; n=24 completed. Double-blind randomized balanced crossover; comparator: Four factorial beverage conditions. acute; duration: Four visits; timing: 30 and 90 min.
  - Subjective: Combination increased alertness and reduced tiredness; theanine alone increased headache
  - Objective: Theanine reduced correct serial-7 answers; caffeine improved vigilance/RVIP; combination improved some other tasks
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Combination simple reaction time d=.7 versus placebo; no common noticeability threshold
  - Null/negative: No convincing theanine-alone alertness benefit; isolated relaxed contrast lacked significant omnibus effect
  - Adverse events: Headache rating increased with theanine alone. Funding/conflicts: Funding not confirmed in inspected material; commercial tea matrix. Limits: Multiple tests; small sample; combination-placebo significance does not prove superiority to caffeine. Verification: Full primary PDF inspected.

- **CA-ST02 — Rogers et al. 2008** ([source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17891480/)). Isolated theanine and caffeine; administration details not verified; **200 mg theanine; 250 mg caffeine; both; neither**. Population: Healthy adults; n=48. Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled; comparator: Factorial caffeine/theanine conditions. acute; duration: Single testing session; timing: From 40 min.
  - Subjective: Caffeine increased alertness and jitteriness; theanine did not significantly alter either or other mood
  - Objective: Theanine attenuated caffeine blood-pressure effect but slowed visual-probe reaction time
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Numeric between-group effect/CI unavailable in abstract
  - Null/negative: No demonstrated anti-jitter or alertness effect attributable to theanine
  - Adverse events: Systematic adverse-event details unavailable. Funding/conflicts: Not verified. Limits: Abstract only; BP result cannot be turned into a no-jitters experience claim. Verification: Primary abstract and author institutional record.

- **CA-ST03 — Dodd et al. 2015** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4480845/)). Suntheanine capsules; pharmaceutical caffeine; **50 mg theanine;75 mg caffeine;both;placebo**. Population: Healthy 18–35;12 habitual and 12 low caffeine consumers; overnight abstinence; n=24. Double-blind counterbalanced crossover; comparator: Four factorial conditions. acute; duration: Four treatment visits; timing: 30 min absorption then 40 min cognitive battery.
  - Subjective: Overall mood improved with caffeine; no corrected alertness pairwise differences
  - Objective: Caffeine improved choice reaction time/Stroop; the combination did not show the same significant placebo-controlled behavioural benefits; direct antagonism was not established
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: No consumer-noticeability threshold; corrected pairwise results
  - Null/negative: No theanine-alone improvement versus placebo; altered cerebral haemodynamics not proof of focus
  - Adverse events: Not systematically established in extraction. Funding/conflicts: Funding/conflicts not confirmed. Limits: Small subgroup sample; physiological outcomes not felt experience; caffeine-withdrawal context. Verification: Full text Europe PMC XML.

- **CA-ST04 — White et al. 2016** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4728665/)). NeuroBliss 430 mL: L-Tea-Active theanine plus alpha-GPC25 mg PS1 mg chamomile 10 mg; **200 mg theanine in blend**. Population: Healthy 18–40; laboratory multitasking stress; n=36 enrolled;34 completed;26 stress responders analysed. Randomized double-blind crossover; comparator: Matched beverage vehicle without actives. acute; duration: Two treatment visits; timing: Stress at 1 h/3 h;MEG2 h.
  - Subjective: Stress response reduced 1 h; general calmness/alertness/anxiety/fatigue not significantly different
  - Objective: Cortisol response reduced 3 h; no cognitive-performance difference
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Cortisol median change placebo .44 versus active−.09 at 3 h;p=.047; subjective magnitude not extracted
  - Null/negative: General mood/cognition null; alpha result limited to high-trait-anxiety subgroup
  - Adverse events: Not fully reported as systematic outcome. Funding/conflicts: Neurobrands-funded. Limits: Blend cannot isolate theanine; responder exclusion; many endpoints; three-hour cortisol is not three-hour felt calm. Verification: Full text Europe PMC XML.

- **CA-ST05 — Hidese et al. 2019** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6836118/)). Enzymatically synthesized Suntheanine tablets; **200 mg/day before sleep**. Population: Adults without major psychiatric illness;mean 48.3 years; n=30. Randomized double-blind crossover;2-week washout; comparator: Matched placebo tablets. repeated; duration: 4 weeks each condition; timing: Baseline/end of each period.
  - Subjective: Some PSQI subscales favoured theanine; total depression/sleep improvements chiefly within group
  - Objective: No between-condition differences in full-sample cognitive changes; lower-baseline subgroup verbal-fluency signal
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: PSQI sleep-latency/disturbance/medication p=.0499/.046/.047; total SDS p=.084;total PSQI p=.073
  - Null/negative: Full-sample cognition null; no cortisol/IgA effect
  - Adverse events: No apparent adverse events. Funding/conflicts: Taiyo Kagaku funded; company researchers participated. Limits: Multiple outcomes and median splits; small sample; no acute social test; compliance verbal. Verification: Full text Europe PMC XML.

- **CA-ST06 — Evans et al. 2021** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8475422/)). AlphaWave L-theanine capsule; **200 mg single dose**. Population: Healthy adults with moderate perceived stress; n=16 randomized;15 completed. Triple-blind randomized crossover;7-day washout; comparator: Matched placebo. acute; duration: Two visits; timing: Cortisol 1 h;EEG3 h;arithmetic stress.
  - Subjective: Results section reports no post-dose between-group state-anxiety/stress difference
  - Objective: Frontal/whole-scalp alpha increased 3 h; cortisol decrease greater 1 h
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Alpha p≤.050;cortisol p<.001; consumer effect size unknown
  - Null/negative: Subjective post-dose stress/anxiety null despite optimistic plain-language statement
  - Adverse events: No attributed serious event; liver-enzyme elevation in one deemed unlikely related. Funding/conflicts: Ethical Naturals funded; employee author; KGK contract researchers. Limits: Tiny study; surrogate endpoints; pre-dose stress imbalance; late trial registration relative to conduct. Verification: Full text Europe PMC XML; Results prioritized.

- **CA-ST07 — Moulin et al. 2024** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11263523/)). AlphaWave capsules; **200 mg twice daily;400 mg/day**. Population: Healthy adults 18–65 with moderate stress; n=30;15/arm. Randomized double-blind parallel; comparator: Matched placebo. repeated; duration: 28 days; timing: Days 14/28.
  - Subjective: PSS improved within both groups; no between-group difference; DASS total/stress favoured placebo
  - Objective: No between-group cognitive RT benefit; lower actigraphy total sleep/light sleep with theanine
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Day 28 DASS-total change placebo−9.20 versus theanine+1.07;p=.017;stress p=.012
  - Null/negative: PSS/cortisol/subjective sleep/cognition not superior; reduced sleep time not proof of better sleep
  - Adverse events: Two possibly related mild events:metallic taste and dry mouth. Funding/conflicts: Ethical Naturals funded; employee and KGK authors. Limits: Small trial; baseline sleep/cortisol imbalances; positive conclusions rely on within-group improvement. Verification: Full text Europe PMC XML.


### D. Expected experience and timing

A demanding task may feel easier or less stressful for some consumers, but many may notice nothing. A reliable subjective caffeine-free boost, onset and duration in healthy rested adults are unknown. Acute studies sample selected points around 30–120 minutes; four-week findings are not acute duration. Hundreds-of-milligrams liquid administration is documented, without proving an effective finished can. No direct sociability or alcohol-like effect established.

### E. Safety and repeated consumption

Headache, metallic taste and dry mouth appear in selected studies. Short trials do not establish unrestricted multiple-can exposure, tolerance/withdrawal absence, pregnancy/childhood suitability or interaction-free use. No driving or universal non-drowsiness claim follows. Medicine use and intended repeated consumption need exact-material review. [CA01](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18006208/); [CA07](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8475422/); [CA08](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11263523/)

### F. EU and Czech feasibility / communication

**Ordinary-beverage route:** **Unresolved and source-specific.** Italian Article 4 decision published 13 Mar 2026 calls ≥98% material made by non-GM *E.coli* strainB fermentation novel, despite comparable identity/purity to tea-derived substance. Taiyo NF2024-15277 requests supplement-only authorisation. Tea infusion history does not establish isolated amino-acid use.

**Claims:** No applicable authorised calm, focus, energy or social-ease claim established. Molecular equivalence does not create claims permission.

**Remaining gate:** Obtain exact manufacturing route and lawful ordinary-food basis; tea-derived, enzymatically made, chemically synthesised and fermented materials must not be treated as one regulatory entry. Do not infer the decision makes every theanine source novel. [EU08](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/1e468cb7-af61-4aa2-8d4f-976f9783dbae_en?filename=novel-food_consult-status_l-teanina.pdf); [EU09](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/a498e343-3403-427d-a95d-6a8fa4226c3b_en?filename=novel-food_sum_ongoing-app_2024-15277.pdf)

Any naturalness statement must match the actual origin and process and comply with EU/Czech wording rules; plant origin or fermentation alone does not clear it. No final extract/material or finished label is approved. [EU06](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/2014-12-13); [EU34](https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/legislativa/vap87546-131)

### G. Beverage feasibility

**Evidence:** P: theanine in model solutions had concentration-dependent mixed umami/sweet/bitter taste, and IMP enhanced umami; this is not proof of taste neutrality at an RTD serving [FM01](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18997398/). S: Taiyo advertises ≥98% enzymatically produced L-theanine for beverages; Blue California's submitted experiments report good retention under specified pH/heat conditions [FM02](https://www.taiyointernational.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/ST_Data-Beverage_2015.pdf), [FM03](https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2014-S-0023-0044/attachment_1.pdf).

**Transfer judgment:** Hundreds-of-milligrams research exposures are not intrinsically a mass obstacle in a can. Exact purity, enantiomer ratio, residual caffeine for tea-derived material, carrier and production route matter. Do not transfer a source's clinical or legal status to all “natural theanine.” Quantitative cold solubility, aroma, precipitation, carbonation, can-lining interactions and end-of-life retention in the intended formula remain unknown. Test pure ingredient versus base at matched pH as well as original pH; altered sourness is not necessarily perceptual masking. White powder/low-colour claims are supplier information. Obtain formulation-specific acid/heat/light data; no proof here of shelf-stable physiological effect.

**Cost and finished product:** quote required. No prototype sensory, shelf-life, carbonation or package testing was performed. Physical feasibility cannot repair absent efficacy or permission.

**Integrated decision:** Research-only calm candidate; not energy substitute

## E02 — Theobromine; purified 3,7-dimethylxanthine

### A. Identity and provenance

3,7-dimethylxanthine, naturally occurring in cacao. Purified theobromine and cocoa are different preparations: cocoa supplies other constituents and may contain caffeine. Extraction versus synthesis must be documented; natural occurrence does not establish commercial origin. Study evidence can transfer only with verified identity/purity and relevant matrix. [EN02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23764688/); [EN03](https://palmerlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Baggott-et-al-Psychopharm-2013.pdf)

### B. Perception and commercial use

A gentler, longer-lasting cocoa-associated energy story is a hypothesis, not an established consumer preference or pharmacological result. No exact current qualifying RTD with a fully verified standalone theobromine dose was established in this 14-product benchmark sample. Familiar chocolate is not proof of recognition of the isolated ingredient. [EN02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23764688/); [CP20](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15458801/)

### C. Controlled human evidence

Two directly caffeine-controlled studies make this a particularly informative negative screen. Food-relevant beverage exposure did not reproduce caffeine mood/vigilance benefits; escalating purified exposure produced more unpleasant responses rather than a dependable good effect. [EN02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23764688/); [EN03](https://palmerlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Baggott-et-al-Psychopharm-2013.pdf)

- **EN-ST02 — Judelson et al. 2013** ([source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23764688/)). Theobromine in cocoa or non-cocoa beverages; **100; 200; 400 mg theobromine in cocoa; 0/400 mg theobromine and 100 mg caffeine non-cocoa controls**. Population: Healthy young men; mean age 23 years; n=24. Double-blind six-condition crossover in one sample; six visits, not six independent studies; comparator: Non-cocoa placebo and caffeine positive control. Acute; duration: Six test visits; timing: Two-hour visual vigilance task; exact post-dose start unavailable.
  - Subjective: Theobromine no consistent mood effect; caffeine reduced lethargy/fatigue and increased vigor
  - Objective: Theobromine no consistent vigilance effect; salivary levels confirmed exposure
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Theobromine P>.05; caffeine fatigue P=.006 and vigor P=.011; effect magnitude unavailable
  - Null/negative: Theobromine failed across tested exposures; mechanism did not predict benefit
  - Adverse events: Not stated in abstract. Funding/conflicts: Not confirmed from abstract; coauthors include cocoa-industry researchers. Limits: Small male sample; no long-term repeated soda use. Verification: Primary abstract.

- **EN-ST03 — Baggott et al. 2013** ([source](https://palmerlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Baggott-et-al-Psychopharm-2013.pdf)). Purified theobromine capsules; caffeine capsules; **250; 500; 1000 mg theobromine; 200 mg caffeine**. Population: Healthy low methylxanthine consumers aged 18–35; n=84 completed/included per full text; abstract says 80. Double-blind placebo-controlled within-subject Williams design; comparator: Placebo and caffeine. Acute; duration: Five laboratory sessions; timing: Theobromine cognition ~190–250 min; caffeine ~40–100 min after its later capsule.
  - Subjective: Caffeine stimulant-like mood; theobromine limited at 250 mg and greater dislike/dysphoria at higher doses
  - Objective: Theobromine heart-rate rise; no digit-span or verbal-learning benefit
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: No global noticeable-energy effect estimate
  - Null/negative: Low-dose theobromine did not reproduce caffeine profile
  - Adverse events: Headache/nausea concentrated at 1000 mg; one vomited; higher heart rate. Funding/conflicts: Unilever R&D and NIH grants; Unilever coauthors. Limits: Dose-dependent harms; multiple outcomes; reporting inconsistency in n. Verification: Primary full-text PDF.


### D. Expected experience and timing

No consistent desirable felt-energy effect or beneficial duration established. Longer residence of a molecule is not a demonstrated longer useful experience. In healthy adults the direct comparator is more relevant than mechanistic extrapolation. The mass studied at the upper beverage exposures poses a dissolution challenge; no single-can effect is established.

### E. Safety and repeated consumption

Higher capsule exposures increased heart rate and unpleasant effects including nausea/headache/dysphoria. No safe multi-can amount, sleep compatibility or tolerance/withdrawal conclusion follows. A flavouring-use limit is neither a recommended physiological dose nor proof of safety for repeated functional enrichment. [EN03](https://palmerlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Baggott-et-al-Psychopharm-2013.pdf); [EU11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/1482/oj)

### F. EU and Czech feasibility / communication

**Ordinary-beverage route:** Familiar occurrence in cocoa is distinct from isolated addition. Regulation 2018/1482 records flavouring use FL16.032, including 100 mg/kg in category 14.1 nonalcoholic beverages. This is a **flavouring-use condition**, not automatic functional-use clearance or a safe-dose recommendation.

**Claims:** No authorised alertness/lasting-energy claim established. Caffeine-free compositional wording would not establish stimulant-free or absence of sleep/cardiovascular effects.

**Remaining gate:** Verify current flavouring annex and legal route for intended physiological use; cocoa extracts also require caffeine testing and composition matching. [EU11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/1482/oj)

Any naturalness statement must match the actual origin and process and comply with EU/Czech wording rules; plant origin or fermentation alone does not clear it. No final extract/material or finished label is approved. [EU06](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/2014-12-13); [EU34](https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/legislativa/vap87546-131)

### G. Beverage feasibility

P: a primary solubility paper reports the literature water baseline at approximately 330 mg/L at 25 °C and experimentally demonstrates strong solvent dependence [FM04](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4923/13/8/1118). Arithmetic: that baseline corresponds to approximately 109 mg dissolved in 330 mL **plain water**, not a validated soda limit. The 400 mg exposure in a human beverage study in the energy workstream would be about 1.21 g/L in a 330 mL package; simply copying that mass into a clear soda is unsupported.

I: expect a substantial solubility/settling-development question at several hundred milligrams; cold precipitation and uniformity require measurement. Cocoa brings particles, colour, other methylxanthines and flavour, and cannot be treated as pure theobromine. Bitter intensity is a sensory question for the exact preparation, not a measured defect in our drink. The research's organic-solvent or eutectic systems are not proposed food ingredients. Exact beverage-grade supply, acidic heat/light stability, carbonation/pack compatibility and cost remain unverified.

**Cost and finished product:** quote required. No prototype sensory, shelf-life, carbonation or package testing was performed. Physical feasibility cannot repair absent efficacy or permission.

**Integrated decision:** Poor efficacy-to-risk choice for energy

## E03 — Paraxanthine; purified 1,7-dimethylxanthine

### A. Identity and provenance

1,7-dimethylxanthine, a major human caffeine metabolite and a stimulant in its own right. The reviewed ENFINITY studies and EU application concern a defined synthetic product; metabolism after coffee does not make the supplied powder extracted/natural. Match purity, caffeine assay, impurities and process before sharing branded evidence. [EN04](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8622427/); [EN05](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8708375/); [EN06](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11089923/); [EU10](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/330ca889-27cc-4db5-835b-a0670180b85a_en?filename=novel-food_sum_ongoing-app_2022-90305.pdf)

### B. Perception and commercial use

UPDATE Grape markets a caffeine-free stimulant alternative and discloses 300 mg paraxanthine. Some forum users report effects and others little benefit; mixed products, histories and self-selection make this non-representative. Purchase-driver status is unknown. Chemically caffeine-free may still conflict with a consumer expectation of avoiding stimulation. [CP07](https://drinkupdate.com/pages/faqs); [CP08](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-1011018246); [CP24](https://www.reddit.com/r/NootropicsDepot/comments/17r0ca1/paraxanthine/)

### C. Controlled human evidence

All three extracted trials are small and substantially from the same sponsor-linked network. Null overall analyses and selected positive time contrasts coexist. The exercise trial includes a caffeine comparator but does not establish everyday equivalence or universal superiority. [EN04](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8622427/); [EN05](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8708375/); [EN06](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11089923/)

- **EN-ST04 — Yoo et al. 2021; Nutrients 13:3980** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8622427/)). ENFINITY paraxanthine capsule; **200 mg**. Population: Healthy adults; 10 men/3 women; mean age 23.6; n=13 completed. Randomized double-blind counterbalanced crossover; 7-day washout; comparator: Placebo; no caffeine arm. Acute; duration: 6 hours; timing: 1;2;3;4;5;6 hours.
  - Subjective: No validated noticeable-energy benefit established
  - Objective: Selected task/time contrasts favorable
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: 6-h PAR errors 8.17±1.5 vs 10.38±3.2; P=.034; selected contrast
  - Null/negative: No overall/univariate treatment×time effects across BCST; Go/No-Go; Sternberg; PVT families
  - Adverse events: No subjective side effects reported. Funding/conflicts: Ingenious Ingredients funded; sponsor principals/patent interests among authors. Limits: Very small; multiplicity; within-condition change is not between-condition efficacy; no sleep or withdrawal follow-up. Verification: Primary full-text XML; results/funding inspected.

- **EN-ST05 — Xing et al. 2021; Nutrients 13:4478** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8708375/)). ENFINITY paraxanthine capsules; **50;100;200 mg/day**. Population: Healthy adults; mean age 22.7; n=12. Double-blind placebo-controlled counterbalanced crossover; comparator: Placebo; no caffeine. Acute and repeated; duration: 7 days per treatment; at least 7-day washout; timing: 1–6 h day 1 and follow-up day 7.
  - Subjective: Side-effect ratings; no established perceived-energy improvement
  - Objective: Selected cognitive/timepoint signals more consistent at 100/200 mg
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: No single primary clinically meaningful benefit estimate
  - Null/negative: Overall/univariate treatment-by-time analyses were nonsignificant for BCST, Go/No-Go, Sternberg and vigilance task families; no consistent dose-response. Selected time-point contrasts do not override these null overall analyses.
  - Adverse events: No significant chemistry/side-effect differences over seven days. Funding/conflicts: Ingenious Ingredients funded; sponsor principals and paraxanthine patent inventors authors. Limits: Tiny multiple-endpoint pilot; seven days cannot establish tolerance/withdrawal or chronic safety. Verification: Primary full-text XML.

- **EN-ST06 — Yoo et al. 2024** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11089923/)). ENFINITY paraxanthine; caffeine capsules; **200 mg paraxanthine; 200 mg caffeine; 200+200 mg combination**. Population: Trained runners; n=12. Randomized double-blind crossover; 7–14-day washout; comparator: Placebo; caffeine; combination. Acute; duration: Single testing day per arm; timing: Baseline; 60 min after ingestion pre-run; after 10-km run.
  - Subjective: No established everyday noticeable-energy outcome
  - Objective: Selected post-run errors and vigilance favored paraxanthine
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Post-run PAR errors vs caffeine −26.9% (95% CI −50.5 to −3.4); P=.026; mean PVT vs placebo −23.2% (−43.4 to −2.4); P=.029
  - Null/negative: Overall error comparison vs caffeine P=.059; caffeine addition no clear extra benefit
  - Adverse events: No adverse side-effect/blood-marker signal. Funding/conflicts: Ingenious Ingredients fee-for-service; sponsor interests disclosed. Limits: Small exercise-specific trial; not equivalence/noninferiority; multiple endpoints; excludes sleep-risk assessment. Verification: Primary full-text XML.


### D. Expected experience and timing

Selected attention/performance changes are plausible research signals. A dependable noticeable boost and sustained benefit are not established. Six-hour measurement does not mean six-hour benefit; seven-day use does not establish chronic advantage. Rested-office and post-run populations are different. General noticeability and benefit-harm comparison with caffeine remain unknown.

### E. Safety and repeated consumption

Tiny short studies cannot establish rare harms, long-term cardiovascular or sleep effects, tolerance or withdrawal. Do not copy caffeine safety levels or interpret no reported events as no risk. Several social servings would multiply a centrally active exposure; no safe count is derived. Vulnerable groups and medication interactions remain insufficiently characterised. [EN04](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8622427/); [EN05](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8708375/); [EN06](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11089923/); [EU10](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/330ca889-27cc-4db5-835b-a0670180b85a_en?filename=novel-food_sum_ongoing-app_2022-90305.pdf)

### F. EU and Czech feasibility / communication

**Ordinary-beverage route:** NF2022-9030 applicant expressly identifies it as novel, chemically synthesised, ≥97%, with proposed functional-drink and supplement uses. **No applicable beverage authorisation established** in current reviewed Union-list material.

**Claims:** Not an authorised caffeine-replacement health claim. Applicant's caffeine safety analogy is not EFSA conclusion.

**Remaining gate:** Wait for applicable authorisation or establish a different lawful exact-material basis; do not import proposed caffeine-like limits into a formula. [EU03](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food/authorisations/union-list-novel-foods_en); [EU10](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/330ca889-27cc-4db5-835b-a0670180b85a_en?filename=novel-food_sum_ongoing-app_2022-90305.pdf)

Any naturalness statement must match the actual origin and process and comply with EU/Czech wording rules; plant origin or fermentation alone does not clear it. No final extract/material or finished label is approved. [EU06](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/2014-12-13); [EU34](https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/legislativa/vap87546-131)

### G. Beverage feasibility

S: one research-reagent supplier gives aqueous solubility as 1 mg/mL [FM05](https://www.scbt.com/de/p/paraxanthine-611-59-6). [EU20](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2023.8006); [LE32](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/); [EU19](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/clanek/obohacene-potraviny.aspx)

### F. EU and Czech feasibility / communication

**Ordinary-beverage route:** Only allowed vitamins/source forms for ordinary-food fortification, with any additional novel-food conditions respected. “B complex” is not one chemical or one efficacy finding.

**Claims:** B5 normal mental performance; B2/B3/B5/B6/B12 reduced tiredness/fatigue and specified energy metabolism; B1/B6/B12 and others have specific nervous/psychological claims. Folic acid is not a general energy-metabolism claim.

**Remaining gate:** Match exact nutrient/claim; formulation and shelf-life assays; multi-SKU B6 exposure; distinguish nicotinic acid from nicotinamide safety. ### G. Beverage feasibility

P: one fortified nutrition drink retained B2/B6/B12 while folate declined markedly over long storage; its nutrient-rich matrix is not a soda [FM26](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8650259/). P: cyanocobalamin stability can worsen in the presence of ascorbic acid in aqueous buffer [FM25](https://doi.org/10.1248/cpb.53.688). ## N03 — Glucose/sucrose or carbohydrate-providing juice

### A. Identity and provenance

Precisely identify glucose/dextrose, sucrose or other carbohydrate. They are not interchangeable by the word “energy”; engineered/slowly digested carbohydrates can have distinct law and physiology. Corn/grain/other source and refining process need documentation for provenance claims. Evidence here focuses on glucose-containing drinks. [LE03](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4265209/); [EN19](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16401645/); [EU30](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2015/7/oj/eng/pdf)

### B. Perception and commercial use

Sweetness, refreshment and calories are recognisable sensory/nutritional properties, but “sustained energy/no crash” is a separate claim. Sugar preference and target-market acceptance were not measured. Current energy-drink associations cannot show that carbohydrate alone causes alertness. Controlled carbonated-drink research is more informative for that question. [LE03](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4265209/)

### C. Controlled human evidence

Glucose can affect selected tasks under some fasting/demanding conditions; a 150 person soda trial found no primary multitasking or mood benefit for glucose-only arms. A combination result without caffeine-only comparator cannot establish glucose contribution or synergy. [EN19](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16401645/); [LE03](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4265209/)

- **EN-ST19 — Reay et al. 2006** ([source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16401645/)). G115 capsules; glucose beverage; **200 mg G115; 25 g glucose; each alone and combined**. Population: Healthy young overnight-fasted adults; n=27. Double-blind balanced placebo-controlled crossover factorial; comparator: Placebo and separate components. Acute; duration: Test morning; timing: Capsules; drink 30 min later; six 10-min batteries from 60 min after capsules.
  - Subjective: Glucose and ginseng reduced later-task mental fatigue
  - Objective: Mental arithmetic improved; glucose RVIP accuracy improved
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Numerical between-condition magnitude/CI unavailable in abstract
  - Null/negative: No cognitive synergy between G115 and glucose
  - Adverse events: Not reported in abstract. Funding/conflicts: Not confirmed. Limits: 25 g glucose adds 100 kcal; short fasted task setting; no all-day energy/no-crash evidence. Verification: Primary abstract.

- **LE-S03 — Scholey 2014** ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4265209/)). 330 ml carbonated orange drinks; **25 g or 60 g glucose;60 g glucose+40 mg caffeine**. Population: Healthy adults;mean age 34.78; n=150. Double-blind randomized parallel 4-arm; comparator: Sugar-free placebo; glucose-only arms. Acute; duration: Single visit; timing: Task 30 min after drink.
  - Subjective: No significant mood or perceived-effort effects
  - Objective: Combination improved multitasking vs placebo/60 g;glucose alone no significant primary benefit
  - Magnitude/uncertainty: Overall multitasking F(3,146)=3.56;p=.015; no meaningful-change threshold
  - Null/negative: Mood null; no convincing glucose-only effect
  - Adverse events: None reported. Funding/conflicts: GSK funded/supplied drinks; consulting disclosed. Limits: No caffeine-alone arm; selected lab task; not sustained energy proof. Verification: full text.


### D. Expected experience and timing

Calories are delivered immediately upon consumption but that does not specify onset of felt energy. Benefit depends on need/occasion; ordinary rested-person sustained alertness remains unestablished. Tens-of-grams exposures may physically fit a soda but create substantial sugar/calorie trade-offs and conflict with low-sugars positioning.

### E. Safety and repeated consumption

Repeated servings add sugar/energy and dental exposure; glucose regulation and medical context may affect suitability. Do not claim a guaranteed crash or no crash from glycaemic mechanisms alone. No safe serving maximum derived, and sports claim conditions do not create a need in every consumer. [EU30](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2015/7/oj/eng/pdf); [EU31](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/2025-04-01/eng)

### F. EU and Czech feasibility / communication

**Ordinary-beverage route:** Conventional glucose/sucrose and permitted common carbohydrates have plausible ordinary-food routes; unusual engineered carbohydrates can have separate conditions.

**Claims:** Calories are energy provision, not guaranteed alertness. Special sports carbohydrate/electrolyte or recovery claims have exact exercise and composition conditions; they do not authorise a generic office-energy soda promise.

**Remaining gate:** Sugars/nutrition declarations, glycaemic and sensory implications, dental exposure and actual use occasion. [EU07](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/432/2025-08-20/eng); [EU30](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2015/7/oj/eng/pdf)

Any naturalness statement must match the actual origin and process and comply with EU/Czech wording rules; plant origin or fermentation alone does not clear it. No final extract/material or finished label is approved. [EU06](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/2014-12-13); [EU34](https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/legislativa/vap87546-131)

### G. Beverage feasibility

The energy workstream's 25 g glucose study entails 100 kcal from carbohydrate and, if delivered in 330 mL, approximately 7.6 g sugars/100 mL. This is arithmetic, not a proposed recipe; it would conflict with an EU low-sugars proposition under the reviewed threshold. Sugars are a readily available food ingredient route with established liquid delivery, but no supplier or price is qualified here.

I: glucose, sucrose and slowly digested carbohydrates are not physiologically or sensorially interchangeable. Define actual carbohydrate and any carriers, sweetness, body, osmotic load and nutritional trade-offs. Reducing sugars can interact with amino compounds; the GABA and taurine model studies illustrate why combined-matrix retention needs verification [FM29](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308814608005037), [FM11](https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.S0022-0302(90)78846-7)78846-7). No model reaction rate is transferred to soda. Acidity, thermal exposure, colour/aroma drift, dissolved solids and microbial/process requirements need a normal co-packer program. A sugar can does not automatically provide lasting alertness or “no crash.”
