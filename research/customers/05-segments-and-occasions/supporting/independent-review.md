## Checks passed at first pass

- **Audience recommendation:** presented as a learning priority with unverified assumptions, not a population-size, profitability or launch-market conclusion. The early-afternoon window is explicitly not caffeine advice. Zero-caffeine and lower-caffeine people are distinguished.
- **Segments:** five behavioural, overlapping need states; work/study and fatigue receive priority while activity/recovery, everyday wellbeing, private evening/sleep and social participation remain covered. Emotional pressures are hypotheses, not stereotypes. S4's combined label is acceptable if sub-occasions remain separate (R02).
- **Occasions:** morning, deep work, meetings, adult study, afternoon, exercise, everyday meals, after work, social gathering and bedtime are explicit. Desired immediate experience is separated from repeated routines and physiological efficacy.
- **Product filter:** sampled exact anchors are cans/bottles. Generic eligible classes are conditional on exact-SKU checks before becoming test comparators. Prepared coffee, rest and supplements are labelled habits, not added commercial benchmarks.
- **Price and frequency:** every segment's acceptable spend is unknown. Offered prices are not willingness to pay; frequency ranges are labelled analyst hypotheses. Multipack, shipping, venue and employer-payer differences are explicit. No US price is used as Czech willingness to pay.
- **Calculations and allocations:** 8 + (4 × 3) = 20 planned interviews; 4 + 4 = 8 diarists; 8 × 7 = 56 possible person-days; CZK49 × 3 = CZK147 and × 5 = CZK245. The report does not treat occasions as independent participants.
- **Fieldwork integrity:** no interviews, purchases or tests are claimed. Adult consent, satisfied users, nonbuyers, no-purchase choices and privacy controls appear. A five-of-eight discovery gate is explicitly a chosen heuristic, not statistical validation.
- **Productivity:** wakefulness, attention, errors, session duration and task completion remain separate. The diary is observational. No causal claim about actual work output is made.
- **Practical scope:** early interviews and ordinary-habit diaries precede formulation/outsourced bench work; no equipment purchase, inventory commitment or invented supplier quote appears. The proposed 60–90-person later concept test is conditional and avoids a full factorial of tiny cells.
