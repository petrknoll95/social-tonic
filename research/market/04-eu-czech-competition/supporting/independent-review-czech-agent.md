## Source checks

**CBD scope check passed.** Independently reopened the full [EFSA synthetic-CBD opinion](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154), published 18 September 2026 and amended 21 September. Its abstract, sections 2.2 and 3.2 support the lead's crucial boundary: the assessed synthetic CBD is dissolved in MCT oil and intended exclusively for supplements; this is neither an aqueous RTD assessment nor efficacy evidence. The positive safety conclusion must not be generalized to a botanical beverage or treated as a marketing authorization. The precise opinion-derived level is **0.026 mg/kg/day**, whereas the [9 February 2026 statement](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/provisional-safe-level-cannabidiol-novel-food) gives **0.0275 mg/kg/day** for qualifying supplements. The lead distinguishes the two documents; retaining that distinction avoids a false contradiction. Both sources accessed 2026-10-17, EU scope.

**Alcohol boundary check passed.** Independently reopened [SZPI's guidance](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/docDetail.aspx?docid=3750373&doctype=ART) (12 March 2025; accessed 2026-10-17; Czechia). It supports differentiating statutory non-alcoholic drinks/beer up to 0.5% from voluntary zero-alcohol representations. The referenced 0.05% analytical capability is not a general legal permission to describe every such product as zero. The lead avoids that error. This matters to Birell, CBD beer and any fermentation-based concept.

**Demand/effect/permission separation passed.** The regulatory memo does not convert retail CBD listings into legality or demand, or turn the insomnia pilot into evidence for a lower-dose mixed beverage. It keeps CBD outside the primary concept dependency and requires actual authorization conditions before consumption work.

## Scope and limits

No material regulatory-scope error was found in the reviewed lead memo. Proposed workplace and adult-evening gaps remain **hypotheses**, because identified competitors already serve both occasions and this pass lacks buyer interviews, store audits, repeat purchase and velocity. The research protocol acknowledges these limits and distinguishes channels appropriately. The quoted consumer-test sizes and thresholds are analyst proposals, not performed research or validated population estimates.

This review did not independently repeat every national legal search, reassess every scientific study, test a product, verify a retailer checkout or clear a formulation. Source availability is not authorization. It did not review final report/matrix files, which had not yet been supplied; a targeted final check should follow integration. Source metadata for regulatory checks already exists in the lead register; the Czech retail checks are CZ09/CZ11 in [Czech sources](czechia-sources.csv).
