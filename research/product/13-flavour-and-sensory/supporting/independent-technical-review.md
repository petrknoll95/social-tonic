## Checks that passed

- **Neutonic:** the current [US Orange Sunrise page, US01](https://www.neutonic.com/en-us/products/orange-sunrise-12-pack-us) supports the named can format, declared caffeine and active-panel figures recorded in the dataset. The published ingredient-list omission of caffeine and the one-can-per-day instruction remain correctly highlighted. The Walmart platform announcement is not silently treated as an Orange formula event. Brand efficacy language is not adopted as established fact.
- **VYBES:** the current [Strawberry Lavender page, US08](https://idrinkvybes.com/products/strawberry-lavender) supports a still 12 oz bottle, 3 g sugar and **300 mg elemental magnesium**, distinct from the compound's total mass. It remains a technical reference, not an EU formulation recommendation. Full-bottle participation still requires material-specific exposure review under Stage 0; neither this review nor the retailer's availability clears it for a Czech tasting.
- **Dream:** the [current ingredients, US15](https://feelthebrez.com/products/dream) support carbonated water and the stated botanical/amino-acid/mineral blend, without quantities sufficient to calculate all active exposure. The dataset keeps caffeine and sugar unknown and does not use THC-line sales as evidence of Dream performance. The exact cannabinoid-free statement rests on its cited launch release, not a whole-brand inference.
- **Hiyo:** the [Pineapple Coconut page, US11](https://drinkhiyo.com/products/pineapple-coconut) supports a finished 12 oz canned drink and the brand's 1.7 g blend statement. Individual functional doses remain unknown; the “float” and fruit richness remain brand positioning or questions to test. A flavour name does not establish coconut cream, fat content or creamy mouthfeel.
- **Format:** the eligible candidates are finished liquid cans/bottles. Chameleon's two unresolved named coffee versions are retained as an explicitly unresolved investigation rather than silently combined into a cleared tasting product. Consumer powders, sachets, pouches, drops and concentrates are not admitted via their parent brands. At follow-up the lead removed the mixed-version Chameleon row from the benchmark dataset entirely and retained the uncertainty only in the memo; the dataset now contains nine references.
- **Flavour briefs:** all six core directions are CBD-free and identify their sensory targets as hypotheses. Tea/focus/hydration/bedtime retain still options. Aperitif bitterness is confined to a separately tested social option. Exact-mineral, botanical, residual-caffeine and low-acid process questions remain open rather than presented as solved.
- **Exposure arithmetic and repeated serving:** 120 mg per 355 ml contributes approximately **10.14 mg per 30 ml**. Two four-sample 30 ml passes total **240 ml**, with exposure calculated separately for each material. Repeats/replacement pours and background caffeine must still be counted. The protocol prohibits compulsory finishing and makes second full servings separate-day by default; 24 hours is correctly labelled a practical schedule, not a demonstrated washout.
- **Evidence boundaries:** caffeine safety reference levels are not sensory-study targets. Full-serving liking, stated repeat interest, actual second-day consumption, paid repeat and functional efficacy are separate outcomes. Unknown active doses and unsuitable legal/safety status exclude ingestion until resolved. These gates matter particularly for imported multi-active products.

## CBD wording check

The briefs do not make CBD essential to any direction. The US memo assigns CBD a separate concept/legal track and does not promote unsupported CBD-only traction into its current-event list. For the integrated report, retain the distinction established by [EFSA's February statement](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/provisional-safe-level-cannabidiol-novel-food) and [September 2026 ingredient-specific opinion](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154): the positive assessment concerns defined supplement uses, not a general authorization, aqueous RTD safety determination or established calm/sleep effect. Do not broaden the single-emulsion acidic-storage finding into a claim that all CBD drinks produce THC.

## Integrated report review

Read [report.md](../report.md) in full on 17 October 2026 after its creation. No new material technical overreach was identified. It keeps the primary directions CBD-free, does not treat US brand claims as established effects, retains Nupreme's sugar-label conflict, names local controls conditionally, and distinguishes small-sip, full-serving, second-day and paid-repeat outcomes. CBD supplement opinions are not presented as beverage clearance. The report correctly states nine benchmark records and keeps unresolved coffee outside the dataset.

**TR05 — minor precision:** the report's “Low acidity for bedtime creates a process-development question” and related F6 wording could be read as equating low perceived sourness with high pH. The brief already says pH is not a sourness proxy. Recommended wording: “If the gentle-acid brief leads to a higher-pH formulation, the process and shelf stability require separate validation.” This is a clarification of an already recognised manufacturing gate, not a finding that the proposed drink is unsafe.

At this review's final read, TR01–TR03 and the USB10 CSV-priority part of TR04 were still pending; the lead had acknowledged them. Publication should apply these small corrections and record the resolution below. No further consumer evidence or tasting result is implied by completing this review.
