# Independent cross-review: CBD, sleep, secondary claims and US benchmarks

**17 October 2026.** Reviewer: caffeine/nutrient workstream agent, independently checking the CBD/secondary workstream and lead's US benchmark memo. Scope: high-consequence legal thresholds, numerical consistency, finished-liquid format, evidence attribution and extrapolation. This is an evidence-quality review; it does not approve a label or establish every national ingredient rule.

Reviewed: [CBD, sleep and secondary claims](cbd-sleep-and-secondary.md) and [US RTD messaging benchmarks](us-rtd-messaging-benchmarks.md). The reviewer did not edit those files. Direct sources were reopened independently; observations below distinguish spot-checks from the original authors' wider work.

## Results

**No material contradiction identified in the checked conclusions.** The most consequential new finding—the Czech melatonin barrier—is well supported. SÚKL's statement dated **14 March 2023**, still published at access, expressly treats orally administered products delivering **0.5 mg/day or more** as medicines and distinguishes the lower-dose position. The memo correctly treats this as daily dose and does not claim that lower doses automatically make a food or sleep claim lawful. The authorised 1 mg sleep-onset condition therefore cannot be treated as an ordinary Czech beverage clearance. [Official SÚKL statement](https://sukl.gov.cz/dulezite-informace/zmena-rozhodovaci-praxe-v-pripade-vyrobku-obsahujici-latku-melatonin/)

The **September 2026 CBD safety opinion is real and materially narrower than a beverage permission**. Independently reopened full opinion confirms publication **18 September**, amendment **21 September**, ≥98% synthetic CBD, exclusive MCT-oil supplement use at a maximum 2 mg/day, adults over 25 excluding pregnancy/lactation, and lack of established safety for medicine users. The opinion itself explicitly separates safety from benefit efficacy. The memo accurately keeps these findings separate from the February provisional calculation and from Commission authorisation. The arithmetic **0.0275 × 70 = 1.925 mg/day** is correct. Neither value is presented as a safe dose for an aqueous RTD. [EFSA opinion](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154), [February EFSA statement](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/provisional-safe-level-cannabidiol-novel-food)

Secondary conditions are consistent with the legal entries inspected in the separate caffeine workstream: carbohydrate-electrolyte claims require the complete composition/exercise context; water's cognitive-function claim has a restricted food category; protein source/high thresholds concern energy share. Independent arithmetic checks also pass: **4 g/kg × 70 kg = 280 g** total carbohydrate, and **3 g fibre ÷ 30 kcal × 100 = 10 g/100 kcal**. The memo appropriately keeps the latter nutrition threshold separate from the native chicory-inulin bowel claim. [Regulation 432/2012](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32012R0432), [Commission nutrition conditions](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims/nutrition-claims_en)

## US benchmark spot-checks

- **Pure Leaf:** the 28 April 2026 official release explicitly specifies Peach/Raspberry **12 fl oz cans** and **69 mg caffeine**. Athlete/device messaging is present. The memo does not assign the device's effect or PepsiCo's total revenue to the tea. [Official release](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/new-pure-leaf-mental-focus-iced-tea-with-olympic-gold-medalist-jordan-chiles)
- **Neutonic:** the current US Orange page states **12 × 355 ml cans**, **120 mg caffeine**, and the rhodiola stack. It also advertises other nonqualifying formats; the memo excludes them. Preserving the distinction from the Walmart release's alpha-GPC formula is necessary. Additional source-quality caution: the text ingredient list does not explicitly name caffeine even though the page prominently declares it; the page is a commercial observation, not a verified manufacturing specification. [Exact product page](https://www.neutonic.com/en-us/products/orange-sunrise-12-pack-us)
- **VYBES:** current page identifies a **12 fl oz bottle**, **300 mg magnesium**, and the precisely qualified SPINS ranking footnote. This supports reporting a brand-attributed narrow-channel rank, not independently verified sales or efficacy. The page also promotes future shots; these were not incorrectly substituted for the current bottle. [Exact product page](https://idrinkvybes.com/products/strawberry-lavender)
- **Hiyo:** the pineapple-coconut page depicts a **12 fl oz can** and contains the effect language and anxiety/sleep review discussed. The benchmark does not turn that testimonial into established science. [Exact product page](https://drinkhiyo.com/products/pineapple-coconut)
- **SLY DREAM:** the exact page states **12 oz**, January 2026 flavour release and melatonin, with reviews shared across multiple lines. The memo correctly reports exact active dose as unknown and avoids assigning pooled reviews to this SKU. [Exact product page](https://drinkonthesly.com/products/sly%E2%84%A2-dream-orange-cream)
- **Unwind/Pepsi/Gatorade:** exact FAQ/release/product pages were reopened; the memo's nutrition/composition distinctions are coherent. **42 mg magnesium** is below **56.25 mg** (15% of EU NRV) and also below the per-100-ml route for a 12 oz can. Pepsi's fibre arithmetic is not evidence for a general gut benefit; “lower sugar” is correctly distinguished from “low sugar”. [Unwind FAQ](https://tryunwindco.com/pages/faq), [Pepsi release](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/pepsi-prebiotic-cola-now-available-nationwide-in-store-and-online), [Gatorade exact bottled SKU](https://www.gatorade.com/sports-drinks/gatorade-lower-sugar/lemonade)

## Integration safeguards

Keep the distinction between **EU-authorised normal functions** and **US effect promises** in the final matrix. Preserve the unresolved caffeine transition route rather than stating that 75 mg is a permission threshold. Preserve the Czech melatonin obstacle and CBD's separate ingredient/safety/claim gates. Keep press-release rollout counts attributed as announcements and every SKU's sales unknown unless a genuine line-specific measure is available. Current product pages can change and contain inconsistent claims; do not turn their marketing into a formula dossier.

The review sampled primary sources and calculations rather than redoing every launch announcement or the entire CBD authorisation search. No purchase, outreach or consumer/clinical test was performed.
