# 04 · EU and Czech competition

**Date:** 17 October 2026. **Status:** completed exploratory research, integrated from three parallel country workstreams and independently reviewed. This is not a retail census, validated launch choice, formulation clearance or sales forecast.

The research maps direct competitors and realistic substitutes for an early-stage Czech-based founder exploring non-alcoholic functional drinks. Focus, clarity, productivity and energy have priority; calm, social occasions and wind-down are assessed separately, with hydration/gut opportunities secondary. The primary concept remains CBD-free. CBD is an independent extension question, never a prerequisite. No formula, budget or launch market is selected.

Only finished ready-to-drink liquids in cans or bottles qualify. The sample contains **52 exact-SKU country records**, including unavailable/watchlist offers: Czechia 16, Germany 8, Austria 7, Poland 6, Netherlands 5, France 6; UK 2 and Switzerland 2 are separate non-EU references. The **137-source register** supplies publication/access dates, geography and claim limitations. Source count is not independent evidence count or research completeness.

The central conclusion is that Czech focus and calm propositions already face direct competition. A useful opportunity must demonstrate a specific customer switching reason or a verified gap at named outlets. Exact-SKU local sales, recognition and repeat are largely unknown; missing public sales are not evidence of zero demand. The proposed opportunities are questions to validate, not established white space.
