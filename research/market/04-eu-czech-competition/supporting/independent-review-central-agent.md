## Outcome

The integrated conclusions are appropriately conditional. Existing products are not used as proof of saturation; missing sales are not treated as zero demand; focus and evening opportunities remain tests of a specific occasion and switch reason. UK and Switzerland remain separate non-EU references. No inspected passage recommends copying US claims or equates a CBD listing with authorization. The main formulation-safety update is supported by the reopened primary source.

No blocking factual contradiction was found in the sampled central conclusions. Two evidence-maintenance points were sent to the lead: use a newly located exact GIMBER retailer URL in place of a broad catalog link, and retain uncertain Chilled availability because the reviewer experienced both a cached result and a later 404. A minor wording point: the Germany summary mentions hydration even though the completed German rows do not independently document a hydration SKU; remove that word or supply a specific source before treating it as sampled evidence.

## Inference and arithmetic checks

The proposed work-occasion, evening-venue and clearer-information opportunities are hypotheses with rejection criteria. The Czech caffeine contrast is not called an unoccupied low-caffeine segment. Retail/interview data is explicitly required before a national distribution, saturation, recognition, margin or market-choice conclusion becomes reliable.

Recomputed examples: Symples €34.90/12=€2.9083; French TRIP €14/6=€2.3333; Chilled €27/12=€2.25; Heineken €6.99/6=€1.165. These are asking prices per full container, not standardized effect doses. Hospitality and DTC/retail context is preserved. Global or UK sales are not assigned to EU countries, and brand revenue from excluded formats is not used as liquid success.

The positive EFSA conclusion is constrained to the specified material and supplement use. The small insomnia study cited by the lead is explicitly reused from earlier research, with route/dose/population transfer limitations; this reviewer did not re-audit that study's full text. The report does not claim clinical effects from botanical branding, fermentation, nootropic terminology or retailer copy.

Residual limitations remain substantive: no physical label assay, no completed checkout, no comprehensive hospital/medicine-interaction review, no national awareness study and no independent store-level functional-SKU sell-through. These are correctly disclosed; this review does not eliminate them.
