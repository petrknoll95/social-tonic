# Initial screen for the forty-name round

**17 October 2026 · Europe/Prague.** Quoted candidate searches with drink/beverage/brand terms; selected names also received broader brand searches. Inspected returned text and excerpts. Common phrases frequently produced partial or separate-word matches. These observations do not constitute official trademark clearance, a global originality check, or a finding that a name is available.

“Single word” includes ordinary dictionary words and readable English compounds. It does not mean an unused string. Existing non-beverage businesses, close food/hospitality uses and unresolved discovery records remain visible below. Several retained candidates are creative alternatives with a higher screening burden, not adoption recommendations.
