# Low-cost validation and retail evidence protocol

**Proposed next work, 17 October 2026.** Nothing below has been conducted. Counts and thresholds are planning choices, not results, quoted costs or industry norms. All candidate products are finished canned/bottled liquids. These are this assignment's next-step proposals; the [US topic](../../01-us-product-landscape/README.md) supplies product and evidence context.

## Resolve the competitive gap before buying inventory

| Question | Inexpensive first method | Required output | What would reject the opportunity? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does a work-focus drink solve a recurring problem better than local alternatives? | 12 adult last-occasion interviews, split between regular caffeine users and people who limit it; recruit beyond friends | Actual occasion, existing choice, spend, caffeine timing, taste complaints and reason the current drink fails | Customers are satisfied with CANS+/Vilgain/coffee/tea/ordinary energy, or only want a cheaper version |
| Is an adult evening drink missing from the places people actually buy? | 8 adult last-occasion interviews plus an audit of 12 relevant Prague/Brno outlets, including grocery, specialist, cafés and coworking/office provision | Specific unavailable drink attributes at named outlets; acceptable taste/price; which incumbent they would stop buying | Customers already have a satisfactory canned/bottled beer, mocktail, kombucha, soda or calm drink at that outlet |
| Are low stimulant, caffeine-free and CBD-free propositions understood? | Randomized Czech concept test with 60–100 adults; equal mockup quality; no-purchase choice; focus and evening sessions separate | Choice at price, comprehension and credibility; compare occasion wording versus ingredient wording | Interest disappears without unsubstantiated immediate-effect promises or with truthful caffeine disclosure |
| Is a claimed distribution gap real? | Observe shelf/menu availability on two dates and request buyer interviews later with user authorization | Exact SKU and barcode, stock state, location, cold/ambient placement, category neighbors, pack/price, buyer terms and rotation | Online listings translate into broad convenient access, or buyers see no unmet occasion and cannot justify another SKU |
| Is the formula worth repeating? | After legal/technical screening, outsourced bench samples in sealed cans/bottles; 20–30 adults; blind taste then branded evaluation over several real occasions | Taste, finish, tolerability, portion suitability and repeat choice against current local SKU controls | Candidate loses blind preference or customers do not choose it again at the intended price |

Interview supplement use and freshly brewed coffee/tea only as **routines**: frequency, cost, preparation friction and whether a sealed RTD would actually replace them. Do not add capsules, powders or café cups to the product benchmark table. Distinguish replacing a purchase from adding another drink.

For a focus concept, include CANS+ Performance and Vilgain Energy & Focus as direct local controls, plus an eligible bottled/canned coffee or tea and ordinary energy control. Include **Magnesia Plus Focus** as a lower-price, larger-bottle control. For an evening concept, include CANS+ Balance, **Magnesia Plus Antistress**, a verified non-alcoholic beer, premium soda and bottled/canned mocktail. Both reviewed Magnesia offers were CZK27.90/700 ml, with CZK35.90 reference prices; this is a promotion/portion comparison, not a permanently cheaper equivalent serving. Compare equivalent volumes/price disclosure, or explicitly test the format difference. Blind caffeine-matched testing is needed to investigate incremental cognitive effects; a consumer preference test cannot substantiate health claims.

## Price experiment

Start from the observed Czech shelf anchors in the [matrix](../competitor-matrix.md), then test, for example, **CZK39 / 49 / 59 per can** in randomized cells with the actual relevant comparator and a no-purchase option. These are proposed test prices, not recommended retail prices or proof of margin feasibility. Use the 60–100-person sample in stages: first narrow the occasion/concept, then test price or wording in a small number of cells. Do not cross every concept, price, caffeine group and message into underpowered segments or claim statistical precision. Separate single-can grocery prices from DTC pack economics, shipping, promotions and refundable deposits. Do not extrapolate a small convenience sample to national demand.

Predefine a decision rule before fieldwork. One possible exploratory gate: recurring unmet occasion described by at least half of the small interview segment, candidate preferred at price over its strongest direct control in the concept sample, and no meaningful taste/tolerance disadvantage in repeated use. This is a chosen stop/go heuristic; report counts and uncertainty rather than a population percentage. If the strongest direct control wins, revise the proposition or stop.

## Retail data request specification

Before a national launch recommendation, obtain a priced proposal or accessible retailer data for **Czechia first** (none obtained here):

- Last 52 weeks and prior comparable period; units, liters, retail value, average and promoted price, numerical/weighted distribution, stock availability and units/store/week. Include the outlet/channel universe and exclusions.
- Exact EAN/SKU separation for CANS+ Performance/Balance, Vilgain Energy & Focus, Magnesia Plus variants and suitable substitute SKUs. Do not merge capsules/powders with liquid lines or global brand sales with Czech sales.
- Retailer split: supermarkets/hypermarkets, drugstores, convenience/petrol, specialist, online grocery, DTC and hospitality. Where a provider cannot measure a channel, leave it unknown.
- New listings, delistings, promotion intensity, stockouts and repeat/household penetration where available. A low-sales SKU with low availability has a different interpretation from low velocity at broad distribution.
- Comparable questions for one second country only after a plausible Czech segment/channel is identified. Compare Germany/Austria/Poland as nearby options, Netherlands/France for alternative retail models; do not assume EU portability of formula, label, tax/deposit or distributor terms.

Buyer questions should seek concrete category-role, margin, case-size, shelf-life, delivery and return requirements. A buyer's willingness to trial is not a binding purchase order. An online stockist count is not numerical distribution.

Include the existing CANS office and business offers in the buyer discussion; a generic office-delivery proposition is already offered. Establish an unsolved problem at a named account before treating workplace access as a gap. The [report](../report.md) links the observed incumbent channel offers.

## Manufacturing and spending gates

Sequence: interviews and shelf audit → concept/price evidence → exact formula/claims screen → outsourced bench work → stability and sensory validation → limited paid pilot. Avoid a filling-line purchase and custom package tooling. Request comparable quotes only after an occasion and legal ingredient route are plausible; no MOQ, capacity or supplier quote is assumed in this research.

Prefer existing can/bottle sizes and a contract packer's established process when the sensory and safety requirements permit. Check solubility, carbonation/foaming, pH, microbiological process, sediment, ingredient and caffeine/nutrient assays, light/heat stability, liner compatibility, storage and transport. Claims and ingredient dose must remain valid at end of shelf life. A common production base for two concepts is only an efficiency hypothesis until bench work confirms compatibility.

Run DTC and wholesale contribution models separately. Use actual receipts excluding VAT/deposit, ingredients, package, packing, analytical testing, freight, fulfillment, distributor/retailer deductions, returns and waste; stress-test reorder and inventory age. No financial forecast is supportable from the public competitor map alone.

CBD remains a **separate non-consumption concept exercise** until its authorization and safety gates clear. Compare identical CBD-free and CBD-labeled concepts without suggesting the latter is legally available for launch. Do not make the core brand dependent on the extension.
