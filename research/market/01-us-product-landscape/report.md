# US functional RTD landscape research report

**Research cut-off: 17 October 2026 · US commercial benchmarks · EU/Czech context**

This report documents US functional-drink products, commercial activity, consumer-demand signals and scientific evidence through 17 October 2026. It compares categories and formulations without selecting a launch concept, ranking opportunities or proposing product-development actions. CBD-containing drinks are analyzed separately from CBD-free products and THC-led products.

The landscape contains **31 exact liquid product records**: 12 focus/energy, 8 relaxation/sleep records with no declared cannabinoids, 3 CBD including one historical withdrawal, and 8 secondary-category products. One secondary record crosses into energy and is counted once. The [dated landscape table](landscape.md) and [consolidated dataset](data/product-landscape.csv) preserve launch chronology, current packaging, disclosed doses, caffeine, prices, claims, timing, occasions, distribution, confidence and unknowns. See [scope and files](README.md), [methods](supporting/research-protocol.md) and [sources](sources.csv).

## Taxonomy: classify the job, then the active approach

| Category | Consumer job and boundary | Useful comparators | Main uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus/productivity | Sustained work or study, mental clarity and task performance are central; not merely the word “focus” on an energy label | Neutonic, Magic Mind Original/FREE, FOCUSfactor, C4 Smart Energy | Incremental benefit beyond caffeine; willingness to pay |
| Alertness/energy | Wakefulness, stimulation, workout or general pick-me-up | Monster, Red Bull, Bloom, Phorm; Odyssey overlaps focus | Crowded distribution and taste competition; high dose is not differentiation |
| Caffeine-free stimulation | Stimulant energy without caffeine as the named molecule | UPDATE/paraxanthine | “Caffeine-free” is not “non-stimulant”; EU use authorization unresolved |
| Calm/daytime reset | Less keyed-up feeling while remaining functional | Recess Mood, TRIP US Mindful Blend, VYBES Mood | Ingredient doses, acute vs repeated-use expectations |
| Social tonic | An adult drink and ritual for social participation or alcohol substitution | Hiyo | Sensory/occasion value versus unproven pharmacological “float” |
| Sleep/wind-down | Bedtime transition, sleep onset/quality or nighttime recovery | BRĒZ Dream, Sly DREAM; Blue Bear and Cheribundi contextual | Strong promises, missing doses, timing, national ingredient/medicine rules |
| Hydration/recovery | Fluid/electrolyte replacement in a defined exertion, heat or travel context | Gatorade Lower Sugar, Electrolit | Benefit over water for the intended consumer; full-bottle nutrition |
| Gut-health soda | Enjoyable soda replacement with fiber/gut positioning | Poppi, OLIPOP, Simply Pop | Fiber type/dose, tolerability, authorized claims and repeat purchase |
| Protein/recovery hybrid | Convenient protein in a refreshing liquid; optionally fiber or energy | Clean Simple Eats Clear Protein Soda, Beyond Immerse | Processing, sensory stability, economics and added complexity |
| CBD calm segment | CBD-containing but not THC-led; separate from CBD-free drinks above | Daytrip CBD, Recess Original; historical VYBES CBD | Demand, actual CBD/THC specification, efficacy and legal route |

Every category can contain more than one ingredient approach. Classifications reflect positioning, not established effects. For example, Red Bull also mentions concentration; that does not make its primary proposition a specialized productivity drink. Unknown cannabinoid status in a dataset is not a verified cannabinoid-free certification.

## What changed in 2025–2026

The consolidated table distinguishes **22 recent-event records**, **7 contextual comparators**, **1 announced/preorder product**, and **1 discontinued predecessor**. Recent events include new flavors and distribution expansion; they are not all new brands, material reformulations or proven sales successes. Poppi and OLIPOP are strategically informative mature comparators: a transaction or funding event is not exact-SKU traction. Their 2025 events do not reset their original launch dates.

- Neutonic originated in November 2023; Magic Mind Original in March 2020; Bloom Sparkling Energy in July 2024. Their recent relevance comes from US expansion or liquid-line traction, not 2025 brand launches. UPDATE's company formation, earliest commercial documentation and 2026 relaunch are distinct dates. [Focus/energy chronology](supporting/focus-energy.md).
- Hiyo's brand dates to May 2021; Pineapple Coconut is a 2025 flavor/retail event. Sly DREAM was already sold by May 2024, so its October 2026 Sprouts announcement is distribution expansion. BRĒZ Dream is a genuine 2025 hemp-free product extension. [Relaxation/sleep chronology](supporting/relax-sleep-cbd.md).
- VYBES explicitly announced its CBD exit in January 2025 and replaced that proposition with magnesium-based Mood. That is a material platform change and negative evidence for depending on CBD for retail access—not proof that consumers rejected CBD. [Founder exit announcement, 24 January 2025](https://milled.com/vybes/big-news-about-our-cbd-drinks-xRwezgyBS7YCKfNb).
- Simply Pop is a 2025 new line; Gatorade Lower Sugar a March 2026 line; Clean Simple Eats' carbonated protein drink launched in February 2025. OLIPOP's shelf-stable 6 g fiber format predates 2025. Beyond's current agave recipe differs from its January 2026 launch composition. [Secondary analysis](supporting/secondary-categories.md).
- Beyond Immerse Protein + Energy was announced on 15 October 2026, but its exact product page was marked **preorder**. Do not count it as fulfilled sales or borrow its base line's retail distribution. [Issuer announcement](https://beyondmeat.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/beyond-meatr-launches-beyond-immersetm-protein-energy-sparkling), [exact SKU](https://beyondplantprotein.com/products/beyond-immerse-protein-energy-cherry-berry).

## Demand, commercial success and efficacy are different evidence sets

**Demand signals.** IFIC's US 2025 survey found energy/less fatigue sought by 40% and digestive/gut health by 37%; the full report was published in January 2026, although fieldwork was in 2025. Its October 2026 release reports energy at 36%, with healthy aging taking the lead. The 2026 US sample was 3,005 adults aged 18–80, weighted separately from Canada. These are self-reported desired benefits across foods, drinks and nutrients, not purchases of functional cans or proof of Czech willingness to pay. [2025 report](https://ific.org/wp-content/uploads/2025-IFIC-Food-Health-Survey-Full-Report.pdf), [2026 results](https://ific.org/media/trying-harder-yet-feeling-worse-americans-self-reported-health-status-hits-a-record-low/), [2026 methods](https://ific.org/research/2026-ific-food-health-survey-a-focus-on-food-nutrition/).

CBD demand evidence is weaker than the broad wellness narrative suggests. A US study published in November 2024 found 58% of 2,010 respondents would **or might** try CBD beverages; that combines firm and tentative interest, predates this commercial window, and measures neither actual purchase nor Czech demand. It is not a CBD-drink sales forecast. [Staples, Journal of Wine Economics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-wine-economics/article/canning-cannabis-consumer-preferences-for-cbd-and-thcinfused-beverages/4343A6856F99D7C186836D32ADBD810A).

**Commercial signals.** Bloom's reported 35 million first-year cans concern its energy line, which is more relevant than its broader supplement business. Monster's earnings commentary specifically identifies Ultra Blue Hawaiian as a fast-selling product, but gives no SKU units. Other strong-looking signals—Hiyo financing, Neutonic store expansion, Recess/VYBES rankings and the Poppi transaction—have different meanings and limitations. None establishes Czech product-market fit. The row-level evidence and attribution appear in the [landscape](landscape.md) and [workstream notes](README.md).

Hiyo's supplied Fitt article reports $20.8 million funding, a **projection** of more than one million cases in 2026, and expansion toward 13,000-plus doors. It does not report audited achieved annual cases or store-level sell-through. Its broad functional-beverage market headline is not used as the size of social tonics or this project's addressable market. [Fitt Insider, 5 October 2026](https://insider.fitt.co/hiyo-raises-20-8m-to-scale-social-tonics/).

**Efficacy.** Caffeine is a better-supported acute alertness rationale than a proprietary multi-herb “brain” blend. A 45-person trial of C4S reported selected cognitive improvements versus placebo, but it cannot isolate added nootropics from caffeine. A separate 2026 study of a C4 drink against a 200 mg caffeine-matched control found no between-group sustained-attention advantage; it is not necessarily the same C4 Smart Energy SKU and is not a direct replication. Neither establishes real-world productivity or every energy drink's effects. [C4S trial, online 2023/issue 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37186677/), [C4 comparison, 3 August 2026](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/282BA962234A78ECE314F5CAD201E85D/S2048679026101220a.pdf/consumption_of_caffeinecontaining_beverages_improves_sustained_attention_and_alertness_but_has_no_effect_on_strength_or_static_muscular_endurance.pdf).

The [evidence memo](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md) compares study doses, delivery, duration and populations with RTDs. Examples: chronic ashwagandha capsule results do not establish an immediate social effect; lion's mane trials are small and mixed; a CBD sleep pilot at 150 mg sublingually does not validate a low-dose can. Disclosed ingredient presence is not proof of an effective dose. No validated onset/duration was found for most exact SKUs; brand timing statements are retained as claims.

## Seven observed patterns and their limits

1. **Positioning separates occasions within overlapping formulations.** Neutonic emphasizes focused work, Hiyo social participation, and bedtime products an evening routine. Caffeine and botanical ingredients appear across several occasions. The reviewed evidence does not establish how Czech consumers interpret these distinctions or what premium they associate with them.
2. **Caffeine levels and dose disclosure vary substantially.** The sample includes 55 mg shots, 85 mg mushroom drinks and 200–222 mg energy cans, alongside products declaring zero caffeine. UPDATE contains paraxanthine despite its caffeine-free positioning. Many botanical amounts remain undisclosed. EU caffeine labeling depends on concentration as well as the applicable beverage category. [Evidence/regulatory memo](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md).
3. **Fruit flavors recur across functional categories.** Citrus, tropical and berry flavors occur in energy, mood and gut-health products; floral, chamomile and vanilla cues appear in evening products. This is a pattern within a purposive sample, not a measured flavor-share estimate or evidence of Czech taste preferences.
4. **Calm positioning exists independently of CBD.** VYBES' pivot, Recess Mood, TRIP's US formula and Hiyo represent distinct non-CBD approaches. Daytrip and Recess also illustrate separate CBD and CBD-free ranges. US TRIP Mindful Blend and regional CBD products have different compositions and availability. [Variant analysis](supporting/relax-sleep-cbd.md).
5. **Format and storage conditions accompany different product propositions.** Bottled shots are associated with compact daily routines, while sparkling cans span energy, social and gut-health occasions. OLIPOP's shelf-stable 6 g and refrigerated 9 g fiber versions differ in composition. The reviewed retail prices do not establish manufacturing costs or margins. [Secondary analysis](supporting/secondary-categories.md).
6. **Formula and packaging changes complicate comparisons over time.** VYBES changed its active ingredient proposition; Beyond changed fiber and sweetening composition; TRIP's current formula differs from older material; Neutonic's PR conflicts with its SKU page. C4's 2026 design refresh explicitly retained its formula. These are different kinds of change, not interchangeable evidence of innovation or growth. [Focus notes](supporting/focus-energy.md), [secondary notes](supporting/secondary-categories.md).
7. **Commercial promises frequently exceed what product-specific evidence establishes.** Bedtime positioning does not demonstrate improved sleep; a few grams of mixed fiber do not automatically meet a specific EU inulin claim's conditions; electrolyte content does not establish superior office-day hydration. Ingredient studies, brand claims and finished-drink effects remain separate evidence categories. [EU/evidence assessment](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md).

## EU/Czech context and evidence gaps

EFSA's 2026 provisional CBD level concerns qualifying supplements and does not authorize drinks. Czech enforcement involving CBD foods is documented. Supplier application validation, UK availability, THC crop thresholds and US retail listings do not establish authorization for the intended food use. The CBD assessment separately covers demand, effects, sourcing, safety, legality and claims. [EU/Czech and CBD assessment](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md).

Ingredient-use permission and advertising permission are distinct. The reviewed US citicoline, paraxanthine and botanical formulas are not evidence of EU authorization for the same ingredient identity, process and food category. Authorized nutrient claims concern specified normal functions and conditions; they do not by themselves establish immediate cognitive enhancement or anxiety treatment.

The research does not establish exact-SKU sales velocity and repeat purchase for most products, current inventory for some CBD listings, dates of several reformulations, standardized extract identities, independent whole-drink effects, Czech category demand or pricing, formula-specific destination-country compliance, or contract-packing costs and minimums. Canned/bottled functional coffee and tea remain a coverage gap in the selected recent-event sample; this does not imply absent competition.

Out-of-stock pages are not treated as discontinuations. No market size is inferred from brand counts, funding or this sample. No manufacturing quotes, interviews, taste tests or purchases were obtained. The [review record](supporting/independent-review.md) and [secondary review](supporting/secondary-independent-review.md) document evidence challenges and their resolutions.
