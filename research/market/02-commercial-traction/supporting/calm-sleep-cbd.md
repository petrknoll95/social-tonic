# Calm, social tonics, sleep and separate CBD commercial evidence

**Cut-off: 17 October 2026 (Europe/Prague). Status: completed delegated evidence workstream.** This memo supports the lead report; it does not select a formula or approve claims. Only finished cans and bottles qualify, including bottled shots. US 2025–2026 events are the main cohort; Goodrays and TRIP's UK drink-line comparison are explicitly secondary geographic comparators. Sources were accessed on the cut-off date. No outreach, purchases, tests or invented customer data.

Read alongside [source ledger](calm-sleep-cbd-sources.csv), [metric records](../data/calm-sleep-cbd-metrics.csv), the earlier [product landscape](../../01-us-product-landscape/supporting/relax-sleep-cbd.md) and [trend review](../../26-trends-and-industry-intelligence/report.md).

## Decision-useful result

Recess Mood and VYBES Mood have the clearest recent US *within-category* sales-ranking signals, but the public evidence is company-reported scanner data with no numeric sales velocity. Hiyo adds meaningful founder-reported case volume; its newer million-case figure is a forecast. There is no defensible public apples-to-apples ranking of US calm brands by annual RTD revenue, repeat purchase, retention or profit in the reviewed evidence.

For a separate CBD assessment, the strongest quantified drink-line evidence found is **Goodrays' UK retail sales**, not a US CBD winner. It demonstrates that some consumers buy CBD beverages in one market, without establishing the CBD ingredient's causal role or EU/Czech permissibility. US VYBES' withdrawal demonstrates route-to-market fragility; it does not establish consumer rejection.

For sleep/wind-down, newer exact-SKU products exist, but achieved liquid-line commercial leadership remains **unknown**. Mixed-format Dream Water/Som figures, Kin company accounts and BRĒZ's cannabinoid portfolio must not fill that gap.

## Strong financial documents that do not establish qualifying drink sales

**TRIP:** [audited FY28February2026 accounts, filed 6June2026](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/11834475/filing-history/MzUyNTE1NzY2OWFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0) cover beverages **and supplements**, without CBD or format segmentation (CS15–16). Net revenue was **£50,949,473** after **£15,987,236** rebates/discounts against gross revenue **£66,936,709**; prior net revenue was **£22,001,632**. Geographic net revenue was **£7,218,636 USA**, **£43,730,837 UK & Europe combined**; EU-only is unknown. Operating loss was **£1,329,272**. The **£133,481 after-tax profit** includes a **£1,243,150 tax credit** following a pretax loss. These are robust business-level facts, not can-line profitability. The separate company **$100m calendar2025 revenue claim** and **$200m 2026 forecast** have different periods and an unbridged revenue basis; do not present them as audited net revenue. Pages10 and25 (PDF13 and28) were visually checked after OCR.

**Kin:** the [26September2025 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1776889/000187285625000007/kinsocialformc.pdf), cross-checked against the [filing mirror](https://app.edgar.tools/filing/1776889/0001872856-25-000007/formc), reports company 2024 revenue **$10,208,139** versus **$7,409,211** and net loss **$5,313,706** versus **$3,292,913** (CS21–23). RTD cans and excluded mixing concentrates are not separated. Revenue growth alongside deeper losses is relevant business-risk context, but does not establish Lightwave demand or imply company failure/closure.

**Dream Water:** [FYJune2025 statements](https://www.delivrahealthbrands.com/_resources/financials/DHB-June-30-2025-FS.pdf?v=030412) explicitly include shots, gummies and powders in US operations; **C$10.686m** US revenue therefore fails liquid-only attribution (CS24). [December2025 MD&A](https://www.delivrahealthbrands.com/_resources/financials/DHB-December-31-2025-MDA.pdf?v=031509) reports all-format Dream Water US/Canada six-month revenue **C$5.055m**, e-commerce **+26%**, retail-channel revenue **−4%** (CS25). These are supplier channel revenues, not measured consumer sell-through. A [29May2026 release](https://www.delivrahealthbrands.com/_resources/news/nr-20260529.pdf) attributes a **C$1.803m quarterly USA revenue reduction** to interrupted outgoing Middle East shipments (CS26): booking geography is not necessarily end-consumer geography, and shipment interruptions do not establish consumer rejection.

**Recess attribution warning:** the [December2025 founder interview](https://www.bevnet.com/magazine/issue/2025/bevscape-the-latest-beverage-brand-news-43) assigns **95% of sales to Mood and mocktails together**, not Mood alone (CS04). Powder inclusion is not resolved. The [broader 2026 post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/drinkrecess_the-future-of-functional-beverages-isnt-activity-7476359953360871424-eXpi) claims number-one Mood Support dollar sales and over twice its closest US MULO competitor using a 52-week2025 source period (CS02); the exact SKU set is unavailable. Use the narrower sparkling-water-singles velocity observation for qualifying RTD evidence. Do not classify 95% of sales or the entire portfolio as CBD beverage success.

## Negative evidence and rival explanations

The [VYBES founder's 24January2025 email](https://milled.com/vybes/big-news-about-our-cbd-drinks-xRwezgyBS7YCKfNb) explicitly announces CBD exit and reports stores stopping carriage; it attributes the decision to state restrictions (CS11). [Mood followed on 29January](https://milled.com/vybes/introducing-mood-OoFc2tnqH2ymRbBw) (CS12). This is a documented withdrawal/pivot, rather than a speculative failed-brand list. Recess' CBD retrenchment and TRIP's non-CBD growth further support ingredient flexibility, although neither provides a controlled comparison holding taste, price, retailer access and promotion constant. No causal claim that magnesium outperforms CBD is warranted.

The strongest blueprint is **an identifiable occasion with a familiar drink format, then measurable repeat selling**: workday pause for Recess/VYBES; adult social participation for Hiyo; distinct bedtime occasion for the unproven sleep cohort. Flavor, price, packaging, retailer access, marketing and habit can explain purchases independently of pharmacological benefit. Hiyo's interview describes DTC customer geo-targeting around stores and an emphasis on rate of sale; that is a testable channel hypothesis, not measured causal proof. No standardized retention, repeat cohort, numeric units/store/week, or eligible-line profit was found for the ranked US calm/social drinks.

A Czech founder can therefore validate **CBD-free calm/social occasions alongside focus/energy**, not assume Hiyo is the sole blueprint. Outsourced, low-volume samples in final cans/bottles can be used later for blinded taste and paid-reorder testing, after safety/claims clearance. Test whether customers pay for flavor and occasion without promising a specific acute mental effect. A CBD extension needs separate demand, ingredient-quality, safety and legal gates; UK CBD sales cannot substitute for those gates. This workstream establishes commercial questions, not study-backed effects or viable EU claims; see the lead's updated EU/scientific assessment.
