# Preliminary naming screen

**Checked:** 17 October 2026 · Europe/Prague.

This is a creative discovery screen, not trademark clearance. Public-web queries were submitted in batches; only the returned material was reviewed. Search location and personalisation were not controlled. No systematic trademark database, social-handle or native-language screen was completed. The purpose was to identify conspicuous existing uses and avoid treating a constructed word as automatically new to the world.

## What remains unresolved

The final names require exact and similar-mark assessment for the actual goods and territories, meaningful native-language checks and consumer testing. Sparse web results do not pass those gates. Use the source report's [evaluation protocol](../../brand/research/2026-10-17_11-07-23_beverage-naming/supporting/evaluation-and-testing.md), with name-only and matched-pack tests, before investing in a final identity.
