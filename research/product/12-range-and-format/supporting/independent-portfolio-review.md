## Checks passed on the reviewed draft

- **Format filter:** the nine products in the report table are named finished cans or bottles; the 11-record dataset adds Recess and Odyssey. Excluded forms are not used as revenue proxies. The dated US beverage-line evidence remains distinct from generic brand performance.
- **Bloom:** $268.6m is correctly identified as first-year **Sparkling Energy line retail sales** reported 10 June 2026. The report does not call it Strawberry Watermelon sales, all Bloom revenue or calendar-2025 sales. The price/nutrition reference is an exact qualifying 12oz can within a mixed pack.
- **Pure Leaf:** 28 April 2026, two flavours, 69mg caffeine, zero sugar/calories and 12oz sparkling format match the manufacturer announcement. The report explicitly leaves new-line sales and finished-product effect unresolved.
- **Magic Mind:** the 2oz FREE bottle qualifies. April 2025 launch history and current offer are distinguished. $148.50/30 = $4.95 is correct for the observed one-time selection; it is not described as a measured willingness to pay or subscription-retention result.
- **VYBES nutrition:** current 12oz, 3g sugar, 25 calories and 300mg **elemental** magnesium match the current page. The report avoids copying this dose and separates old 14oz packaging and a scoped US Natural Expanded ranking from absolute sales.
- **SLY:** the daypart range is treated as a visible commercial architecture, with sales and combined-use safety unknown. Melatonin is not made a prerequisite for the proposed evening product.
- **Product brief:** 2.5g sugars/100ml gives 6.25g/250ml. Sugar alone could contribute 25kcal at that upper bound, so the stated potential conflict with other energy-bearing ingredients is appropriate. These are tagged development targets, not measured sensory preference or nutrition-claim clearance.
- **Recommendation:** no claim is made that one SKU universally maximizes demand. Daytime versus evening remains a choice to test, a two-formula range is conditional, and focus/energy are not split merely to increase range size. Subscription availability is not presented as retention.
- **URL-ledger check:** apart from the two incorrect benchmark links and unregistered Daytrip link above, all other external links in the reviewed lead report matched a URL in one of the current supporting source CSVs. Matching a ledger is a provenance check, not independent verification of every legal/scientific claim.

## Review limits

This was a targeted integration and attribution review. It was not a fresh audit of every original source, a systematic clinical review, legal advice, package testing, or validation of supplier economics. Safety and mathematical review belong to the complementary reviewers. All issue resolutions should be recorded in the lead's publication checks before publishing.
