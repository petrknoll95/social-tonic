## Decision-relevant findings to preserve

1. **Supply gap is already disproven in generic Czech focus/calm.** CANS+ Performance 330 ml (about 42 mg caffeine; dmCZK 39.50 snapshot), Vilgain Energy&Focus 330 ml (106 mg; CZK 45; marketed as supplement), CANS+Balance 330 ml (theanine/chamomile/magnesium; 24-packCZK 43.71/can) and Magnesia PlusFocus/Antistress 700 ml (CZK 27.90 promotional/reference 35.90) already cover adjacent vocabulary. Need a defined underserved occasion/customer/route, not “first European functional drink.” Retail legality and clinical superiority are not established by listing.
2. **New social and functional spaces should remain separate.** An adult social drink can earn taste, participation and multi-serving value without an efficacy claim. A focus or sleep product centrally depends on a benefit, preparation/dose, claim route and foreseeable intake. Private evening ritual, hosting/dinner and bedtime should not be pooled as one demand estimate.
3. **Paid demand is the central missing evidence.** No proposed EU concept has validated willingness to pay or repeat. The 39 public voices are a discovery input, not a segment-size survey; 1–3social evenings, 2–5workdays and other proposed frequencies are assumptions. Subscription availability does not demonstrate paid retention.
4. **Current substitutes are strong counterevidence.** Local packaged coffee/tea, ordinary energy, mineral water, beer 0.0, adult soda/mocktails and home/free habits compete. French Tourtel/PolishLech/CzechBirell prices show an affordable incumbent option; dietary-supplement buyers may prefer dry formats even though primary product must be liquid.
5. **Science-to-claims and preparation transfer require new verification.** Existing memos explicitly avoid importing US blends, compound grams as elemental mineral dose, capsule data as finished-can evidence or novel-food opinions as authorisation. The new report should make a claim-independent proposition explicit where possible.
6. **Outsourcing is plausible, quote economics unknown.** Public manufacturer pages establish leads and range boundaries. They do not establish formulation acceptance, capacity, exact MOQ per flavour, landed unit cost, QA/stability cost or commercial shelf life. Topic 12's 22 CZKbase unit cost is not a quotation; model fixed allowances may be lower than the published development fee alone.

## Contradictions, obsolete versions and unresolved coverage

- **Version/stock contradictions, not proof of failure:** Dutch TRIP old/new ingredients; French TRIP15/20 mg CBD mismatch; UK10/15 mg and Goodrays dose/size transitions; BrainMax 375→250 mg and 330→250 ml aggregator/title mismatch; VYBES14oz→12oz; Neutonic OrangeSunrise label differs from October Walmart announcement. The current physical-market label is unresolved for affected candidates.
- **Sensory facts are not validated preferences:** declared flavor is not tasting; body/aftertaste suggestions remain hypotheses. Nupreme 0versus 4.1 g sugars/100 ml source conflict remains a physical-label check. Ingredient dosage from nutrient label cannot reverse-engineer preparation mass.
- **Planning models cannot be averaged:** topic 06 euro net receipts/costs and topic 12/22 CZKcosts use distinct horizons/channel/pack purposes. Each assumes costs and repeat. Reproduction validates formula bookkeeping only.
- **Regulatory chronology:** older public guidance may predate judgments; 09is concurrent; new legal work must use latest acts and exact country/category/preparation. Prior live-register claim audit is stronger than a 2013cached list but is not a legal opinion for a final pack.
- **Geographic gaps:** current competition scan already covers selected 6EUcountries, but no systematic 27-country screen; no comparable paid-repeat metrics across countries. France has supply but not topic 06 economics. Spain/Italy/Nordics/Ireland and other markets deserve broad screening, not automatic dismissal.
- **Channel gaps:** no wholesaler quotation, manufacturer RFQ, secured listing, on-trade depletion data, returns/expiry record, shipping tariff or negotiated margin. A chain webpage is not active nationwide distribution.
- **Opportunity-specific gaps:** six-market social-serving price menus, alcohol moderation occasion data by customer segment, functional shot substitution/paid cadence, country supplement-versus-beverage classification, multi-serving active exposure, evidence of actual repeat at required premium and a route to initial cold availability.
