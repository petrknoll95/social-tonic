## Reuse, gaps and contradictions

- Topics 01 and 02 distinguish US events from original launch dates, line sales from company sales, and claims from effects. Topic 02's newer commercial analysis adds usable beverage-line evidence; no new whole-brand sales estimates are needed here.
- Topic 04 establishes real Czech focus/calm competitors; it rules out a generic empty-market claim. Topic 05's reviews/discussions reveal possible sweetener, taste and portion objections, but are self-selected and not representative sensory data.
- No reviewed repository study establishes a Czech optimum for sugar, acidity, carbonation or bitter botanicals. Availability/familiarity is not preference. Neither an aperitif direction for productivity nor sparkling format for every occasion follows from prior research.
- Neutonic US SKU/press formula disagreement, flavour-specific Vilgain formulas, VYBES bottle-size/formula history and historical magnesium labels must not be silently reconciled. Use the exact current label/batch at fieldwork; unknown stays unknown.
- CBD UK/US sale and Czech online listings are not EU authorization. Demand, finished-product effect, safety and lawful use/claims are separate.

## Evidence rules

Research cut-off/access date: 2026-10-17, Europe/Prague. Product discovery and recommendations accept only finished liquid cans/bottles, including verified RTD tea/coffee and bottled shots. No pouches or preparation-dependent consumer formats. Manufacturing ingredients and scientific interventions may differ in form; explicitly limit transferability. US event relevance is separated from older/local contextual controls. THC/intoxicating products are excluded from the intended launch.

Use direct product/manufacturer, government/regulator and primary research sources where possible; dated commercial reporting is attributed. Preserve publication date separately from access date; undated pages use `unknown` and any data-window date stays in the claim. Source registers record geography, evidence type, supported claim and limitations. Ingredient/label claims do not establish outcomes; studies of isolated materials do not establish an RTD's efficacy or taste. Brand sensory descriptions are not researcher tasting notes.

The final documents label **published evidence**, **brand/label claims**, **planning hypotheses** and **unknowns**. All numerical test settings, sample sizes and decision thresholds are proposed planning assumptions unless explicitly sourced. These are not predicted consumer results. No national stereotype, supplier quote, sales figure, interview or tasting result is invented.
