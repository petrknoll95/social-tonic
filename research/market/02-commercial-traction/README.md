# Commercial traction in functional RTD beverages

**Date / cut-off:** 17 October 2026, Europe/Prague. **Status:** completed targeted research synthesis; parallel workstreams integrated and independently reviewed. This is documentary due diligence, not completed market validation or formula clearance.

**Scope:** US functional non-alcoholic beverages launched, materially reformulated or gaining meaningful traction in 2025–2026, with older contextual leaders and separate Czech/EU/UK evidence. Focus, productivity, concentration and energy lead; CBD-free calm/social, sleep and CBD are assessed separately. Gut-health, hydration and recovery are secondary. Only finished liquids in **cans or bottles**, including verified RTD shots and coffee/tea, qualify. No consumer preparation formats or THC-led launch propositions. CBD is not a prerequisite for the main product.

The report distinguishes consumer retail value from company revenue, annual from cumulative figures, shipments from purchases, achieved from forecast results, and US from global/UK/EU geography. Strong evidence for a whole company can be unusable for a particular drink. No clean universal ranking by sales is claimed.
