# 40 more grounded beverage names, including single words

**Created 2026-10-17T20:22:16+02:00 · Europe/Prague**

This round continues the direction the user preferred: familiar English, a small creative twist, and a plausible connection to choosing or enjoying a drink. There are **16 single-word names and 24 phrases**, with no repeats from previous final lists. Eight single-word options are ordinary mood or manner words; the remainder add concrete drinking imagery or readable English constructions.

## The mood we are naming

My proposed centre is **ease and warmth, with brightness and conviviality**. It is the feeling of enjoying a good drink in a moment that already matters. The user's follow-up about mood is useful: an identity should express that feeling before we start explaining ingredients or constructing a brand story.

These ten words are **creative vocabulary, not additional screened name candidates**:

| Word | What it contributes |
|---|---|
| Ease | Comfort without pressure or ceremony. |
| Warmth | A friendly, human welcome. |
| Belonging | Being included in the occasion. |
| Conviviality | The pleasure of food, drink and company. |
| Lightness | Refreshment and an unburdened tone. |
| Presence | Enjoying the moment already happening. |
| Delight | A small, genuine pleasure in the taste. |
| Savour | Taking time to enjoy the drink itself. |
| Bright | Fresh, optimistic and lively. |
| Unhurried | Time to stay, talk and finish the glass. |

**Ease, warmth and savouring** are the clearest anchors for the current research. For a more sociable interpretation, bring belonging and conviviality forward; for a daytime break, bring lightness and presence forward. This is a proposed identity, not a claim that a formula changes mood. We have not selected one launch occasion or validated customer preference.

## The candidates I would develop first

**Pleasantly** has the closest fit to quiet enjoyment: familiar English, a friendly voice, and room for flavour to supply the excitement. **Unfussy** is more direct and confident. **Unrushed** gives the occasion a clear point of view. **Warmly** and **Affable** express welcome, though both need interpretation testing with a chilled drink and Czech audience.

Among the phrases, **Save a Seat** offers the clearest social scene; **Fine by Me** has an easygoing voice; **No Big Plans** has the strongest relaxed-evening identity. These are editorial priorities, not consumer rankings. Existing uses and search limitations are visible in [screening.md](screening.md).

For initial pack tests, a conditional example could be `PLEASANTLY / Peach / Tea drink` or `SAVE A SEAT / Grapefruit / Sparkling soft drink`. These are illustrative product descriptions, not selected formulas. Use the actual recipe, caffeine information and a localised beverage descriptor in any real execution.

## Research connection

- The [naming research](../../brand/research/2026-10-17_11-07-23_beverage-naming/report.md) recommends a distinctive identity beside a clear drink descriptor. This round continues the ordinary-word personality of **innocent**, the occasion logic of **Recess**, and the shared-drinking ritual of **TÖST**. It does not copy their spelling or imagery. The report records established evidence for innocent and emerging traction for Recess/TÖST; it does not isolate the commercial effect of their names.
- The [customer work](../../research/customers/05-segments-and-occasions/report.md) separates work breaks, meals and flexible social participation. That supports exploring time, welcome and companionship while keeping the actual occasions distinct.
- The [sensory research](../../research/product/13-flavour-and-sensory/report.md) puts enjoyment of a recognisable drink before an elaborate functional story. Leaflet, Peelside and Daymint are narrower options, conditional on a supporting recipe.
- The [ingredient research](../2026-10-17_10-56-22_claim-led-ingredient-research/report.md) leaves strong stimulant-free effect promises unresolved. Mood is therefore expressed through identity and occasion, without claims of improved focus, disinhibition or physiological relaxation.

Compare the names on the same specified drink, price and layout, before explaining their stories. Ask what the name sounds like, how it is spelled from hearing, what drink it suggests and what effect people expect. The [existing evaluation protocol](../../brand/research/2026-10-17_11-07-23_beverage-naming/supporting/evaluation-and-testing.md) remains the next step. This is a creative round with an initial screen, not consumer validation or trademark clearance.
