# Netherlands and France; separate UK and Swiss references

Research snapshot: 17 October 2026. Status: completed desk research, with physical labels, local stock and sell-through unresolved where stated. [Dataset](../data/western-europe-competitors.csv) contains 5 Dutch, 6 French, 2 UK and 2 Swiss exact canned/bottled liquid records. [Source register](western-europe-sources.csv) records dates, geography and limitations. The UK and Switzerland are references outside the EU shortlist. They do not establish EU distribution or permission.

This work reuses the distinction between commercial evidence, ingredient evidence and formula clearance from the [earlier EU/Czech memo](../../01-us-product-landscape/supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md). The [current regulatory update](regulatory-and-evidence-update.md) governs the integrated assessment. No product was bought, no person contacted, and no formulation effectiveness tested.

## What the evidence supports

The Netherlands is useful for comparing how a small premium-functional offer reaches both grocery and specialist drink retail. France adds local botanical brands, a large mainstream alcohol-free substitute, and unusually measurable 2025 fermented-drink traction with an important alcohol-content exclusion. These are research-country choices, not a ranked launch recommendation. Retail pages establish that a product is offered in a channel, while sales, repeat purchase and brand recognition remain separate questions.

Prices below are one container, not a pharmacologically standardized serving. Pack division is explicit in the dataset; delivery and deposits are excluded unless stated. They are observed offers, not an average market price. Carrefour stock/pricing varies by store and delivery mode. No address-specific checkout was tested. Unknown recognition means no local consumer-awareness study was found; it does not mean nobody knows the brand.

## Country implications and unresolved questions

**Netherlands.** Grocery distribution of a matcha-energy RTD and fermented/premium refreshments means a new productivity drink competes across tea/coffee-coded energy, premium soda and ordinary energy, not only with social tonics. PerfectTed's Dutch recipe has 0.9% cherry while the inspected UK page has 1%; therefore the UK's 80 mg caffeine is not silently copied into the Dutch row. [Albert Heijn](https://www.ah.nl/producten/product/wi614047/perfect-ted-matcha-energy-very-cherry), [Tesco UK](https://www.tesco.com/shop/en-GB/products/330745884), accessed 2026-10-17.

TRIP illustrates why exact labels matter. Wonderful's Dutch portfolio describes magnesium with lion's mane, ashwagandha and theanine; the current UK Blood Orange page lists different previous/reformulated botanical recipes. Both identify a Mindful drink without CBD in the ingredient list, whereas Drankerij's generic brand page describes all TRIP as CBD drinks. The matrix gives precedence to exact product/range evidence and flags the unresolved Dutch batch recipe. A formula copied from a retailer description would be unreliable. [Dutch distributor](https://wonderfuldrinks.nl/portfolio/trip), [UK exact product](https://drink-trip.com/products/mindful-blood-orange), [conflicting generic retailer page](https://drankerij.nl/trip/), all accessed 2026-10-17.

The identifiable distribution pattern is mainstream refreshment plus a specialist calm offer; the commercial gap is unmeasured. A workday-focused, CBD-free offer with a clear caffeine quantity could be tested with office workers who already buy these drinks. Claims of an unmet need or insufficient shelf choice require Dutch buyer interviews and store-level velocity, not this small sample. No reliably stocked, exact CBD beverage with resolved packaging was confirmed in this Dutch workstream. Azarius's sold-out Zen listing conflicts with other 60 ml packaging references, so it was excluded rather than counted as a domestic success. [WE42](https://www.azarius.nl/cbd-shop/cbd-eetbaar/cbd-drank-zen-cbd).

**France.** Symples establishes that energy and calm can coexist under a botanical beverage brand, with locally recognizable herbs and different occasions. It does not establish that these drinks improve concentration or sleep. Its €2.91 DTC bottle is substantially above the observed €1.25 energy can and €0.65 Tourtel multipack bottle, but different channels, packaging and pack commitments prevent a like-for-like willingness-to-pay conclusion. [Symples energy](https://shop.symples.fr/collections/frontpage/products/potion-bio-energisante-12-x-33cl), [Symples relax](https://shop.symples.fr/collections/frontpage/products/potion-bio-relaxante-12-x-33cl), accessed 2026-10-17.

The useful hypothesis is a taste-led botanical work break, potentially with separate caffeinated and caffeine-free versions. Buyers must establish whether shelf placement belongs with energy, premium soft drinks, refrigerated drinks or supplements; a website's existence does not answer that question. Tourtel gives stronger evidence of an established substitute than the CBD listings. Kronenbourg's December 2025 portfolio results are not assigned to Tourtel alone. [WE24](https://www.kronenbourg.com/newsroom/cp-dry-january-2026/).

### Excluded French fermented boundary: Ciao Kombucha

Ciao Pêche is a finished 330 ml bottle listed at €2.89 at Carrefour, and its May 2025 French launch has unusually useful retail-panel reporting. Rayon Boissons reported NielsenIQ first-month peach sales of €156,000 and range sales of €788,000 on 17 June 2025; its 3 October 2025 follow-up put range sales at €2.6m over the first three launch months in French hyper/supermarkets. These are measured launch-period sales, not current run-rate, margin or repeat. [Retail listing](https://www.carrefour.fr/p/kombucha-saveur-peche-ciao-3770037280023), [June panel report](https://www.rayon-boissons.com/boissons-sans-alcool-et-eaux/ciao-kombucha-fait-le-buzz), [October panel report](https://www.rayon-boissons.com/chiffres-du-marche/exclu-decouvrez-le-top-10-des-meilleurs-lancements-de-l-annee).

However, its own FAQ says **0.1–1.2% alcohol**. It is excluded from the core matrix and recommendations for a strictly nonalcoholic launch. French “sans alcool” presentation is not proof of Czech eligibility or a verified ≤0.5% SKU. The data show commercial interest in that French fermented proposition, not validation of a compliant Czech nonalcoholic or gut-effect drink. A replacement French kombucha with resolved ≤0.5% batch specification was not established in this workstream. [Manufacturing FAQ](https://www.ciaokombucha.com/fabrication), accessed 2026-10-17.

## CBD: demand, formulation and law remain separate

France's Agriculture Ministry stated on 20 May 2026 that CBD-added foods remain unauthorized novel foods and announced reinforced controls. Traditional hemp ingredients are a separate question. TRIP's unavailable listing and Chilled's ambiguous stock cannot be treated as legal, dependable distribution; neither proves zero customer demand. The Dutch NVWA separately explains authorization requirements for novel foods. [French statement](https://agriculture.gouv.fr/node/110883), [NVWA guidance](https://www.nvwa.nl/onderwerpen/voedselveiligheid/nieuwe-voedingsmiddelen-op-de-markt-brengen).

No inspected competitor provides a sufficiently verified sourcing package for a launch decision: ingredient authorization identity, batch CBD/THC assay, contaminants, emulsifier specifications, finished-drink stability and dose uniformity remain unknown. Supplier marketing such as “water soluble” would not establish bioavailability or finished-drink efficacy. The observed French 15/20 mg inconsistency and UK shift to a 10 mg labelled version demonstrate why a serving specification must be tied to the exact market and batch. This work does not validate CBD effects or recommend a CBD dose. The primary concept remains CBD-free; the optional extension must independently pass the [regulatory/evidence assessment](regulatory-and-evidence-update.md).

## Separate non-EU reference appendix

**United Kingdom.** Neutonic Tropical Ice is a 330 ml canned productivity drink with 120 mg caffeine, listed by Ocado at £2.75 and Sainsbury's at £2.85. Brand DTC is £29.99 for 12, or £2.4992 per can, excluding delivery; the subscription price is separate. Its supplement presentation and botanical blend are commercial references, not EU formulation clearance. No inspected source establishes exact-SKU sales. [Ocado](https://www.ocado.com/products/neutonic-natural-energy-productivity-drink-tropical-ice/679023011), [Sainsbury's](https://www.sainsburys.co.uk/groceries/product/neutonic-330ml-tropical-ice-food-supplement-productivity-drink), [brand](https://www.neutonic.com/products/tropical-ice-12pk), accessed 2026-10-17.

ASDA lists TRIP Peach Ginger in four 250 ml cans containing **10 mg CBD and 60 mg magnesium per can**, observed at promotional £4.50 versus displayed regular £6.47. Do not merge this with French or older UK 15 mg products. England/Wales guidance updated 28 August 2026 still described CBD foods as unauthorized; September board recommendations to ministers concern specific applications and are not equivalent to an enacted authorization. The public list is an enforcement tool, not an endorsement. These rules cannot be extended to the EU or all UK jurisdictions. [ASDA](https://www.asda.com/groceries/product/health-wellbeing/trip-peach-ginger-4-x-250ml/7411936), [FSA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cannabidiol-cbd-guidance/cannabidiol-cbd-guidance), [September paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/food-standards-agency-september-2026-board-meeting/cbd-novel-food-applications-recommendations-to-ministers-on-first-authorisations), [public list](https://data.food.gov.uk/cbd-products/).

**Switzerland.** FOCUSWATER Active pineapple/mango is a 500 ml PET vitamin-water bottle, CHF12.80 for six. Its FAQ says the guarana amount is not stimulating; the brand name cannot justify classifying Active as a high-caffeine focus drink. Rivella reported double-digit FOCUSWATER range growth in 2025 and a CHF16m production-line investment on 24 February 2026. This is better range-level momentum evidence, but neither group turnover nor litres are assigned to this SKU or an EU market. [Active](https://focuswater.ch/produkt/active/), [FAQ](https://focuswater.ch/faq/), [Rivella announcement](https://rivella-group.com/fr/news/groupe-rivella-un-exercice-2025-fructueux-et-un-changement-a-la-direction/).

Hempfy Bitter Lime qualifies by its 250 ml glass bottle only; it is sold out on the product page. The CHF3.70 homepage offer lists hemp without quantified CBD, so it is a hemp social reference, not a confirmed CBD-dose competitor. Exclude the brand's pouches, gum and alcoholic products. Swiss federal guidance treats cannabinoid extracts under novel-food authorization rules; a Swiss listing does not establish Swiss compliance or EU permission. [Product](https://hempfy.com/products/hempfy-bitter-lime-drink-glass-bottle-250-ml), [homepage](https://hempfy.com/), [BLV guidance](https://www.blv.admin.ch/de/cannabis-cannabidiol-lebensmittel).

## What must be resolved before using this to choose a launch

1. Obtain SKU-level, country-specific retail velocity, distribution, promotions and repeat data. The missing data cannot be replaced by global brand revenue, number of web listings or social followers.
2. Interview grocery, convenience, office/café and specialist buyers about shelf placement and acceptable price. No verified hospitality footprint is claimed here; menu listings and wholesaler portfolios alone would not establish sales.
3. Check local physical labels for Dutch PerfectTed caffeine, Dutch TRIP formulation, fermentation ABV over shelf life, and any CBD dose. This is necessary before a formula comparison or procurement decision.
4. In customer interviews, distinguish alertness, sustained work, afternoon refreshment, evening ritual and sleep intent. Caffeinated coffee/tea and supplement habits inform substitution questions, but powders/capsules are not RTD product benchmarks in the dataset.
5. Test the identified workday and botanical-pause hypotheses at low cost only after the integrated Czech assessment identifies a plausible customer and channel. No saturation, demand size or optimal launch-country conclusion follows from this purposive sample.
