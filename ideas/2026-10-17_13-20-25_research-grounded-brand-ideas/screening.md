# Naming screen — 17 October 2026

This is an initial public-web conflict screen and a small domain registry check, completed on **17 October 2026, Europe/Prague**. It is not trademark clearance or proof of worldwide originality. Names already used outside beverages are identified rather than presented as newly unused words. The creative proposals concern this project's beverage identity.

## Scope

Searched the quoted candidates with drink, beverage, brand or soda terms; added coffee searches for selected candidates. Inspected the returned titles, excerpts and page text. Common words produced substantial irrelevant results. A search with no clear beverage match is weak negative evidence, particularly for Along, Between and Nearby.

The screen did not systematically cover Czech phonetic variants, all intended EU languages, trademark classes, company registers or social handles. Third-party trademark/directory results are discovery flags, not verified legal conclusions. No name, domain or mark was purchased or filed.

## Quick domain registry checks

Checked the exact `.com` domains for the three leading directions using Verisign RDAP. Each endpoint returned **HTTP 200** and a **domain object matching the requested name**. Therefore each exact `.com` was already registered at check time. No conclusion is drawn about purchase price, willingness to sell, trademark rights or website activity.

| Ovelo | ovelo.com | Registered domain object: OVELO.COM | [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/ovelo.com) |

Earlier exploratory checks also returned matching registered objects for unfold.com and nelo.com. Nelo was subsequently removed for the beverage-name similarity above. No `.cz` domains, modified domains or social handles were checked in this round.

The initial Python HTTPS request failed certificate verification; the subsequent system `curl` requests succeeded with normal certificate verification and their JSON bodies were parsed to confirm the domain objects. No failed request is counted as availability evidence.
