# Claims and messaging matrix

**17 October 2026 · EU and Czechia. Provisional research decisions, not label clearance.** Every row assesses the stated context, not a universally lawful or unlawful word. All proposed consumer formats are finished liquids in cans/bottles. Numerical conditions are eligibility screens, not recommended formulae or efficacy promises. [Report](report.md) · [CSV](data/claims-matrix.csv) · [Source ledger](sources.csv) · [Final review checklist](supporting/final-legal-review-checklist.md).

## Shared conditions

- **G — general health-claim gate:** lawful/safe ingredients and food category first; truthful and substantiated whole presentation; match the authorised meaning; required varied/balanced diet and healthy lifestyle statement, consumption quantity/pattern, applicable avoidance/excess warnings; nutrition/substance declaration. Assess label, ads, e-shop, imagery, name and adopted testimonials together. Regulation1924/2006 Arts1–10,12 and Regulation1169/2011 Art7 apply. A named nutrient claim does not grant a whole-blend or stronger effect claim.
- **N — nutrient gate:** at least source of the named nutrient, usually ≥7.5% NRV/100 ml beverage **or** ≥15%NRV in a package genuinely containing one portion. High requires twice source. Check permitted form, finished-product available amount and shelf life, reasonable consumption and safety. [Reproducible thresholds](data/nutrient-thresholds.csv).
- **C — caffeine gate:** above150mg/L from all sources, covered beverages need the AnnexIII high-caffeine warning in the same field of vision as the name and caffeine in mg/100ml. The exception requires a coffee/tea-based drink with coffee/tea in its name. Factual per-can quantity is not a health claim permission.
- **B — botanical gate:** exact species/part/extract/claim ID and endpoint, current status, scientific support, food-use legality and the correct Article28 transition. Psychological/behavioural claims require the relevant28(6) history; on-hold alone is insufficient. [Worked records and legal routes](supporting/botanicals-and-czech-enforcement.md).
- **H — exercise hydration gate:**80–350kcal/L carbohydrate,≥75% energy from high-glycaemic carbohydrates,460–1150mg sodium/L and200–330mOsm/kg water. Exercise context is part of the claim.

**Risk labels:** conditional = recognised route but this drink is not cleared; lower = lower-risk factual/sensory/occasion proposal, still context-dependent; high/not cleared = no adequate route established or excessive meaning; non-authorised = specific negative decision. Legal eligibility and scientific efficacy are separate columns in the CSV and below.
