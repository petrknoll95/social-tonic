# Rejected, high-concern and unresolved candidates

**17 October 2026.** These are screening decisions for an ordinary, casually consumed EU/Czech soda. “Reject” is an analytical product decision unless an explicit prohibition is identified. “Unresolved” does not mean prohibited. No entry provides a preparation method, consumption formula or safe maximum.

## CBD: optional variant, separate from every core direction

No CBD route is selected. Current reviewed EFSA opinions concern tightly specified **supplement** formulations and conditions; an opinion is neither Commission market authorisation nor evidence that an aqueous soda produces noticeable calm. There is no established applicable ordinary-beverage permission or authorised calm/focus claim in this review. A low amount compatible with a narrow safety assessment must not be advertised as pharmacologically equivalent to much larger exposures in clinical research. [EU42](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/provisional-safe-level-cannabidiol-novel-food); [EU43](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154)

A lawful future variant would still need exact source/process identity, carrier/emulsion and particle-size assessment, CBD/THC and contaminant assays, dose uniformity and shelf-life testing in the actual pack. A published acidic-beverage nanoemulsion study observed THC formation in its tested systems; that result is a formulation-specific concern, not a statement that all CBD drinks behave identically. No method for cannabinoid conversion is proposed. The finding does not establish the behaviour of every CBD emulsion. [FM28](https://doi.org/10.1089/can.2024.0064)

CBD brand familiarity and an alcohol-alternative narrative do not establish incremental Czech demand. The appropriate commercial question is whether a legally permissible CBD variant adds preference or repeat purchase beyond an otherwise matched CBD-free drink. No participants were recruited and no ingestion experiment was run.

## Unresolved questions that apply to all retained actives

1. **What material is being purchased?** Latin name/compound, part, strain, process, solvent, carrier, marker assay, enantiomer and batch impurities as appropriate. A trade name or extraction ratio alone is insufficient.
2. **What exact food category is allowed?** Ordinary drink, supplement and food for special medical purposes are not interchangeable. The Novel Food catalogue is informative and nonbinding; decisions, authorisations and history must match.
3. **What is actually experienced?** Ingredient exposure, statistical task signal, meaningful self-reported benefit and ordinary-life usefulness are different outcomes. Null results and adverse responses remain part of the decision.
4. **What happens across an occasion?** Several cans, mixed SKUs and background foods/supplements/medicines can change exposure. Small single-dose trials do not establish long-term safety or safe driving.
5. **What survives manufacture and storage?** Intended process, acidity, oxygen, light, carbonation, packaging and shelf life remain untested for this startup. A capsule result or fresh laboratory drink is not industrial validation.
6. **What may be communicated?** Exact authorised wording/conditions or a demonstrable transitional route are separate from effect testing. A successful finished-product study does not itself confer EU health-claim permission.
