# Proposed intelligence watchlist

**As of 17 October 2026. Proposal only: no subscriptions, paid services, scheduled tasks or automations were created.**

The objective is to detect changes that could alter a Czech/EU concept, formula or route to market. More mentions of an existing announcement should not trigger a trend upgrade.

## Cadence and decision triggers

| Cadence | What to inspect | Record or escalate when |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly, one focused scan | Fitt, BevNET/Brewbound, Food Dive, FoodNavigator/BeverageDaily, selected retailer news and Czech trade headlines | A qualifying can/bottle launches; a confirmed listing expands or disappears; a company reports closure, reformulation, sale or funding. Classify announcement versus executed event. |
| Monthly | Exact SKU labels, can/bottle size, price and promotion, multipacks, local retailer assortment; current company claims | A dose/ingredient, size, storage requirement or intended occasion changes. Keep dated old/new evidence rather than silently replacing a formula. |
| Monthly while selecting a concept | Czech competitors and consumer alternatives: Rohlik, grocery, convenience, cafés, gyms and hospitality menus | A directly comparable local offer appears, or a realistic price/placement hypothesis changes. Menu visibility is not sell-through. |
| Quarterly / when results publish | Celsius, PepsiCo, Simply Good Foods, Nichols and relevant public filings; available scanner summaries | Defined revenue, retail takeaway, volume, velocity, margin or distribution changes. Preserve metric, geography, period and included formats. |
| Quarterly | Human studies and regulatory developments; exact pending applications | Evidence matches the candidate drink's dose and outcome, or authorization/claim status changes. An application, opinion and authorization are different events. |
| Before any formula, label or country decision | Commission Union list/conditions, claims register, destination-country guidance, caffeine labeling, supplier technical documentation | A candidate ingredient or claim fails the intended use. Recheck rather than relying on this October snapshot. |
| Immediately after a material signal | Original source and one independent or authoritative check | Closure, product recall, official authorization, meaningful retail withdrawal, or a result that could reverse the investment thesis. Do not contact companies as part of this proposed desk-monitoring workflow. |

Keep routine collection modest: a weekly scan, a monthly consolidated update and a quarterly decision review. Review new retail experiments after roughly three and six months, when disclosed evidence may begin to distinguish initial stocking from sustained demand. No growth rate is implied by those intervals.

## Search terms

Search both benefit and occasion; add `can`, `bottle`, `RTD`, `ready to drink` or `shot`. Remove powders only after checking whether an article also covers a qualifying liquid line.

- Focus: `mental focus drink`, `concentration beverage`, `productivity drink`, `mental clarity tea`, `cognitive performance RTD`, `nootropic coffee`, `theanine canned tea`, `caffeine free focus shot`, `paraxanthine drink`.
- Energy: `yerba mate launch`, `moderate caffeine can`, `energy drink retail sales velocity`, `energy brand delisting`, `coffee protein bottle`.
- Calm/social: `calm sparkling water`, `relaxation beverage`, `after work drink`, `social tonic`, `mood support drink`, `magnesium drink`, `CBD free`, `THC free 0mg`, `alcohol alternative`.
- Sleep: `sleep drink can`, `wind down beverage`, `bedtime drink bottle`, `melatonin beverage`, `non melatonin sleep drink`.
- Adjacencies: `prebiotic soda`, `fiber iced tea`, `protein soda`, `sparkling protein`, `electrolyte bottle lower sugar`, `functional shots retail`.
- Negative/commercial: pair brand with `closure`, `wind down`, `reformulation`, `recall`, `delisted`, `discontinued`, `retail takeaway`, `repeat purchase`, `sales per store`, `gross margin`, `earnings`, `acquisition`, `impairment`.
- Czech: `funkční nápoje`, `nápoj soustředění`, `nápoj koncentrace`, `nápoj proti únavě`, `nápoj relaxace`, `nápoj na spaní`, `nápoj hořčík`, `ledový čaj kofein`, `nízkocukerné nápoje`, `nové potraviny CBD`, `zdravotní tvrzení nápoje`.
- EU/regulatory: `site:food.ec.europa.eu novel food [exact ingredient]`, `site:efsa.europa.eu [exact ingredient]`, `site:szpi.gov.cz [ingredient or claim]`, and the EU register's exact proposed claim. Search the actual chemical/botanical identity, not just a brand ingredient trademark.

## Update rules

1. Preserve publication date, event date, original launch and observation date separately.
2. Verify a finished can/bottle SKU before adding a product. Mark future launches, preorder and unavailable offers explicitly.
3. Link the original release or filing; group syndicated copies and coordinated retailer launches.
4. Identify whose number it is and what it measures. Record denominator, period, geography and included formats; otherwise mark them unknown.
5. Promote a narrative from isolated launch to recurring pattern only with independent companies. Promote it toward commercial validation only with defined sales or sustained operating evidence.
6. Record counterevidence and keep failed or changed hypotheses visible. Add a short “what decision changes?” note; discard updates that merely repeat promotion.
