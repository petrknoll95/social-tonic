# US sensory benchmark rationale

**17 October 2026 · US market · Desk research only; no product was tasted.**

Use this selection to define contrasts to measure, not to import a US winning flavour or functional claim. Eight core references have 2025–2026 launches, material platform changes or relevant line activity. A still tea is an explicitly older/contextual control; one coffee investigation stays outside the benchmark dataset until resolved. The [dataset](../data/us-sensory-benchmarks.csv) records exact packaging, sweeteners, materials, questions, event scope and unknowns. The [source register](us-benchmark-sources.csv) holds dates and limitations for US01–US27.

## Practical tasting order

**Start with workday focus.** Use the lead protocol's citrus and peach-tea prototypes with a locally available eligible focus RTD and Nupreme HiMatcha BIO 330 ml still tea bottle after its physical-label reconciliation (see the local evidence memo). Halfday is an optional US still tea control after its own version check. If affordable lawful access to current US cans is available later, use **one** of Pure Leaf/Neutonic first according to the question being answered; Bloom is an optional stronger energy-market reference. There is no need to import all nine products for a low-cost first study. This plan makes no purchase or import-legality assumption.

Run separate later category flights: social fruit/botanical versus VYBES/Hiyo; bedtime versus Dream; hydration versus Gatorade; gut soda versus Simply Pop only if that occasion survives interviews. Compare each against its own occasion and measure. A high score for a bedtime drink is not directly a win over a workday product. Use full serving and second-serving interest testing on separate days or within the approved ingredient exposure limit; a second serving should never be mandatory merely to obtain a score. Follow the [tasting protocol](tasting-protocol.md).

These flights are proposed research, not evidence of effects. Ingredient taste risks in the dataset are questions to detect, not assertions that a named brand is bitter, earthy, metallic, astringent or unpleasant. Sweetener identity does not establish perceived intensity or aftertaste in a finished formulation.

## Contradictions that matter before tasting

1. **Neutonic:** Orange Sunrise declares rhodiola and citicoline; the Walmart release instead lists alpha-GPC and names other flavours. Do not merge these into one formula or claim Orange is the new Walmart product. The Orange webpage's detailed list also omits caffeine although its headline/dose panel says 120 mg. Require actual-can photographs and lot code before exposure calculations. The brand recommends one can a day.
2. **Pure Leaf:** its [retailer label](https://www.shoprite.com/sm/delivery/rsid/643/product/pure-leaf-sparkling-real-brewed-tea-peach-12-fl-oz-id-00098000101421) describes research using 50 mg caffeine plus 100 mg theanine. That statement does not establish either actual can dose; the manufacturer launch states 69 mg caffeine. Actual theanine dose remains unknown.
3. **VYBES:** current 12 oz bottle and older 14 oz launch references must remain separate. A current magnesium drink is not evidence about current CBD-beverage taste or demand. The [24 January 2025 CBD exit email](https://milled.com/vybes/big-news-about-our-cbd-drinks-xRwezgyBS7YCKfNb) describes retail/regulatory constraints, not demonstrated consumer rejection.
4. **Halfday:** current manufacturer lists fructan fiber; [Whole Foods](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/grocery/product/12-fz-b0f79ymc6k) lists acacia within the blend. Record actual version; retailer-declared 45 mg caffeine needs physical-label confirmation. Keep this sample on hold until resolved.
5. **Chameleon reserve, not a ready tasting control:** [26 February 2026 launch](https://bevnet.com/pr/2026/02/26/chameleon-coffee-launches-usda-organic-functional-flash-brew-readytodrink-cans-with-erewhon) names 8 oz Flat White/Latte functional cans with theanine and zinc; a [current Classic Latte listing](https://www.ubereats.com/product/b/6fc4be3c-87d1-5b03-8372-511b665945ba) omits both and calls the base cold-brew. Flat White and Classic Latte are different SKUs. This memo retains the unresolved investigation outside the benchmark dataset; do not treat it as a verified matched formula, buy it on this basis, or infer label quantities from another coffee. A validated still coffee can could replace a tea control in a separate coffee-user study.

For optional CBD work, keep a separate concept and technical/legal track. No CBD product was promoted into this recent-event tasting shortlist on unsupported traction. The [commercial CBD/transfer assessment](../../../market/02-commercial-traction/supporting/eu-czech-transfer.md) provides the launch gate; US sale is not EU permission. This avoids confounding CBD presence with flavour, branding and expected relaxation before demand and legality are established.
