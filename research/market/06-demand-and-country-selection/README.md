# 06 · Demand and country selection

**Date:** 17 October 2026 (Europe/Prague). **Status:** completed desk research and independent review; proposed concept, demand, cost and channel access remain unvalidated. **Path mapping:** exact requested topic folder; no equivalent existing topic found.

Compares Czechia with **Austria, Germany, Poland and the Netherlands** for an early-stage Czech founder. Finished ready-to-drink liquid cans/bottles only, including eligible coffee/tea and bottled shots. The primary launch hypothesis is CBD-free; CBD is assessed separately, with no assumed EU beverage route or THC-led/intoxicating launch scope.

**Recommendation:** test a CBD-free workday alertness/focus occasion in Czechia first. This narrow category lead is conditional on a reason to switch and paid repeat; it is not an approved health claim or validated market. Provisional expansion: one German region, Austria, Poland, Netherlands, with German/Austrian order decided by access and contribution tests. Base Czech scenario is about 9,811 cans/€10,792 net sales; it does not fund a full business under the illustrated cost assumptions.
