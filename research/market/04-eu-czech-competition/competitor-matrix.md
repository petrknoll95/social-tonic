# Country-by-country competitor matrix

**Observed 17 October 2026.** 52 exact-SKU country records: 48 across six EU countries and four separate UK/Swiss reference records. This is a purposive competitor sample, not an exhaustive market census. The same brand in different countries is a separate offer, not an additional unique brand. All consumer formats below are finished cans or bottles.

Read with the [report](report.md), [source register](sources.csv), [data dictionary](data/README.md) and [full CSV](data/competitor-matrix.csv). The CSV preserves detailed distribution and scale evidence, qualitative maturity, recognition, original evidence wording and source IDs.

Prices are local-currency observations **per entire can/bottle**, excluding explicitly identified refundable deposits and shipping; multipacks, promotions and unavailable catalogue offers are stated. A 700/750 ml bottle is a larger-volume control, not an equivalent portion to a small can. Unknown prices are not inferred. Source links establish dated observations, not assured checkout or current stock.

**Evidence quality refers to the observation, never efficacy or sales.** High = exact product/offer well evidenced; medium = material variant, stock, formula or retrieval qualification; low = cross-border/announcement/menu-only support. Even high-quality unavailable evidence proves unavailability, not availability. All exact local SKU sales/velocity remain unknown; reported range/company proxies are named. **Measured country-specific brand recognition is unknown for all rows.** Established/emerging classifications concern the operator or range, not established success of a new SKU.

Ingredient quantities are label/retailer statements or explicit calculations, not assays. “None declared”, “not listed” and uncollected/unknown CBD values are not analytical zero; ordinary unknown cells do not imply CBD inclusion. Non-alcoholic beer and fermentation can have residual alcohol; see row notes and the [alcohol boundary](supporting/regulatory-and-evidence-update.md#packaging-and-alcohol-description-boundary). No listed formula or marketing claim is automatically a lawful EU launch formula.
