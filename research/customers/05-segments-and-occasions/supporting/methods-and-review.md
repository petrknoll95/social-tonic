## Collection and source handling

Search combined work/study, afternoon/caffeine, taste, price, regret/nonpurchase, evening, sleep, social, hydration and wellbeing terms; used Czech-language sources and primary official/brand research alongside public reviews. Negative and satisfied counterexamples were deliberately retained. This is a purposive search without a complete search-result census, preregistration or claim of saturation.

The 47 source records document access on 17 October 2026; publication dates are exact only where exposed. Unknown/relative dates are retained. Search-index dates and snippets are weaker than full dated pages and are identified by agents. Dynamic retailer pages can vary by access mode, promotion and location. Target Dream review text had indexed-access limitations; Pure Leaf retailer recheck was rate-limited, so issuer evidence supports that entry. No paywall was bypassed.

Thirty-nine coded observations are not unique participants or independent replications. Same-thread comments share selection context, and work/Czech streams use related Aktin review pages. Geography describes the source/community unless a study establishes residence. Public adult status is usually unknown. Retail purchase badges and review incentives are preserved without treating them as independent verification. No usernames, medical histories or full source copies are needed for conclusions; short paraphrases and source locators suffice.
