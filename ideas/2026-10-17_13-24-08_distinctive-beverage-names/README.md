# Twenty more distinctive beverage names

**17 October 2026 · Europe/Prague**

[Names and creative rationale](report.md) · [Initial conflict and domain screen](screening.md)

Leading creative directions: **Rindigo**, **Rattlefruit**, **Puckerbird**. Additional compact contenders: **Zindle** and **Fizoro**.

This is an additional round. None of the twenty names repeats a previous project candidate. The report uses established beverage brands as references for naming mechanisms, with no claim that their names caused commercial success.
