# Secondary categories: commercial traction and counterevidence

**Cutoff/access: 17 October 2026. Status: completed targeted documentary review.** US benchmarks for a Czech/EU founder; finished liquids in cans or bottles only. Read alongside the [metrics](../data/secondary-metrics.csv), [source register](secondary-sources.csv), [existing product/formula landscape](../../01-us-product-landscape/supporting/secondary-categories.md) and [prior trends report](../../26-trends-and-industry-intelligence/report.md). Sources below identify both publication date and the metric period; they are not interchangeable.

Poppi and OLIPOP provide the clearest demonstrated scale in gut-positioned soda. Electrolit offers a distinct hydration blueprint with recent retail growth, although audited standalone revenue is unavailable. Oikos Pro Drinks and Shots provide a measured protein/recovery comparator; Halfday is a smaller, promising adjacent tea example. New protein sodas and incumbent line launches remain unproven at exact-line level. None of these establishes Czech paid demand, clinical efficacy or affordable startup economics.

**Current counterweight to Poppi's 2025 success:** PepsiCo's **8 October 2026** 10-Q values the performance earnout at **USD12m at 5 September**, versus **USD278m at 27 December 2025**. The USD266m year-to-date decline concerns a modeled liability tied to revenue/margin forecasts and milestones through Q3 2027. It signals weaker earnout expectations; it is **not** a 96% sales decline, a brand impairment or proof of commercial failure. This is higher-quality evidence than an acquisition headline and must accompany the historical retail result. ## Claims that become wrong when repeated

**OLIPOP's near-USD500m:** Food Dive's 8 October 2026 interview says it achieved nearly USD500m in 2024, but the linked Bloomberg original is dated **24 May 2024** and describes a forecast. Therefore use the later founder-reported achieved range USD400–450m; 2025 and 2026 achieved revenue remain unknown. Its USD1bn is a goal. The prior topic-26 wording needs this additional source-chain qualification. [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/olipop-ceo-christian-patino-webb-interview-1-billion-sales-better-for-you-soda/831539/) (SC05); [forecast origin](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-05-24/olipop-soda-pitched-as-healthier-than-coke-pepsi-eyes-500-million-in-sales) (SC06).

**Poppi purchase price:** PepsiCo's USD1.95bn transaction, including anticipated tax benefits and separate contingent arrangements, is neither annual brand sales nor retail sales. It stays outside the traction ranking. [Issuer, 19 May 2025](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2025/pepsico-completes-acquisition-of-poppi-accelerating-strategic-portfolio-transformation) (SC26). The fresh USD745m retail result adds evidence absent from the previous research's acquisition-only comparison.

**Electrolit USD617m / USD727m:** these circulating figures lead to trade coverage that was not fully accessible. They are deliberately not promoted to accepted metrics. A readable primary KDP transcript establishes >30% growth, while its April 2026 transcript confirms continued share gains and qualitative velocity. No denominator is invented. [Q1 2026 call](https://filecache.investorroom.com/mr5ir_keurig_drpepper/506/Keurig%20Dr%20Pepper%20Inc._First_Quarter%202026%20Earnings_Transcript.pdf) (SC09); [paywalled reporting lead, 7 July 2026](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2026/mid-year-snapshot-promo-pricing-pushes-sports-drinks-ahead) (SC27).

**PRIME's purported 42% decline:** Tubefilter traces this to Darren Rovell's social post citing Circana. Its narrative mixes Hydration and Energy, and the raw category/channel cut is absent. ## Retrenchment and execution failures

**OWYN is a brand-level caution, not bottle-specific sales proof.** Its 355mL Smooth Vanilla Pro Elite bottle qualifies, but the same product page also sells excluded 330mL cartons. Whole-brand net sales fell 16.8% in fiscal Q2 2026; management cited weak velocity, including new distribution, and quality problems. Q3 brand net sales rose 3.6% while estimated retail takeaway fell 1.3%; nine-month takeaway was still +4.8%. Shipment recovery and consumer recovery are different. Financial Q3 ends 30 May; takeaway ends 31 May. None isolates bottles, and the corporate loss/impairment cannot all be assigned to this SKU. [Q2, 9 April](https://www.thesimplygoodfoodscompany.com/news-releases/news-release-details/simply-good-foods-company-reports-fiscal-second-quarter-2026-0) (SC14), [Q3, 9 July](https://www.thesimplygoodfoodscompany.com/news-releases/news-release-details/simply-good-foods-company-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2026) (SC15), [bottle versus carton](https://liveowyn.com/products/smooth-vanilla-pro-elite-protein-shake) (SC16).

**Unwell's wind-down is reported, with completion still prospective.** October trade reporting says beverages would continue through Halloween before cessation. The same article's statement that it was already shut down in August is inconsistent with continued production through Halloween; the August event date remains unresolved rather than being recast as a verified announcement date. Its Hydration + Focus bottle is verified at 16.9 fl oz and 75mg caffeine. Reasons and line-level sales are unknown, so no invented attribution to taste, women-focused marketing, caffeine or consumer rejection. [BevNET, 13 October](https://www.bevnet.com/magazine/issue/2026/bevscape-the-latest-beverage-brand-news-48/) (SC17), [brand FAQ](https://drinkunwell.com/pages/faq) (SC18).

## Why the blueprints may work, and what is transferable

**Taste-led nutritional substitution is the strongest secondary blueprint.** Poppi and OLIPOP attach a nutritional story to a familiar soda habit and flavors. The company-reported scale and independent growth ranking support actual purchasing, but do not isolate the causal role of fiber, packaging, social marketing, taste or distribution. A Czech founder can test a familiar flavor and acceptable price before promising a gut outcome. Current Strawberry Lemon and OLIPOP 6g shelf-stable pages verify qualifying cans; historic portfolio sales do not validate those current formulas separately. [Poppi SKU](https://www.drinkpoppi.com/products/strawberry-lemon) (SC21), [OLIPOP SKU](https://drinkolipop.com/products/strawberry-vanilla-6g-fiber) (SC22).

**Electrolit's blueprint is a specific hydration occasion plus route-to-market execution.** The KDP call attributes gains to distribution, velocity and innovation; it does not show a quantified repeat cohort. Scaling with a national distributor is not a low-cost startup baseline. For this founder, a narrow venue/occasion with measurable paid reorders is more transferable than a US national rollout. Electrolit's pharmaceutical heritage is positioning, not permission to make EU medical claims. Its historical size also exceeds a newly launched brand's starting point.

**Halfday shows an adjacent format hypothesis:** familiar iced tea plus fiber, using the same canned-liquid filter. A new refrigerated slim-can rollout to Target in July 2026 is distribution activity, not fresh sell-through. Its lower disclosed scale and missing annual/retention economics keep it separate from the soda leaders. [Release, 22 July](https://insider.fitt.co/press-release/halfday-iced-tea-lands-nationwide-at-target-with-exclusive-new-flavor/) (SC13).

**New nutritional hybrids are still a watchlist.** Simply Pop, Gatorade Lower Sugar, Clean Simple Eats Clear Protein Soda and Beyond Immerse have qualifying can/bottle records in the [prior verified dataset](../../01-us-product-landscape/data/secondary-categories.csv). This review found no sufficient exact-line revenue, repeat or margin disclosure to upgrade them. Beyond's 15 October Protein + Energy extension remained preorder in that same-day repository review and is not a proven sold product. No parent meat-business, powder or broad supplement sales are borrowed. Protein complexity, sweetness, texture, sediment and shelf-life require validation before these become an outsourced first-launch blueprint.

## Category maturity, missing information and next steps

Sports drinks already have mass distribution and a large established base. Circana-based reporting puts US sports drinks near USD11.8bn with +1.8% dollars but -0.2% volume for 52 weeks to 19 April 2026. A different 12-week Nielsen/Goldman cut to 13 June shows +8.5% dollars, with promotion discussed. These are different windows and universes, not a contradiction or proof every hydration startup wins. Do not import the report's mixed liquid-and-powder weight-control category as bottled protein TAM. [Beverage Industry, 14 July](https://www.bevindustry.com/articles/98521-2026-state-of-the-beverage-industry-sports-protein-drinks-serving-consumers-throughout-the-day) (SC11), [BevNET public excerpt](https://www.bevnet.com/content/2026-launchexplorer/sports-drinks) (SC28).

Exact retail sales per store per week, consumer cohort repeat, retention, promotion-free velocity and line profitability remain **unknown** for the accepted secondary leaders. No annual-sales/store-count division is used to fabricate store velocity. No US result is relabeled EU-only or Czech.

The cheap next validation is a paid-choice comparison of a few qualifying liquid competitors against a simple concept: one soda/tea occasion or a defined hydration occasion, realistic local prices, and a planned repeat interval. Track paid units, net revenue, variable contribution after fulfillment and trade spend, cohort repurchase, outlet reorders and wastage. Favor an existing outsourced can/bottle platform before adding protein or a novel ingredient. Test taste at intended shelf life, not just a fresh bench sample. Physiological benefit and lawful EU wording require separate ingredient/formula review; neither is established by sales. No interviews, purchases, supplier quotes or product tests were performed here.

## Recovery comparator added during evidence review

**Oikos Pro Drinks + Shots** closes a gap between newer unproven protein sodas and OWYN’s mixed-format negative context. The [primary Circana release, 21 May 2026](https://www.circana.com/post/circana-unveils-2025-new-product-pacesetters-as-top-innovations-drive-6-2-billion-in-year-one-sales) explicitly groups only Pro Drinks and Pro Shots at number five; [Food Business News, 10 June 2026](https://www.foodbusinessnews.net/articles/30478-beverages-dominate-in-circanas-2025-pacesetters) reports **USD91.6m first-year retail sales** (SC29–30). Its 2025-class traction is the qualifying recent event: [Danone’s 21 May 2024 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/danone-dairy-drinks-shake-up-your-routine-with-campaign-featuring-athletes-shacarri-richardson-jordan-chiles-and-kristie-mewis-302150821.html) dates initial US retail entry to May 2024 (SC33). The exact first-year measurement window remains unknown.

Exact-format checks: [Mango Pineapple Pro Drink](https://www.oikos.com/all-products/pro-yogurt-cultured-ultra-filtered-milk/product/mango-pineapple-drink/) is a **7 fl oz / 207mL bottle**; [Mango Pro Shot](https://www.oikos.com/all-products/pro-yogurt-cultured-ultra-filtered-milk/product/mango-shot/) is a **3.1 fl oz / 93mL bottle** (SC31–32). Both are ready-to-drink cultured dairy liquids. No yogurt-cup revenue is counted. Pro Drinks versus Shots sales are unseparated; later Oikos Fusion and separate Protein Shakes cannot inherit this result. The plausible blueprint is a familiar, portioned protein drink with a clear post-exercise or convenient-nutrition job. Danone’s brand, refrigeration and distribution advantages mean it is not a low-cost manufacturing template or proof of startup margins.
