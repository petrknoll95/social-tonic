# Dated US canned and bottled functional-drink landscape

**Cut-off and access date: 17 October 2026. Geography: United States unless a field explicitly says otherwise.**

31 purposively selected records: **22 recent-event records, 7 contextual comparators, 1 announced/preorder extension, and 1 discontinued predecessor**. Recent event does not necessarily mean a new brand or verified paid traction. Every benchmark is a finished liquid can or bottle; UPDATE's named variety pack is one retail SKU, not five products. Bottled shots qualify.

Commercial and formula tables are separated below so availability, consumer claims and established effects cannot be mistaken for one another. All benefit and timing wording is **brand positioning**, not an endorsement. “Sold” means offered for sale or supported by dated retail evidence; no purchase was made. Prices are USD per entire can/bottle, before tax/shipping unless noted. Unknown never means zero. Current stock, formula and price may vary. Nominal ml are used where verified; US fluid-ounce conversions are approximate.

The [full CSV](data/product-landscape.csv) adds detailed source IDs, pack-price basis, ingredient/serving caveats and uncertainties. Publication dates and limitations are in the [source register](sources.csv). See the [analysis and benchmark comparisons](report.md), [taxonomy](report.md#taxonomy-classify-the-job-then-the-active-approach), and [evidence assessment](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md).

## Interpretation limits

- Recess Original's less-than-0.3%-THC wording is not an assay showing zero THC per can. Daytrip's THC-free statement is a brand claim without an independently reviewed batch certificate. CBD rows are research context, not EU launch formulas.
- VYBES' CBD exit is documented; Red Bull stockouts, Electrolit limited-edition wording and an old live Recess page do not establish discontinuation.
- Population survey interest, company fundraising, distribution authorization, reported shipment volume, store velocity and clinical outcomes are separate evidence categories. No whole-brand supplement, powder or THC-line revenue is assigned to a qualifying RTD.
- Some brand-declared CBD-free entries rely on the ingredient list rather than analytical certification; absence of a listed ingredient is not a measured zero. Caffeine-free UPDATE contains paraxanthine.
- No exact-product efficacy is inferred from disclosed ingredient mass. See the [study-to-drink assessment](supporting/eu-czech-and-evidence.md) before interpreting any benefit claim.
