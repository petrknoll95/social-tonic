# Tasting scorecard / Degustační formulář

**Blank instrument, not results · 17 October 2026.** Pilot Czech wording before use. Use one anonymous participant ID, session, product code, sequence position and actual serving time/temperature per record. Consent and product suitability are handled separately. Do not show brand, function claims, price or the unblinded recipe key.

## Pass A: enjoyment before diagnostic prompts

**Taste only the coded sample in front of you. There are no right or wrong answers. You may stop at any time.**

*Ochutnejte prosím označený vzorek. Neexistují správné ani špatné odpovědi. Kdykoli můžete přestat.*

1. **How much do you like this drink overall? / Jak vám tento nápoj celkově chutná?**

   1 Extremely dislike / Vůbec mi nechutná; 2 Dislike very much / Velmi mi nechutná; 3 Dislike moderately / Spíše mi nechutná; 4 Dislike slightly / Trochu mi nechutná; 5 Neither like nor dislike / Ani mi chutná, ani nechutná; 6 Like slightly / Trochu mi chutná; 7 Like moderately / Spíše mi chutná; 8 Like very much / Velmi mi chutná; 9 Extremely like / Mimořádně mi chutná.

2. **What, if anything, stands out? / Co vás na tomto nápoji zaujalo, pokud vůbec něco?** Free text; do not prompt sweetness, bitterness or functionality.
3. **How much would you want to drink a full [actual volume] serving now? / Jak moc byste teď chtěl/a vypít celou porci [objem]?** 0 Not at all / Vůbec; 10 Very much / Velmi. This is desire, not a requirement or paid purchase.

After all products: **Which would you choose for [named occasion]? / Který byste si vybral/a pro [konkrétní příležitost]?** Code ___ / tied codes ___ / none ___ / uncertain ___. **Why? / Proč?** ___

Lock this page before Pass B. Do not revise earlier ratings.

## Pass B: what you perceive

Use fresh coded samples under the [protocol](tasting-protocol.md). For each selected attribute, **0 = not perceived; 10 = extremely strong**. Ask: *Jak silně vnímáte následující vlastnost? 0 = vůbec; 10 = mimořádně silně.*

| Attribute / Vlastnost | 0–10 or missing |
|---|---|
| Sweetness / Sladkost | ___ |
| Sourness / Kyselost | ___ |
| Bitterness / Hořkost | ___ |
| Aroma strength / Intenzita vůně | ___ |
| Body/thickness / Plnost, hutnost | ___ |
| Carbonation / Perlivost | ___ |

Only if relevant to the flight: saltiness, mineral impression, astringency/drying, earthy impression, coating or grittiness. Select at most three extra dimensions. No attribute is inherently a defect.

**Which descriptions fit? / Které popisy odpovídají vašemu vjemu?** Choose any from the short randomized flight-specific vocabulary; also offer “none,” “not sure,” and an open field. Aroma associations do not establish ingredients.

**Relative to your preference, is the level… / Vzhledem k tomu, co máte rád/a, je intenzita…**

For sweetness, sourness and carbonation separately: much too low / výrazně příliš nízká; somewhat too low / trochu příliš nízká; just right / tak akorát; somewhat too high / trochu příliš vysoká; much too high / výrazně příliš vysoká. Optional body item only when useful. Do not describe lower sugar, stillness or bitterness as better.

At **30 and 90 seconds after swallowing**, before rinse: **What remains? / Jaká chuť nebo pocit přetrvává?** ___; **How strong? / Jak silně?** 0–10 ___; **How much do you like that finish? / Jak příjemné je toto doznívání?** 1–9 ___ using the same liking scale.

After all sensory questions: **Did you recognize a product? / Poznal/a jste některý nápoj?** No / possible / yes; code and suspected identity ___. Do not reveal the answer.

## Full-serving and actual second-serving day

Record serving number 1 or 2, anonymous product code, actual can/bottle size, start temperature, time, occasion, food/activity context and departures from instructions. Serving 2 is normally on a separate matched day. Respect label restrictions and the protocol's exposure gate.

- Overall liking after first sips ___ / halfway if reached ___ / at stopping or end ___ (1–9).
- Amount consumed ___ ml; amount left ___ ml; duration ___ min; amount is measured / estimated (select).
- **If you stopped, what was the main reason? / Pokud jste přestal/a pít, co bylo hlavním důvodem?** Open answer first; then code taste, full/not thirsty, caffeine/ingredient limit, portion, interruption, temperature, other. Never code all leftovers as dislike.
- Aftertaste at 90 seconds: description ___, intensity 0–10 ___, liking 1–9 ___. Optional repeat diagnostic: drying or coating if raised in the pilot.
- **How well did the drink fit this occasion? / Jak dobře se nápoj hodil k této příležitosti?** 0 Not at all / Vůbec; 10 Extremely well / Výborně. **What makes you say that? / Proč?** ___
- **Would you choose this again on another similar occasion? / Vybral/a byste si tento nápoj znovu při podobné příležitosti?** Yes / maybe / no / uncertain, reason ___. This intention is kept separate from actual second-day consumption.
- If offered a permissible further serving: drink / water / no drink / other; reason ___. Record actual quantity separately. No pressure to drink; no reward for finishing.

For bedtime ask about taste/volume/ritual fit, not “Did it improve your sleep?” For work ask about drink fit, not “Did it boost focus?” For exercise ask about ease of drinking and sensory comfort, not physiological recovery. Any adverse experience prompts stopping and the organizer's incident procedure, not continued scoring.

## Optional labelled concept/price follow-up

Only after blind responses and analysis choices are locked, present truthful, legally reviewed factual concept information and one explicitly hypothetical price per whole can/bottle. Ask choice among this, usual option, water and no purchase; reason; expected purchase frequency. Record revealed versus blind condition. The questionnaire is not proof of paid demand, function, safety or lawful claims. CBD interest belongs in a separate concept exercise with accurate legal availability information, never covertly in the main samples.
