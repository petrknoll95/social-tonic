# Methods, repository evidence map and audit

**Folder created:** 2026-10-17T10:57:12+02:00, Europe/Prague (UTC+02:00). **Access/cut-off date:** 17 October 2026. This is a broad, targeted public-source investigation, not an exhaustive worldwide census, a systematic clinical review or completed commercial validation.

## Scope and rules

Primary: finished non-alcoholic sparkling liquids in cans/bottles where creatine is a central named purchase reason. Still drinks, creatine-led energy/recovery drinks, dairy/protein drinks and ready-to-drink shots are explicitly adjacent. Dry-cap activation bottles require mixing and are excluded from the qualifying finished-liquid category even when sellers call them RTD. Powders, gummies, sachets, concentrates and drops are substitute or historical context only. Creatine as an incidental part of a generic pre-workout stack does not qualify.

The matrix distinguishes current purchasable offers, announced/preorder, out-of-stock/unclear, historical and discontinued products. A functioning product page is evidence of an offer, not completed deliveries; an indexed retailer page is not proof of current store inventory. We did not place orders, supply addresses or test delivery checkout. Where a page and search cache differ, the fresh page has priority and the conflict is retained. An empty collection is not automatically a discontinued business.

Labels and facts were extracted from official product pages, label-image links and retailer panels where accessible. Formula identity, form, active-equivalent basis, carbonation and complete labels remain unknown where not disclosed. Ingredient order is never converted into exact quantities. A company logo on a store locator is weaker than a priced local SKU listing. A translated storefront or currency selector does not establish shipping to that country.

## Search approach

Global searches combine `creatine soda`, `sparkling creatine`, `creatine ready to drink`, `creatine water`, `creatine shot`, brand names and terms for launch, sales, revenue, retention, funding, stock, discontinued and stability. EU searches combine local creatine terms with can/bottle/drink/sparkling/stock: Czech `kreatin nápoj plechovka skladem`, German `Kreatin Getränk Dose`, Polish `napój kreatyna puszka`, French `boisson créatine gazeuse`, Dutch `creatine drank`, Finnish `kreatiini juoma`, Swedish `kreatin dryck` and Danish `kreatin dåse`. The [EU memo](eu-market-and-consumer.md) records the actual market coverage and further local-language queries; the [product memo](product-discovery.md) records global/history coverage.

Non-EU follow-up concentrated on the US, Canada, UK, Australia and UAE. Asia, Africa and Latin America did not receive systematic local-language retail screening. EU coverage also varies: Bulgaria/Hungary lacked dedicated searches, while several smaller countries received grouped English searches only. These gaps materially limit any claim to global completeness or national absence.

Scientific searches follow exact forms and study titles/DOIs, pH, temperature, creatinine, HPLC, shelf stability and human outcomes. Reviews are discovery maps; the decisive technical extractions distinguish original experiments from supplier figures, patents and brand claims. The [study register](../data/studies.csv) preserves matrix, conditions, design and transfer limits. Regulation was checked using EUR-Lex, Commission consultations, SZPI, Ministry of Agriculture and official Czech-law index sources; blocked interfaces and dated consolidated versions are disclosed.

## Evidence classes and calculation rules

- **Independently documented:** original external research, public authority/court record or genuinely independent evidence. This does not make a small study broadly generalisable.
- **Company-reported:** brand, founder, supplier or issuer assertions, including press releases carried on third-party sites and seller statements on a business marketplace.
- **Retailer-observed:** an offer, labelled composition, stated stock or shipping policy observed at the retailer; not verified physical availability or sales velocity.
- **Third-party estimate:** an estimate with its method and limitations; not relabelled actual sales. Unverifiable sales-estimation sites are not accepted as decisive evidence.
- **Anecdotal:** individual reviews and public discussions, including authenticity/selection limitations.
- **Unknown:** absent, inaccessible, ambiguous or not adequately supported; not zero and not failure.

Prices retain local currency and snapshot date. No exchange-rate comparison is implied. Price/serving = pack price ÷ containers. Price/disclosed gram = serving price ÷ disclosed grams, with compound/creatine/premix denominator stated. Unknown or mixed-blend active potency does not support a price per gram of active creatine. Tax, deposit, freight and subscription conditions are recorded separately where known. Full pack intake and retained shelf-life dose may differ from a nominal serving claim.

Company revenues are not product-specific unless explicitly isolated. A door count is not velocity; a reorder announcement is not a measured cohort; reviews are not repeat; sold out may mean supply limits. One company release repeated in five publications remains one evidentiary event. Each commercial row records period, geography, attribution and limitations or marks them unknown.

## Coverage and access limits

No proprietary scanner data, paid market reports, private accounts, retailer reorders or subscription cohorts were obtained. No companies or consumers were contacted; no product purchases, paid subscriptions, recruitment, consumption experiments, lab work or formula trials occurred. No independent current-batch end-of-life assay of the principal reviewed sodas was found in the retrieved evidence. This is a search result, not proof that private testing does not exist.

Stock and pricing can change; rendered pages sometimes contain conflicting old/new variants or inactive subscription controls. Some pages were available only through indexed extracts; exact access limitations appear in the source register. Some Eur-Lex HTML pages were robot-blocked while original PDFs/search-index text remained accessible; e-Sbírka's full interactive decree text did not render. These limitations are not filled with invented content.
