# Beverage naming research

**Created:** 2026-10-17T11:07:23+02:00
**Timezone:** Europe/Prague · UTC+02:00
**Research cut-off:** 17 October 2026
**Status:** completed desk research and internal independent review; no final name, consumer fieldwork or trademark clearance.

Evidence-grounded naming research for a Czech-founded nonalcoholic can/bottle beverage with possible EU expansion. Formula, positioning, audience and architecture remain open. The core is CBD-free; an optional CBD extension is a separate future decision. The purposive sample contains 48 names: 12 non-beverage consumer brands and 36 beverage brands, including adjacent architecture cases. Its 69 commercial/status observations and 144 source records do not constitute a representative market study or proof of naming-caused success.
