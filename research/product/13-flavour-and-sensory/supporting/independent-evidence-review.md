## Elements that pass this review

- Six briefs are hypotheses, not predictions. They explicitly allow still workday/tea/hydration/bedtime directions and reserve aperitif bitterness for an optional social arm.
- The portfolio does not presume Czech national taste. Personal familiarity, segment-specific rejection and full-container experience are explicitly measured.
- All proposed consumer formats are cans or bottles. Ingredient powders are kept as manufacturing inputs, not product benchmarks.
- The briefs maintain CBD-free core scope, separate caffeine choices, avoid assuming actives are tasteless and require material-specific review.
- The proposed small launch range requires incremental preference coverage and repeated enjoyment. It does not turn two high mean ratings into evidence that two flavours are needed.
- The bedtime brief recognizes process constraints and separates sensory occasion suitability from sleep efficacy.
- The draft lexicon distinguishes sourness from bubbles, bitterness from astringency, mineral impression from mineral assay, and refreshment from hydration benefit. Piloting Czech language is explicitly required.

## Integrated report assessment

The report correctly treats the Czech sweetener thesis as a tiny local exploratory panel and the Warsaw experiment as a different matrix; it does not select a convenient side of their apparent disagreement. Sour-liking heterogeneity is not converted into Czech market shares. Local product availability and botanical repertoire are not turned into national taste stereotypes. The Nupreme sugar conflict, Vilgain daily serving limit, BOHEMSCA formula discrepancy and Crodino's Polish-market scope remain visible.

The recommended sequence is coherent: two workday directions first, controlled descriptive and liking measures, matched refinement, then full-serving and another permitted serving. Social/food, activity and bedtime questions remain separate. A one-flavour starting proposal is framed as a learning recommendation, with incremental preference coverage needed for expansion. Neither sensory enjoyment nor stated purchase intent is substituted for efficacy or paid repeat. These conclusions pass this review.

No reason was found to predict a winner or replace local tasting with US flavour traction. ER05 is the remaining minor source-precision correction; the report's core recommendation and evidence distinctions are supported within the stated desk-research limits. Legal/technical details and US commercial-event claims are outside this review's independent-check scope and require the other specialist reviews.
