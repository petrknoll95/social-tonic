## Implications for copy testing

Test whether adults understand a work-break or evening-social occasion and prefer the taste/price before adding a physiological promise. Use competing cans/bottles in priced choice exercises; other habits can inform interviews but are not product benchmarks. Do not test public ads containing unavailable CBD products or unreviewed health claims. Private concept research is not blanket permission for otherwise unlawful commercial communications.

Useful comparison: a plain afternoon drink description versus the same description with a qualifying, ingredient-attributed normal-function claim. Ask participants what improvement and time-to-effect they infer. If they hear instant focus, freedom from stress or better sleep from a narrow nutrient claim, revise the surrounding name, imagery and copy. Comprehension research cannot authorize a claim or prove efficacy.
