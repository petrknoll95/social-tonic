## Evidence protocol

- Finished ready-to-drink cans/bottles only, including exact canned/bottled tea, coffee and bottled shots. No consumer powders, sachets, pouches, tablets, capsules, gummies, gels, drops or dilution products enter product comparisons or proposed formats. Coffee preparation habits are contextual survey evidence only.
- Separate measured purchasing, stated interest, observed listings, issuer commercial claims, scientific effects, analyst estimates and hypotheses. A financing event, door count or forecast is not achieved sell-through. No parent-brand revenue proves a liquid SKU's sales.
- Original sources and local-language reporting are preferred; unavailable underlying scanner/survey data remain a limitation. Reopen decision-changing existing sources and preserve conflicts rather than blend incompatible series.
- No broad energy, supplement, alcohol-free or wellness market is used as the addressable market for novel focus/calm drinks. Population is a ceiling/context, never a conversion estimate.
- Positive EFSA opinions, Union-list authorization, national classification and permitted claims are separate gates. CBD is independently evaluated and excluded from the primary launch scenario; THC-led/intoxicating drinks are out of scope.
- No contacts, purchases, paid panels or actual tests were performed. Proposed budgets are analyst spending caps, not supplier quotes. Published manufacturing terms remain vendor claims, not an accepted offer.

## Model design

Compare Czechia with four EU alternatives: Austria and Germany for proximity and higher household material consumption; Poland for regional scale and a different price/retail structure; Netherlands for premium beverage context and a contrasting compact market. Slovakia would be a sensible inexpensive language-adjacent follow-up, but adds less contrast to this five-country comparison; France adds another language/route before neighboring markets are tested. Neither is empirically rejected.

Model the first 12 months after a compliant pilot becomes sellable, one country and one lead category at a time. A reachable adult pool is a **hypothetical channel delivery plan**, not an existing audience, signed distribution or measured willingness to buy. Distinguish unique reached adults, occasion-qualified adults, first paid purchasers, repeat purchasers, purchase frequency and units/order. All pools overlap across categories; rows are alternative launches and must not be added.

Weights are decision preferences for a small Czech founder, not estimated causal parameters. Country operating factors account for 45 points; category fit accounts for 55. The scoring workbook is transparent CSV plus a deterministic Python model. Run sensitivity for conversion, repeat, pack size, frequency, price, channel reach, costs and weights. Report uncertainty instead of presenting a point forecast as a market fact.
