## Three independent gates

1. **Ingredient:** Exact species/part, strain, chemical form, solvent, process, purity, standardisation and intended category must match either a demonstrated pre-15May1997 significant EU food-use history or applicable Union-list authorisation. Food-supplement-only history does not establish ordinary-beverage use. The Novel Food catalogue is explicitly nonbinding and non-exhaustive; not-novel status is not a general safety certificate. If unclear, the operator should use the Article4 competent-authority consultation procedure. [EU01](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food_en); [EU02](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food/consultation-process-novel-food-status_en); [EU03](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food/authorisations/union-list-novel-foods_en); [EU04](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food/novel-food-status-catalogue_en)
2. **Safety of the finished drink:** General food safety applies even where no ingredient-specific statutory ceiling exists. Exposure must cover foreseeable multiple cans, other SKUs, diet, supplements, medicines and vulnerable groups. Absence of an EFSA UL is **not** an unlimited-safe-intake finding. [EU05](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2002/178/oj); [EU20](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2023.8006); [EU21](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/efsa_rep/blobserver_assets/ndatolerableuil.pdf); [EU22](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/caffeine); [EU23](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2009.935)
3. **Communication:** A study or permissible ingredient does not authorise a health claim. Claims, product names, icons, selected testimonials, websites and ads must satisfy Regulation1924/2006 and misleading-information rules. A nutrient normal-function claim cannot justify a stronger promise for the entire blend. [EU06](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/2014-12-13); [EU07](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/432/2025-08-20/eng); [EU14](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/clanek/voditka-k-problematice-zdravotnich-a-vyzivovych-tvrzeni)

A supplier specification, GRAS assessment, US product label, EU competitor listing, supplement notification, EMA herbal monograph or EFSA positive opinion cannot substitute for all three gates. Notifications are not case-by-case approvals. EU member-state medicinal and botanical differences remain relevant even when an ingredient is non-novel.

## Authorised nutrient wording and thresholds

Claims source: Regulation432/2012, associated conditions and Regulation1169/2011 AnnexXIII; statutory text controls over paraphrases. For most relevant vitamin/mineral function claims, food must qualify as a **source**. In beverages, significant amounts are generally7.5% of NRV per100ml, **or15% NRV per portion if the package contains only one portion**. High-source requires twice source amounts. These are eligibility conditions, not recommended inclusion levels or proof that a healthy replete person will notice an effect. Shelf-life content, analytical tolerances and normal consumption must support the claim. [EU07](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/432/2025-08-20/eng); [EU19](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/clanek/obohacene-potraviny.aspx); [EU31](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/2025-04-01/eng)

## Caffeine and modifiers: separate legal questions

Caffeine has a favourable EFSA scientific assessment for certain alertness/attention outcomes, but that is **not an EU-authorised claim**. Prior topic09 retrieved the live Commission register and found no authorised caffeine alertness/concentration claim; the2016 proposed claims were vetoed, and the40–75mg application was formally refused. Any proposed transitional reliance needs a specific legal assessment, not reuse of an old75mg draft condition. [EU32](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20160701IPR34496/parliament-vetoes-energy-drink-alertness-claims); [EU33](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/1411/oj)

“Caffeine-free” is a compositional representation requiring reliable analytical/supply-chain substantiation and national labelling review. It does not mean **stimulant-free**, universally sleep-neutral, pregnancy-safe or non-interacting. Tea, mate, guarana and cocoa ingredients need caffeine accounting. Chemically caffeine-free paraxanthine is nevertheless an active caffeine metabolite; whether “caffeine-free” alone creates a misleading stimulant-free expectation requires consumer-interpretation and legal review. This is an analytical interpretation of misleading-information principles, not an ingredient-specific ruling. [EU06](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/2014-12-13); [EU14](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/clanek/voditka-k-problematice-zdravotnich-a-vyzivovych-tvrzeni)

“Natural” concerns the real material/process and overall impression. A chemically identical fermentation/synthetic amino acid is not automatically a tea extract. Czech Decree417/2016§2 addresses potentially misleading natural descriptions with narrowly justified exceptions. Natural flavouring under Regulation1334/2008 has its own definitions; it cannot be extrapolated to the whole drink. [EU11](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/1482/oj); [EU34](https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/legislativa/vap87546-131)

“Lasting”, “no crash”, “without jitters”, “non-drowsy” and “unwind without impairment” add duration, safety or physiological-performance implications. They require support and a permissible claim route; caffeine absence alone is insufficient. No ingredient reviewed here establishes driving compatibility. Claims of correcting anxiety, ADHD, insomnia or depression introduce disease/medicinal boundaries. [EU06](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1924/2014-12-13); [EU14](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/clanek/voditka-k-problematice-zdravotnich-a-vyzivovych-tvrzeni)

## Botanical claims after C-386/23

The30Apr2025 judgment confirms Article10 still applies while botanical assessment is suspended. It is not a blanket botanical ban or blanket on-hold licence. Psychological/behavioural claims and general wellbeing references in the case require relevant Article28(6) transitional eligibility. Article28(5) concerns Article13(1)(a) body-function claims and must not be used by casually relabelling a psychological claim. [EU15](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A62023CJ0386); [EU16](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2025-04/cp250057en.pdf)

Article28(6) requires qualifying lawful use before19Jan2007 and the applicable historical evaluation/notification/application route; the unevaluated route required an application before19Jan2008. The founder need not personally have operated in2007, but the claim's eligibility must be documented. A pending-list ID, EFSA receipt date or supplier claim sheet alone is not that dossier. The older Czech guidance predates the judgment and must be read subject to it. No exact botanical claim route was cleared in this workstream. [EU14](https://www.szpi.gov.cz/clanek/voditka-k-problematice-zdravotnich-a-vyzivovych-tvrzeni); [EU15](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A62023CJ0386); [EU16](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2025-04/cp250057en.pdf); [EU17](https://ec.europa.eu/food/food-feed-portal/backend/claims/files/claims_pending.pdf)

## High-concern/rejected register inputs

- **Yohimbe bark/preparations:** EU2019/650 moved them to prohibited AnnexIII PartA. Do not describe this as merely uncertain novel-food status; isolated yohimbine is not a loophole or proposed soda route. [EU35](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/650/oj)
- **Kratom/extracts:** Czech rules effective12Nov2025 place them in a licensed psychomodulatory regime. The mandatory notice explicitly says the product is not a food [EU44](https://mzd.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Sb_2025_448_PZZ.pdf). They are unsuitable for this ordinary soda project; the Sept2026 governmental proposal for tighter rules is a proposal, not proof every proposed measure is already in force. [EU36](https://mzd.gov.cz/seznam-psychomodulacnich-latek-byl-vyhlasen-ve-sbirce-zakonu/)
- **Phenibut:** synthetic pharmacological agent with reported intoxication, tolerance/escalation and withdrawal; WHO notes evidence limitations and confounding. Exclude from casual drinks without converting adverse case doses into instructions. [EU37](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/phenibut-critical-review-report)
- **Kava:** EMA did not adopt an EU herbal monograph; safety and preparation issues, including hepatic concerns, need specialist consideration. Exclude from this casual-soda shortlist rather than claim an unverified universal EU statutory ban. [EU38](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/herbal-report/final-assessment-report-piper-methysticum-g-forst-rhizoma_en.pdf)
- **Synephrine-enriched extracts:** ANSES flags cardiovascular-risk/combination concerns, particularly caffeine and vulnerable groups. Bitter-orange flavour is not identical to a synephrine-enriched pharmacological preparation. Exclude stimulant-enhancement direction. [EU39](https://www.anses.fr/en/system/files/NUT2012sa0200EN.pdf)
- **Melatonin:** authorised EU sleep-onset claim does not clear Czech product classification. SÚKL treats oral products at daily doses≥0.5mg as medicinal by function; the EU sleep-onset claim condition is1mg near bedtime. Poor fit for non-sedating social drink and Czech ordinary-soda route. Below0.5mg is not automatic food clearance. [EU07](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/432/2025-08-20/eng); [EU40](https://sukl.gov.cz/dulezite-informace/zmena-rozhodovaci-praxe-v-pripade-vyrobku-obsahujici-latku-melatonin/)
- **Theacrine/methylliberine:** novel/status, chronic safety and stimulant-effect questions unresolved; supplier products and US use cannot resolve them. No recommended exposure.
- **5-HTP/tryptophan neuroactive strategies:** do not treat conversion into neurotransmitters as proof of meaningful relaxation or a safe multi-serving soda; serotonin-related medicine interactions and exact legal/material status need independent assessment. No reviewed basis to prioritise5-HTP. This workstream did not complete a candidate-specific official assessment; record as **unresolved/high-concern**, not proven EU-wide prohibited.
- **Citicoline:** existing reviewed novel-food authorisation covers supplements and foods for special medical purposes, not general soda. **MagnesiumL-threonate** likewise cannot be substituted for beverage-permitted magnesium salts using its supplement authorisation. [EU29](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2024/2694/oj); [EU41](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2014/423/oj)
- **Mycelia / chaga:** fruiting-body decisions do not transfer to mycelia. In reviewed Czech decision chaga extract is supplement-only; no general soda route established. [EU18](https://food.ec.europa.eu/document/download/05e13eb0-7bc0-4c94-b2ac-180ad3f43a05_en?filename=novel-food_consult-status_2006-8614745.pdf)

## Optional CBD: distinct project gate

Keep the main line CBD-free. EFSAFeb2026 provisional level0.0275mg/kg/day applies only qualifying ≥98% CBD **supplement formulations**, no nanoparticles, with specified production/genotoxicity prerequisites. It does not establish safety under25, pregnancy/lactation or medication use. Sept2026 opinion concerns a particular synthetic ≥98% CBD in MCT oil, exclusively supplements at2mg/day for specified adults. Neither is a Commission authorisation, an aqueous canned-soda safety evaluation, a recommended serving or efficacy evidence. No applicable CBD ordinary-beverage authorisation or calm/focus health claim was established in current reviewed sources. [EU03](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/novel-food/authorisations/union-list-novel-foods_en); [EU42](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/provisional-safe-level-cannabidiol-novel-food); [EU43](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154)

For any retained concept, a final review requires exact supplier identity, ordinary-food category proof, permitted claims/transition dossier, realistic daily aggregate exposure, medication/vulnerability policy, finished-pack analytical shelf life, and the actual Czech label/ads. This is necessary scope for later development; no authority or supplier was contacted.
