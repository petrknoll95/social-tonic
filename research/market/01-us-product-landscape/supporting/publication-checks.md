## Structured checks

- Master product count: **31 unique IDs**; 22 recent-event, 7 contextual, 1 announced/preorder, 1 discontinued predecessor.
- Master source register: **141 records**, with unique source IDs and recorded URL, title, publisher, publication date (or unknown), access date, geography and supported claims.
- All CSV files parse with consistent row widths. Every product source ID resolves to the master register. Source extracts preserve workstream provenance and do not add product counts.
- Every benchmark identifies a finished can or bottle. Consumer powders, pouches, gummies and concentrates are absent as benchmark rows. References to excluded formats explain exclusions or describe study/ingredient inputs.
- Pack-price arithmetic was reviewed against its stated denominator. Rounded displayed prices are separate from exact divisions; unreproduced CSE promotional pricing is excluded. No missing price is imputed.
- Gatorade whole-bottle scaling uses 28/12; EFSA's illustrative CBD arithmetic is 0.0275 × 70 = 1.925 mg/day and is explicitly not a permitted drink dose.
- Local relative links and external citation URLs were checked for formatting and register linkage. No credentials, copied full articles, transient execution logs or supplier quotations are included.

## Publishing method

For the initial publication, the repository was empty at both inspection and pre-publication refresh; no existing research was replaced. The research-only revision preserves unrelated concurrent work and retains the original evidence cut-off. The requested folder is used exactly, with root and research indexes added. No repository visibility change or force push is part of the workflow.

The delivered Git commit and remote file verification are reported in the final response. This document deliberately does not embed its own commit hash, which would require another commit and create a self-reference problem.
