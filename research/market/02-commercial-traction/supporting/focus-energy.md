Research cutoff/access date: **17 October 2026, Europe/Prague**. Status: completed workstream for integration. Files: [metric dataset](../data/focus-energy-metrics.csv), [source register](focus-energy-sources.csv). Prior work reviewed: repository README, research index, [US landscape focus workstream](../../01-us-product-landscape/supporting/focus-energy.md), and [trends report](../../26-trends-and-industry-intelligence/report.md).

**Bloom is the strongest recently scaled energy blueprint in this sample; Neutonic provides narrower evidence that a work-specific focus proposition can sell.** Functional tea has a demonstrated RTD business in Yerba Madre. None establishes an incremental cognitive benefit from a nootropic blend, Czech willingness to pay, or startup profitability. Mature energy and specialist focus must remain separate comparisons.

## Corrections, contradictions and provenance

**Bloom:** the older >35m first-year cans claim originates in a 1 July 2025 company release. Later reporting gives >$100m first-year energy sales with an undefined sales basis, and >155m **beverages** moved in 2025. The latter cannot become energy cans automatically. Different timing, wholesale/retail bases and channel coverage may explain disparities; the available record does not reconcile them. Prefer the scoped panel figures above. [Company release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bloom-nutrition-launches-better-for-you-soda-bloom-pop-exclusively-at-walmart-302495845.html), [Entrepreneur, 6 July 2026](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/his-new-product-sold-more-than-100-million-in-one-year-heres-his-strategy).

**Neutonic:** the 2025 "$20m brand" headline described **valuation**. A founder said more than half of US customers reorder, but supplied no cohort, observation window, denominator, channel or drink-only allocation: this is low-confidence repeat evidence. The same interview disclosed early stockouts and $60,000 of stock taken offline because of taste. The 2026 >$25m revenue claim is a **whole-brand forecast**, including excluded products. April release repetitions are one claim family, not separate confirmations. [July 2025 interview](https://www.beveragedaily.com/Article/2025/07/01/tapping-into-the-productivity-market-how-two-influencers-built-a-20m-nootropic-brand/), [April 2026 release](https://www.streetinsider.com/Business%2BWire/Neutonic%2BRaises%2B%246m%2Bat%2B%2460m%2BValuation%2Bto%2BAccelerate%2BGlobal%2BExpansion%2BAcross%2BRetail%2Band%2BNew%2BMarkets/26380446.html).

**Distribution is exposure and execution risk.** Neutonic's October announcement promises a rollout into nearly 1,000 Walmart stores from 3 October, not audited active doors. Its new assortment names alpha-GPC while the existing US Orange Sunrise page names rhodiola; do not transfer one formula's evidence to the other. [October release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neutonic-expands-us-retail-footprint-with-launch-in-walmart-stores-302895438.html), [current SKU](https://www.neutonic.com/en-us/products/orange-sunrise-12-pack-us).

**Revenue and profitability are different.** Alani's issuer-reported $364.4m Q2 2026 brand revenue includes distribution transition orders and discussion of discontinued non-RTD products. The group's 48.1% gross margin and $55.3m net income are not Alani/Bloom/Neutonic unit economics. CELSIUS brand net revenue fell 11.7%, whereas tracked consumer retail fell 2%; inventory, promotions and channel mix matter. [Issuer results](https://ir.celsiusholdingsinc.com/news/news-details/2026/Celsius-Holdings-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

**Retrenchment is not always product failure.** Guayakí/Yerba Madre's January 2025 shift from self-distribution to partners caused more than 100 layoffs despite growing RTD sales. Separately, Synergy's July 2026 filing says Costco discontinued FOCUSfactor products and represented 58% of 2025 company revenue. The filing does **not** isolate cans, so this is a customer-concentration warning, not an RTD delisting verdict. [BevNET, 31 January 2025](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2025/guayaki-distro-pivot-sparks-layoffs-as-ymco-is-mostly-dissolved), [Synergy 8-K, 29 July 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562733/000121390026082791/ea0299706-8k_synergy.htm).

## Format check and practical blueprints

Exact qualifying references, with no allocation of brand-line sales to individual flavor: [Alani Cherry Slush 12 fl oz can](https://www.alaninu.com/products/energy-drink-cherry-slush); [Bloom 12 fl oz canned variety](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-95013440); [Neutonic US Orange Sunrise 355ml can](https://www.neutonic.com/en-us/products/orange-sunrise-12-pack-us); [Magic Mind Original 2 fl oz bottle](https://magicmind.com/products/mental-performance-shot-original); [Odyssey Tropical Breeze canned 85mg line](https://www.odysseyfunctionalenergy.com/products/tropical-odyssey-functional-energy-85mg-caffeine-12-pack); [Yerba Madre Enlighten Mint 15.5 fl oz can](https://yerbamadre.com/products/enlightenmint); [FOCUSfactor Orange can](https://focusfactor.com/products/focus-energy-drink-orange); [Rockstar Original 16 fl oz can](https://www.rockstarenergy.com/products/the-original); [CELSIUS Sparkling Orange can](https://www.celsius.com/products/celsius/sparkling-orange/); [Red Bull Pink US can](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/energydrink/products/red-bull-pink-edition); [5-hour Berry 1.93 fl oz bottle](https://www.safeway.com/shop/pd/5-hour-energy-berry-regular-strength-shot-1-93-fl-oz/960013667). Chameleon and Pure Leaf exact can formats are documented in the sources above and dataset. Powder, pouch, gummy and concentrate revenues are excluded.

Analyst hypotheses to test cheaply, not demonstrated causal explanations:

1. **Bloom/Alani:** familiar energy, accessible shelf presentation, distinctive flavors and a specific audience may matter more commercially than maximal ingredient novelty. Existing distribution and large audiences are major confounders; this is not a low-budget launch cost template.
2. **Neutonic:** a concrete work-session occasion is worth testing against a taste-matched simple caffeinated drink. Its cumulative cans support taking the proposition seriously; its formula's incremental benefit and premium remain unproven.
3. **Yerba Madre:** test a recognizable brewed-tea option alongside a sparkling focus can. The consumer may want a drinkable alternative to energy branding, rather than a complex supplement narrative.
4. **Magic Mind:** a bottled daily ritual is an alternative format hypothesis. Require paid cohort repeat, refunds, fulfillment cost and acquisition payback before interpreting subscriptions as a healthy business.

For an outsourced pilot, record paid units/store/week by SKU, sales excluding promotions, six-to-eight-week reorder, repeat purchasers by acquisition cohort, wastage, returns, freight and contribution after discounts. Compare equal caffeine and price where possible; otherwise any apparent preference may be dose or price preference. Collect these data in Czechia or another deliberately chosen first market before committing to large production. No customer tests, supplier quotes or contacts were performed here; no formula, launch geography, MOQ or budget is selected.
