# 13 · Flavour and sensory

**Date:** 17 October 2026. **Status:** completed desk research and proposed sensory programme; parallel specialist workstreams and independent reviews integrated. No tasting, interviews, purchases, supplier contact or validated formula/market preference.

**Scope:** premium adult non-alcoholic functional RTDs in cans or bottles for Czech/EU investigation, prioritizing focus/workday energy. Hydration, social relaxation, bedtime and selected secondary categories receive separate sensory treatment. Primary concepts are CBD-free; CBD remains a separately gated extension. US 2025–2026 product events and line activity provide benchmarks, not proof of Czech taste or effects. No THC/intoxicating launch scope.

The requested folder `research/product/13-flavour-and-sensory/` had no equivalent existing topic and is used unchanged. Owner was verified from the repository remote. The [method](supporting/research-plan-and-method.md) records repository review, delegation, evidence rules and publishing protections.
