# Initial naming screen

**17 October 2026 · Europe/Prague.** Public-web discovery only. Each final name was searched as a quoted phrase with drink/beverage/brand terms; selected candidates received additional wine, tea or broader brand queries. Inspected results were often ordinary phrase occurrences rather than brand names. That makes negative results weak evidence. No official multi-country trademark clearance, consumer testing or social-handle check was performed.

No-results or no-clear-match findings are not availability claims. Broader exact-name, pronunciation, related-goods and destination-market searches remain necessary before adoption.

## Candidates removed during this round

The screen eliminated several otherwise natural names rather than presenting an existing drink identity as a new proposal:

- **Good Measure:** existing [coffee](https://www.goodmeasurecoffees.com/) and [wine identity](https://russellshawdesign.com/good-measure-wine-brand-label-and-packaging-design).
- **Bright Hour:** existing [AJB beer](https://untappd.com/b/ajb-co-bright-hour/5949540).
- **Second Serve:** existing [Barrel Theory beer](https://untappd.com/b/barrel-theory-beer-company-second-serve/6157609).
- **Easy Company:** existing [brewery](https://easycompanybrewing.com/).
- **Goodkind:** existing [coffee shop and roastery](https://www.goodkindcoffee.com/).
- **Side Porch:** existing [Ten Bends beer](https://untappd.com/b/ten-bends-beer-side-porch/1967111).
- **Garden Press:** existing [lemonade business](https://gardenpresslemonade.com/).
- **Peel & Pour:** an existing Splenda drink-mix name, documented in its [launch announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-splenda-peel--pour-zero-calorie-drink-mix-allows-consumers-to-transform-water-into-a-delicious-beverage-with-four-flavors-301837713.html).
- **Steady On:** existing oral supplement-spray name on [Phew's site](https://phewcrew.com/).
- **Long Lunch:** a [wine tasting kit](https://www.tinywine.co.uk/collections/wine-tasting-kits/products/the-long-lunch) and extensive beverage-event use.

Fresh Set, Table Talk, Fresh Company and Garden Hour were also set aside after food, hospitality or serving-concept overlaps surfaced. Late Lunch, Good Break and Daylight Club were not advanced in the creative selection. These are early-round decisions, not legal findings.
