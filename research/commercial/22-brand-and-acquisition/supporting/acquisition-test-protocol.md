# Acquisition test protocol and measurement dictionary

**17 October 2026 · proposals only · Czech learning setting unless stated otherwise.** Amounts are analyst cash caps in CZK, not supplier/panel/creator quotes, market CAC benchmarks or a committed launch budget. Founder time is excluded from cash caps but logged. Nobody has been recruited or contacted; no ads, samples, purchases or sales have occurred.

## Design and safeguards

Recruit adults by recent occasion and purchase behaviour; include satisfied incumbent users, caffeine avoiders and people who choose no drink. Do not target minors or diagnosed anxiety, ADHD or insomnia. Use one identical can/bottle rendering, portion, proposed flavour, price and factual recipe assumption when comparing messages. Randomize exposure order or assignment; never mistake different audiences or formulas for a wording test. Show a familiar eligible canned/bottled alternative and a no-purchase option. Coffee preparation, sleep and work habits may be interview context, never additional product benchmarks.

A novel-food/claims/formula/process/label review precedes candidate sampling, including free samples. Public concept ads also need review: clearly say the product is in development and unavailable for purchase, disclose proposed rather than final composition, use no false scarcity, fake checkout or invented endorsements. Private concept research is not clearance to deploy a message commercially. Make marketing consent separate and optional; minimise personal data and do not collect diagnoses. Keep adverse experience reports and stop candidate sampling if safety concerns arise.

Pre-register the audience, exact stimulus, allocation, sample target, time window, spend ceiling, primary outcome, exclusions and stop rule. No switching to CTR as success because purchases disappoint. Small panels and venue pilots are directional. The numeric rules below are founder management gates, not confidence statements or industry standards. Reaching a spending cap without the minimum observations means **inconclusive**, not a failed population proposition. Any optional extension requires a newly declared budget.

## Ranked tests

| 1 / T01 | A recurring work-break need exists that a can/bottle can solve without a cognitive promise. Code unaided meaning, actual alternative, last paid choice, payer, problem recurrence. Sales and retention unavailable. | Complete 24. Advance a workday proposition only if at least 6/12 describe a recurrent specific switch reason and at least 4 give an existing paid-drink occasion. Do not pool social/evening counts into this gate. Otherwise revise segment; do not claim prevalence. |
| 2 / T02 | Specific use-case language is clearer and worth choosing at a stated price. Separate workday and evening adults. | 120 respondents; 60 workday randomized 30 focus-occasion / 30 ordinary-energy framing; 60 evening randomized 30 social / 30 bedtime-ritual framing. Same neutral can, 250 ml assumption and CZK49 price within comparisons; explicit no-purchase/current option. **9,000** = 120 × 60 + 1,800 setup. | Correct unaided product/occasion/caffeine interpretation; hypothetical choice at price. | 4 / T04 | A clear occasion earns qualified interest without an effect promise. | 7 / T07 | Convenient chilled availability creates ongoing demand. Three coworking/office accounts OR three independent cafés/retailers near target occasion, not a pooled mix. | Six selling weeks plus 60-day consumer follow-up where consented. **9,000** placement/transport/materials allowance; product at landed cost additional, discounts recorded; no fixture purchase assumed. | Units sold per in-stock venue-week; net account contribution, account reorders; consumer repeat only for identified consented buyers. Employer-funded consumption is a separate account cohort. | Three venues × six weeks with ≥80% intended hours in stock and verified sell-through. Advance when ≥2/3 reorder on standard terms from consumer depletion, each contributing positively after service costs. No numerical units/week benchmark imposed without margin and traffic. Free office depletion or a consignment restock is not a paid consumer reorder. |
| 8 / T08 | A credible small creator can generate incremental occasion-specific buyers. Two adult-audience Czech creators (work/process or food/hosting, chosen for the selected territory). | Stock and legal review first. **12,000** = 2 × 4,000 fee/usage-rights allowance + 4,000 product/fulfilment/coordination; no quotes or guaranteed reach. Expand only with positive cohort contribution after recurring creator costs and no material unapproved claims. Do not declare causality from codes; record existing customers, overlaps and leakage. |
| 9 / T09 | Referrals add new buyers without purchasing loyalty through discounts. Existing real buyers after initial repeat observations. | Randomly invite 100 of 200 eligible buyers to a give/get test, 100 holdout; reward only completed nonrefunded new-customer order. Max **3,000** = 50 successful pairs × 40 combined credit (20 per side) + 1,000 setup. Pre-screen against actual contribution including all rewards and administration; reduce the reward or use an unrewarded invitation if economics fail. Do not run a known negative-margin reward test. Wait for sufficient buyers; do not seed fake referrals. | Excess new paying customers per invited buyer vs holdout where referral attribution is observable; 60-day referred-customer contribution and paid repeat, reward liability/redemption, self-referrals. | 100 eligible in each arm; ≥20 new referred buyers mature to day60. Advance only with positive contribution after both rewards and a positive difference consistent across cohorts; small uncertain difference remains inconclusive. Do not call code-only purchases incremental. Report discounted acquisition separately from non-promotional repeat. |

## Definitions and logging

- **Eligible exposure:** deduplicated adult fitting the occasion/delivery region shown the complete price and an actually buyable, in-stock offer. A digital impression is not this denominator.
- **First paid buyer:** distinct customer with a completed first paid order, net of cancellations/refunds. Separate consumer and B2B accounts. Count the first order once across codes/venues.
- **60-day repeat:** first paid buyers making ≥1 second paid nonrefunded order by day60 / all first buyers whose first order is ≥60 days old. Also report non-promotional repeats separately. Preserve nonresponders in the denominator. Report stockout and availability days; show a sensitivity cohort without hiding failures.
- **Attributed CAC:** recurring channel cash, allocated creative/fees, samples at landed cost, distribution, incentives and commissions / deduplicated attributed new paying customers. Report research/setup spending and fully loaded total separately; never silently omit costs to make CAC look attractive.
- **Incremental CAC:** same relevant spend / excess new customers versus an adequately comparable randomized holdout; unknown when the design cannot identify excess. Subtract neither estimated organic sales nor assumed repeat without evidence.
- **Pre-acquisition contribution:** net receipts excluding applicable tax/refundable deposits, less drink landed cost, packaging, fulfilment, net shipping subsidy, payment costs, trade fees and ordinary discounts/refunds. Acquisition-specific creator fees/commissions, samples and referral rewards belong in acquisition spend; count every item once. **Post-acquisition contribution** subtracts that acquisition spend from pre-acquisition contribution. Compare CAC with the pre-acquisition measure, then show the post-acquisition result. Customer lifetime value remains unknown; report the realized horizon only.
- **Velocity:** units sold to customers divided by in-stock outlet-weeks; distinguish deliveries to retailer from sell-through. No direct mapping from account reorders to consumer retention.

Suggested minimal fields: anonymous participant/customer ID; consent; adult/occasion/region eligibility; randomized cell; visit/session/venue; first exposure date; format/portion/formula version; price and promotion; stock availability; first paid date; net receipt/units/cost/refund; follow-up maturity; repeat date and discount; referral/creator code; alternative attribution; complaint. Retain only necessary data with a declared deletion schedule. Use cohort and arm summaries, not identifiable customer data, in GitHub.
