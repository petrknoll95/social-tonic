# Commercial traction in functional ready-to-drink beverages

**Research cut-off: 17 October 2026 · US benchmarks, with separate Czech/EU and UK context**

## Decision in brief

**The strongest recent commercial evidence supports energy and better-for-you soda; it does not yet establish that a new cognitive-ingredient proposition sells at comparable scale.** Bloom provides an emerging energy benchmark with tracked liquid-line sales. Neutonic offers more specific productivity positioning and reported cumulative cans, but weaker verification and no clean US annual sales series. In CBD-free calm/social drinks, Recess and Hiyo are meaningful specialist comparators with narrower, less transparent commercial evidence. Dedicated sleep and genuinely non-stimulant focus remain much less proven in the public data reviewed.

For this founder, the best blueprints are **commercial mechanisms to test**, not formulas to copy: an understandable work-break occasion, familiar enjoyable refreshment, precise portion/caffeine choice, an adult evening ritual, and repeat purchases at a price that supports outsourced production. No category, formula, budget or country has been selected. Hiyo is one social comparator. The primary product should work independently of CBD; a CBD extension needs its own demand, safety, legal-use, supply and claims case.

This report ranks **evidence within categories**, not all brands by a misleading revenue league table. The [metric dataset](data/commercial-metrics.csv) retains amounts, currencies, periods, geography, scope, source IDs and limitations. The [source register](sources.csv) records publication/access dates and claims supported. The [method](supporting/research-protocol.md) explains discovery, delegation and source grading; [cross-stream review](supporting/independent-review.md) and [secondary-category review](supporting/independent-secondary-review.md) record challenges and resolutions.

## Scope and evidence standard

Only finished non-alcoholic liquids in **cans or bottles**, including verified bottled RTD shots and canned/bottled coffee or tea, qualify as product benchmarks. Powders, sachets, pouches, tablets, capsules, gummies, gels, drops and consumer concentrates are excluded. Mixed-format business results appear separately as attribution warnings, never as proof of the liquid line. THC-led/intoxicating products are outside the intended launch scope. Original launches are distinguished from 2025–2026 reformulations, traction or distribution events.

**A** = filing/audited financial evidence; **B** = retailer/panel consumer-sales data; **C** = independent reporting; **D** = company/founder claim; **E** = weak proxy; **U** = unknown/unusable for the liquid line. A source's underlying origin matters: a brand quoting SPINS is a panel-origin claim transmitted by the brand, not an independently inspected panel export. A filing can contain unaudited estimates. Confidence rates the exact observation, not efficacy or business quality. “Unknown” does not mean zero.

## Repeat, velocity and profitability: the missing bridge

No comparable, independently verified **units per store per week** series across these specialist brands was established. Retailer rankings omit absolute volume; authorizations omit live/in-stock store-weeks. Dividing annual revenue by the latest store count would create false precision. Annual/cumulative cases cannot be converted to cans without a defined case pack.

Neutonic's reported reorder claim is not a validated cohort retention rate without a purchase window, denominator and channel/format definition. Company subscribers, product ratings, repeat-buyer badges and review counts are not substitutes. Most qualifying lines' net margins, contribution after promotions/freight and operating profitability remain unknown. A whole-company net profit can coexist with operating losses or include excluded products.

The founder should request an evidence bridge before copying a blueprint: exact eligible line → consumer takeaway → base/promoted velocity → paid cohort repeat → net revenue after deductions → contribution and cash payback. The [validation protocol](supporting/validation-and-diligence-protocol.md) defines each measure and sets out a small, outsourced route to obtaining it.

## Czech/EU transfer and CBD assessment

US traction does not establish EU demand or permission to copy a formulation. New Czech NIQ reporting puts a **combined energy/vitamin-water/sports-isotonic category at CZK7.127bn** for the 52 weeks ending 30 November 2025; it must not be called specialist focus-drink sales. The underlying energy segment grew 15.6% in value. This supports an existing occasion, with strong incumbent competition and promotion pressure. [Zboží & Prodej / NIQ, 2 March 2026](https://www.zboziaprodej.cz/2026/03/02/zboziprodej-1-2-2026-energeticky-apetit-trva/).

The [transfer memo](supporting/eu-czech-transfer.md) distinguishes local demand, ingredient effects, use authorization and claims. Caffeine has a firmer acute-alertness rationale than an undisclosed nootropic blend; chronic capsule findings or a task-specific experiment do not establish real-world productivity from a finished can. A commercially successful calm drink need not cause a reliably perceptible acute effect.

**CBD remains a separate, gated extension.** The strongest CBD-specific commercial evidence located must be kept apart from CBD-free US products and from THC drinks. A 2024 US survey's “would or might try” result is hypothetical interest, not purchases or Czech demand. The reviewed low-dose drink propositions cannot borrow efficacy from a 150 mg sublingual insomnia trial. No beverage-specific EU authorization or usable CBD calm/focus/sleep claim was established.

There is meaningful new scientific progress: EFSA's **18 September 2026** favorable opinion concerns a specified synthetic CBD ingredient **in MCT-oil supplements at 2 mg/day**, not aqueous RTDs or a general safe can dose. It is not Commission market authorization. Czech enforcement is documented. Supplier application validation, UK availability and US sales do not resolve this. See the [dated CBD safety, sourcing and legal assessment](supporting/eu-czech-transfer.md) and [EFSA opinion](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2026.10154).

## Low-cost next actions

1. **Validate a narrow occasion before selecting a category.** Interview reachable adult customers about their last work-break or evening/social drink purchase, then compare a few can/bottle concepts at actual local prices against qualifying incumbent RTDs and a no-purchase option. Separate taste, caffeine preference, ritual and ingredient expectations.
2. **Choose one hypothesis for outsourced bench work only after a switch reason emerges.** Compare a restrained workday proposition, a CBD-free caffeine-free adult evening drink, and a secondary refreshment/hydration option only where a distinct need appears. Ask later for written development/filling quotes at realistic small volumes; no budget or MOQ is assumed here.
3. **Measure unpromoted sell-through and paid repeat in a small channel pilot.** Define success from buyer benchmarks and actual contribution/cash economics before the test. Do not use national door counts, projected sales or an influencer following as the hurdle.
4. **Keep CBD off the main product's critical path.** Investigate concept demand independently, but require the exact legal-use, safety, supply and claims route before a CBD beverage trial or launch. Do not replace this gate with a THC proposition.

The [full protocol](supporting/validation-and-diligence-protocol.md) supplies proposed interview/pilot ranges and metric definitions. These are research recommendations, not completed validation, supplier commitments or a launch decision.
