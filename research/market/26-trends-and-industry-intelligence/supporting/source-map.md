## Sources that answer harder questions

| Question | Preferred source and examples | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Are people buying, not merely being offered distribution? | [Celsius results](https://ir.celsiusholdingsinc.com/news/news-details/2026/Celsius-Holdings-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx), Simply Good Foods results; explicitly scoped scanner data | Coverage, time windows and unmeasured-channel estimates matter. Neither directly establishes individual repeat. |
| Is the business economically substantial? | Nichols' VITHIT acquisition disclosure; SEC acquisition and Kin filing tables | Whole-business results may include powders, mixers or non-EU geographies. Revenue growth can coexist with losses. |
| What is actually in the liquid? | Exact company SKU, nutrition/ingredient panel, current retailer listing | Version conflicts remain possible. No physical labels, lab assays or certificates were purchased. |
| Is the offer genuinely available? | Target/Walmart/Alcampo/Rohlik/Sainsbury's product listing plus dated rollout | Listings can be regional, cached, out of stock or preorder. They do not establish chainwide execution. |
| Can it legally be used and marketed in Czechia/EU? | European Commission Union list and claims register, EFSA, CJEU, SZPI | Exact ingredient identity, product classification and intended use require a later formula-specific check. FSA is useful for the UK distinction, not EU clearance. |
| Could a startup manufacture it? | Hardtank public FAQ/facility information, Refresco capability descriptions | Supplier assertions only. No quote, outreach, capacity reservation or suitability assessment was performed. |
| Is the benefit scientifically supported? | Primary human studies with matching population, formulation, dose and outcome | This project is a targeted evidence screen, not a systematic review. Ingredient results cannot silently validate finished drinks. |
| What is appearing at trade shows? | BevNET Live recap and exhibitor/brand materials | Discovery leads; stage presence and awards are not sales. No claim of in-person attendance. |

Social posts and founder communications were used as attributed leads. VYBES' founder email, for example, establishes its stated CBD exit and rationale; it is not independent proof that consumers rejected CBD.
