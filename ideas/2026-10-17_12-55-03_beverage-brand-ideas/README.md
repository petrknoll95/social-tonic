# Beverage brand ideas

**17 October 2026 · Europe/Prague**

Twenty distinct brand concepts based on the [beverage naming report](../../brand/research/2026-10-17_11-07-23_beverage-naming/report.md), spanning social occasions, connection, sensory metaphors, invented names, unexpected combinations and credible craft.

- [20 concepts and recommended first tests](report.md)
- [Public-web screening, discarded drafts and domain checks](screening.md)

Each concept includes meaning, positioning, a visual direction, working copy, a sample pack stack, pronunciation and its main tradeoff. First creative shortlist: **Spoluno, Vlnora, Přisedni**. These are untested proposals; originality and trademark availability are not certified.
