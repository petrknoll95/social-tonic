# US functional RTD product landscape

**Date / cut-off:** 17 October 2026, Europe/Prague. **Status:** complete research synthesis; independently reviewed. Market validation, formula clearance and supplier quotations remain future work.

**Scope:** finished, non-alcoholic liquid beverages in cans or bottles, including bottled RTD shots. Primary coverage is focus/productivity, cognitive positioning, alertness and energy; relaxation, social occasions and sleep are separate priority categories. Hydration, gut health and protein/recovery are secondary. US 2025–2026 product events inform EU/Czech hypotheses; US availability and claims do not imply EU legality or effects.

The primary launch concept is CBD-free. A separate CBD segment examines demand, effects, sourcing, safety, legality and claims. THC-led/intoxicating products are excluded as launch blueprints. Powders, sachets, pouches, pills, gummies, gels, drops and consumer concentrates are excluded as product benchmarks. Ingredient studies in other delivery forms are explicitly assessed for transferability.

## Limits and maintenance

This is a purposive landscape, not a market census, sales audit, systematic clinical review or formula-specific legal clearance. Source records are not necessarily unique domains or independent studies. Product pages are snapshots; unknown dates, doses, current availability and commercial metrics remain unknown. Confidence rates documentary support, not efficacy. A press release establishes an announcement; it does not prove its promised rollout occurred.

Keep original launch history distinct from later events. Recheck exact labels, orderability, prices and rules before acting. The main gaps are SKU sales/repeat purchase, some formula versions, clinical transfer, functional coffee/tea coverage, Czech demand/pricing and actual contract-packing economics. No people were contacted and no purchases or tests were performed.
